## The five findings that matter most

1. **Most of this is rearranging, not rebuilding.** The band already has a page stack with a Back chip (`dashboardPanelBus.js`), the attribute preview the owner wants kept exists whole, a player card that draws both yourself and another player exists (`PlayerProfilePanel.jsx`), the DPS number exists, the Gear tab already has nine cells. What is missing is MEMORY (closing the band throws away where you were) and ONE of each thing: there are at least five separate item-detail screens and two separate "look at a player" screens.
2. **The points rule in the document is not the game's rule.** The document says every weapon level-up gives 3 shared points, so a 3 / 3 / 3 player has 18 and a 3 / 1 / 1 player has 6. Since v2.3.3538 (the owner's agreement of 2026-10-21, "SIMPLICITY and INTELLIGIBILITY") shared points come with the CHARACTER level, which is the best weapon's: both players have the same. And a weapon's points may be spent on body stats (the owner's rule of v2.3.2176), so the pools are not sealed from each other.
3. **Another player's page today shows what THEIR phone says, and only for players standing near you.** Weapon levels and item names reach you in a blob the other phone writes (`rpgData`, "vanity-only" in the worker's own words); item stats, combat stats and placed points do not reach you at all. "Complete transparency" needs the worker to send its own facts: the one phase that touches the server.
4. **The three screens that landed on 2026-11-25 each do something the document rules out.** Bro moves by page marks; Quests shows the steps still to come, in grey, and moves by dots; Ranks shows a level chip beside every other board's value, pins your row always, and a tap on a row does nothing.
5. **Several things the document names are not in the game as worded.** No effective-health number; no Homestead page on the dashboard (the farm's window opens only at the farm); clans are parties now and "guilds" are the skill guilds every player is in, with no clan or guild profile; the Codex is monsters only and IS hidden until your first kill; quests have no Story text and no Locate (there is "Show on map"); there is no Equipment leaderboard.
