# V9c · Spending the phone decides alone: furniture, dungeon packs, and points
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Severity: medium (no gold or item is really lost -- the server never charged -- but players are shown spends that undo themselves, furniture "buffs" and packs that do nothing, packs that can never unlock, and trees that vanish from the screen after crafting a bed; and a script can recycle points through the free reset, writing about 6 rows a level a minute against the free plan's 100,000 rows a day for the whole game)
- Who can trigger it: honest player: BroTown's Land Office -> "Travel to Farm" -> the haystack's "Furniture Workshop" -> Craft a Wooden Bed (5 wood, 30 gold, Woodworking 1), or the notice board's "Dungeon Workshop" -> Store -> Buy "Bone Crypt" (300 gold); points: Points tab -> [+] -> Spend, then turn the phone (or press the sheet's key) before the answer lands. The write loop (reset, respend, repeat): modified phone only. The legacy points grid: owner only (a cap-named live flag) or deploy timing.
- Summary: Two windows on your farm still spend on the phone alone. The Furniture Workshop takes your gold and wood and adds XP without telling the server, so they come back later; the furniture does nothing, and the "wood" it takes can be your axe, so trees vanish from your screen. The Dungeon Workshop's Store sells packs the same way, and its boss-locked packs can never unlock. Spending points is server-run and sound, but writes one saved row per point, and the free reset hands every point back each minute.
- Key files: src/ui/panels/FurniturePanel.jsx:169-195, src/ui/panels/DungeonCreatorPanel.jsx:645-661, src/data/gameSystems.js:2778-2799, server/src/prog3.js:1470-1488, server/src/pointsreset.js:85-97, src/networking/wsClient.js:1679-1742

- States:
  - InStep: the phone's coins, bag and skill XP equal the worker's.
  - PhoneSpent: the phone shows a spend the worker never heard of (furniture craft, pack buy).
  - NoAxeOnPhone: the craft took `woodcutting_axe` as "wood"; trees are hidden and unharvestable on this phone.
  - BuiltOnPhone / OwnedOnPhone: `_furniture[id]` or a pack id held in this device's `bt_rpg` only.
  - BossLocked: a boss-gated pack or terrain; on the Wheel it never leaves this state.
  - SpendOpen: the Points window with its stepper (frozen `blocked`/`max` from open).
  - Spending: n `prog3_allocate` sent, "Spending..." shown, waiting for acks or 3 s.
  - Settled: the window put up again with "✓ k points spent".
  - StaleWindow: the sheet closed while Spending; 3.6 s later the old window's Spend works again on old numbers.
  - Prog3Rules / LegacyGrid: the sheet's dialect, picked by caps and the blob.

## What needs further thought
- The farm's two workshops: hide the Furniture Workshop and the Dungeon Workshop's Store (and Save) until the server settles them, or give the server real handlers? The owner's "remove or rebuild" for the Dungeon Workshop is already waiting (docs/specs/dungeon-fixes.md:243); the furniture has no plan at all and promises buffs nothing gives.
- Should a points spend be one message (`{stat, cat, n}`, one saved row, one answer), and should the free reset's "once a minute" survive a reconnect (kept with the record) or be counted against a daily write budget?
- The old points rule sets (legacy grid, linear math, flat crits) have been switched off on the live server for months: keep them for rollbacks, or retire them so the sheet has one rule set?
- Making it explicit: on the server, one `spend` record per character -- `{kind: 'furniture' | 'pack' | 'points', opId, cost, at}` -- and one transition function per kind that checks, charges, records and answers with an echo (`furniture_built`, `pack_bought`, `points_spent {stat, cat, n, pool, poolBy, shared}`), each behind a cap the phone gates on and a lower-case kill switch. The phone keeps no `_furniture`/`_ownedPacks` of its own: it draws what the record holds, and its Points window owns `{open, spending(opId, n), settled(k)}` itself, so closing the sheet cannot orphan the wait. The rule set becomes one object built once per state_sync (`{kind, x, elem, shared, rel, statFree, oneFormula, oneLevel}`), handed to every reader.
- Quick fixes vs design work: one-line fixes: skip tool keys (`*_axe`, `*_pickaxe`, `*_pole`) in the furniture wood match, or hide its button; if the Workshop stays, read `wheel_<land>` as `<land>` in the boss gate (the Earth, Storm and Wind packs and their terrains would then unlock from today's hollows, thunder and sky dungeons; ember, frost, mist and tidal have no dungeon, so their four stay shut); close the popup when the sheet unmounts. The furniture craft also sends nothing through BroTown's stats_update (its signature, src/ui/BroTown.jsx:3413-3450, carries no coins, bag or furniture), so hiding the button removes the problem without touching the worker. Needs a decision: remove or rebuild the two workshops; a batched points message; where the reset's minute lives; retiring the old dialects.
