# V6 · A duel and an arena match, on the worker and on the phone
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Severity: high (an honest player's phone can believe it is in a duel that no longer exists, or never existed; in No man's land every swing then attacks any nearby player the level rule allows, with no Attack window, giving the swinger a red skull, and a red skull killed by a player loses everything worn. Separately, a duel whose players end up in different zones stops both players' healing, shop, market, trades and rides for up to 10 minutes)
- Who can trigger it: honest player: (1) tap Accept on a challenge card that has been up over 2 minutes, or whose challenger has since logged off, gone through a door, or got into another duel, or that popped up during your own duel; (2) put the phone away for over 15 s during a duel or arena match; (3) walk into a dungeon mouth or onto the farm during a duel, or be pulled into your party leader's dungeon. Deploy/restart timing leaves both duellers' phones in a duel too. A wager (and so hit-and-hide for gold) needs a modified phone.
- Summary: A duel is tracked twice, by the server and by each phone. The accepting phone marks itself "in a duel" before the server answers, and only the server's "duel over" message clears it. If the server refuses, the duel ends while the phone is away, or the server restarts, the phone keeps a duel that does not exist; in No man's land its swings then hit nearby players unasked. And walking into a dungeon or onto the farm mid-duel ends nothing: both stay locked up to 10 minutes.
- Key files: src/ui/panels/DuelRequestPanel.jsx:189-215, src/networking/gameEvents.js:4717-4790, server/src/duel.js:142-213, src/game/monsterCombat.js:3195, server/src/movement.js:721, server/src/dungeon.js:288-392

## The machine
- Entity, and where its state lives: one duel between two players; an arena match is the same duel tagged `arenaMatch`.
  - Worker: `this._duelChallenges` 'challenger>target' → {wager, ts} (server/src/duel.js:120,133); `this._duels` id → {status, a, b, wager, startedAt, expiresAt, away{pid: deadline}, arenaMatch?} (server/src/duel.js:79-91,207); `this._pvpConsent` pair → until (server/src/duel.js:210; server/src/gladiator.js:388); storage `duelEscrow:<id>` (server/src/duel.js:198); the arena match {winner, active, deadline, how} in `this._arena.matches` and `ps._arenaMatch` (server/src/gladiator.js:358-360,383-390,600-602).
  - Phone: `S._inDuel` and `S._activeDuel` (src/ui/panels/DuelRequestPanel.jsx:201; src/networking/gameEvents.js:4643-4652,4720-4736; cleared ONLY at gameEvents.js:4772-4773); the duel lock `S.lockedTarget` (src/game/duelLock.js:41-51, re-taken every frame for an arena flag at :78-79 via src/game/targeting.js:502); the card's React state `duelRequest` (src/ui/BroTown.jsx:2053; gameEvents.js:4708,5223); `S._arenaMatch` from the arena poll (BroTown.jsx:9102,9116, self-healing).
- States:
  - None: no challenge, no duel.
  - Challenged: the worker keeps the challenge 2 minutes; the target's phone shows a full-screen card with no clock.
  - Accepted on the phone: the accepter's phone is already "in a duel"; the worker has not answered.
  - Active: the worker's duel: the consent pair, no HP regen, the bag fight-locked, no ride, one bite every 15 s, no second duel.
  - Away: one or both players disconnected, each on a 15 s forfeit clock.
  - Split (never declared): the two players stand in different zones, so no blow can land; only a death, a 15 s disconnect or the clock ends it.
  - Resolved: kill, death, forfeit or timeout; `duel_end` to the room; the pot paid.
  - Phantom (phone only, never declared): the phone keeps a duel the worker does not have.
  - Arena match: pending → active (a wager-0 duel, 3-minute clock) → won; or walkover, double-forfeit, or timeout without ever becoming a duel.
- Moves:
  - Duel button: None → Challenged (src/ui/panels/InspectPlayerPanel.jsx:278-291 → server/src/duel.js:124-135 → src/networking/gameEvents.js:5220-5231)
  - Decline: Challenged → None (DuelRequestPanel.jsx:50-61; duel.js:137-140)
  - 2 minutes pass: Challenged → None on the worker only; the card stays (duel.js:285-288; the card's `ts` is never read)
  - Accept tap: Challenged → Accepted on the phone, lock on (DuelRequestPanel.jsx:189-215)
  - Worker accepts: → Active; the settled echo enters the challenger's phone only (duel.js:189-213; gameEvents.js:4717-4744)
  - Worker drops the accept silently (no or expired challenge; either side already duelling): Accepted on the phone → Phantom (duel.js:145-146) [wrong]
  - Worker refuses with declineBoth (a side gone or dying, zones differ, stake over the shown one, escrow failed): Accepted on the phone → Phantom, with only a "Duel declined" popup (duel.js:149-156,175,188,199; gameEvents.js:4787-4790) [wrong]
  - Socket close: Active → Away (server/src/index.js:6435; duel.js:260-268)
  - Rejoin within 15 s: Away → Active (server/src/join.js:1598; duel.js:270-280); a reloaded page is told nothing (no social counterpart to gladiator.js:413-419) [missing]
  - 15 s away: Away → Resolved 'forfeit' (duel.js:301-310); the away phone misses `duel_end` and returns in Phantom [missing]
  - Zone change (a dungeon mouth, the farm trip): Active → Split, no clock starts (server/src/movement.js:489,721,814-830; duel.js:172-174) [missing]
  - Dungeon start or party pull: Active → Split (server/src/dungeon.js:288-350,363-393) [missing]
  - Death: → Resolved 'kill' or 'death' (index.js:3417; duel.js:219-226)
  - 10-minute (arena 3-minute) clock: → Resolved 'timeout', higher hp fraction wins (duel.js:328-335)
  - `duel_end` naming me: phone → None; an arena flag only for an end naming its own opponent (gameEvents.js:4754-4784, 4771)
  - Deploy: the worker forgets every duel; both phones keep theirs → Phantom (ARCHITECTURE-HANDOFF rule 11) [missing]
  - Arena activation: pending → active with no zone check; walkover or double-forfeit when a side has no playerState; deferred while either is in a duel, then a timeout at the deadline (gladiator.js:366-399)
  - Arena rejoin: `arena_match_start` sent again (gladiator.js:413-419; join.js:1888)
- Diagram edges:
  - `None --Duel button--> Challenged`
  - `Challenged --Decline, or 2 min on the worker only--> None`
  - `Challenged --Accept tap--> AcceptedOnPhone`
  - `AcceptedOnPhone --settled echo (read by the challenger only)--> Active`
  - `AcceptedOnPhone --!! silent drop or duel_decline--> Phantom`
  - `Active --socket close--> Away`
  - `Away --rejoin within 15 s--> Active`
  - `Away --!! 15 s forfeit, phone away misses duel_end--> Phantom`
  - `Active --!! zone change, dungeon start, party pull--> Split`
  - `Split --death or the 10-min clock--> Resolved`
  - `Active --kill, death, timeout--> Resolved`
  - `Resolved --duel_end--> None`
  - `Phantom --!! no exit but a reload or a later duel's end--> Phantom`
  - `ArenaPending --activation, no zone check--> Active`

## What needs further thought
- When one duellist walks into a dungeon or onto their farm, should the duel end (they forfeit after 15 s, as if they had closed the app), or should those doors refuse them until the duel is over? And should a party leader's dungeon start leave behind a member who is in a duel or an arena match?
- Should a duel challenge card time out on its own after 2 minutes (when the server forgets it), and should a challenge that arrives while you are fighting wait as a small banner instead of covering the screen?
- Should Block also stop duel challenges at the server (the phone's check misses the game's own Duel button today)?
- Making it explicit: one duel record per player on the phone, `S._duel = {id, phase: 'pending'|'on', foe, arena?, deadline}`, written ONLY from the worker's messages: the settled accept read by both sides (the accepter reads its own echo), a `duel_decline` naming the foe (pending → none), a short timeout on 'pending', `duel_end` carrying the duel's id, and a private duel state sent after every state_sync (the party's "clear on state_sync, re-send after it", join.js:1889-1893, which the arena already half-does). On the worker, one `_duelLeave(pid, why)` exit that a zone change, a disconnect and a death all call (a zone change starts the same 15 s clock), and one "may I leave what I am doing?" check that a dungeon start, a party pull and the farm trip all ask.
- Quick fixes vs design work: one-liners: the block check in the `duel_wager_request` handler (gameEvents.js:5220) plus a `duel_wager_request` line in mp-blockasks; the phone clearing an unsettled `_inDuel` on a `duel_decline` from that foe (gameEvents.js:4787); the accepter entering only on its own settled echo, or dropping the flag after a few seconds without one; a duel's melee claim naming its foe as `target` (monsterCombat.js player_attack; the worker already honours it, combat.js:2262), which alone closes the No man's land red-skull path for phantom and live duels; and answering the two silent drops (duel.js:145-146) with declineBoth. Design decisions: what a zone change does to a duel; whether doors and the party pull refuse duellers and arena fighters; the rejoin duel message; a grace before an arena walkover; and the card's clock and mid-fight behaviour.
