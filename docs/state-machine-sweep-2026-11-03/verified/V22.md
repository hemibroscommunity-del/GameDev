# V22 · A loot pile on the ground: how long it lies, who may take it, and the phone's own copy
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Severity: medium (items lost: a dead player's whole bag is destroyed by a server restart inside its two minutes; a player standing on a bag as it opens to them can lose it to a phone clock a fraction of a second fast, and in No man's land that is their own bag; the rest is display: fake remains piles, piles gone from the phone at 30 s while the worker keeps them 60 s)
- Who can trigger it: deploy/restart timing (the bag: any deploy within 2 minutes of a death); honest player (wait on a death pile for its minute to end, with a phone clock ahead of the worker's by more than the message's trip time; a phone clock 2+ minutes fast never shows its own bag; die with a pet out; fight a slime that swells); plausibly an honest solo player whose socket closes after dying (the empty room may be dropped from memory)
- Summary: When a monster or a player dies, the server lays a loot pile on the ground and keeps it in memory: 60 seconds for a monster's, 2 minutes for a dead player's bag (theirs alone for the first minute). Each phone keeps its own copy on its own clock, and its drawing code throws monster piles away at 30 seconds. A server restart inside those 2 minutes destroys a dead player's whole bag. A phone clock running slightly fast can get 'not yours' as a bag opens, then hides it.
- Key files: server/src/index.js:4381, server/src/index.js:4514, src/networking/wsClient.js:2414, src/game/groundLoot.js:36, src/rendering/systems/effectsRenderer.js:10459, src/networking/wsClient.js:3923

## The machine
- Entity, and where its state lives:
  - Worker: one pile in `this.loot[zone]` (server/src/index.js:1433), in memory only, no storage key. A kill pile has `ts`, `recipients`, `shares`, `claimedBy`, `inventoryClaimed`, `weaponClaimed`, `armorClaimed` (index.js:4190-4227). A death pile adds `isDeathDrop`, `deathItems`, `ownerOnlyUntil` (now + 60 s) and `expiry` (now + 120 s) (index.js:4410-4432). A dropped item adds `dropped`, `revealed`, `flashAt` (itemdrop.js).
  - Phone: a rebuilt copy in `S.groundLoot[i]` (src/networking/wsClient.js:3923-3992). It takes the worker's `ts`, `expiry` and `ownerOnlyUntil` as worker times (wsClient.js:3944, 3979-3980). Only a dropped item's clocks are turned into this phone's time (`_dropAt`, wsClient.js:3916-3922).
  - Per-copy flags: `_sx/_sy`, `_pickupPending/_pickupSentAt`, `_pickupRetryAt/_pickupTries/_lastRejectAt`, `_petPickupPending/_petSentAt`, `_collected/_despawnAt`, `_expired`, `_notYoursBeeped`, `_shine`, `_rareShown/_rareT0`, the `_pixi*` sprites, and the renderer's `_knownLoot` Set (effectsRenderer.js:10442).
  - Every whole list replaces the copies and resets their flags: state_sync (wsClient.js:1860), zone_loot and zone_state (wsClient.js:4395). About twelve zone changes empty the list (zoneTransitions.js:1135, gameEvents.js:934, respawn by zone change).
  - Piles the phone makes itself (no `lootId`, no `_serverLoot`): `dropLocalRemnantOnce` (src/game/combatHelpers.js:614-630), and the tick's death edge (wsClient.js:1339-1363, N1-18).
- States:
  - minted: the worker made the pile and told the room (`loot_drop`).
  - landing: the phone's first 100-500 ms (remains 100 ms, a slime's coin magnet 220 ms, the pet 500 ms), counted from the worker's `ts`.
  - owner's minute: a death pile, the dead player's alone for 60 s; in No man's land the killer's.
  - anyone's: a death pile from 60 s to 120 s.
  - partly claimed: a kill pile with several recipients. Gold goes to each recipient; the one-of item, the weapon and the armour go to the first.
  - asked: a request is in flight (5 s watchdog).
  - refused: one of three buckets on the phone. Retry fast (out of range, 300 ms, 8 tries); retry slow (dead, disconnected, no-ps, no-loot-zone, 1 s); dropped from this phone (no-pile, already-claimed, not-recipient, wrong-zone).
  - credited: drawn for 0.5 s more, then gone.
  - last call: pulses over its last 10 s.
  - cut by the phone: a monster pile at 30 s, a death pile at its `expiry`, both on the phone's clock, while the worker may still hold it.
  - despawned: all recipients claimed, a death pile's first pickup, or the worker's 60 s / 120 s.
  - lost: a room restart or eviction drops every pile. The death's save has already left the bag's items out.
  - phantom: a pile the phone made. The phone credits it to the bag itself, and the next bag update takes it back.
- Moves:
  - a kill: none → minted kill pile (server/src/combat.js:1949-1955; index.js:4092-4227)
  - a death: none → owner's minute (index.js:3467, 4381-4440). In No man's land the killer is the recipient (nomansland.js:824-828).
  - loot_drop / state_sync / zone_loot / zone_state: → a copy on the phone (gameEvents.js:283-327; wsClient.js:1860, 4395)
  - the phone's clock passes `ownerOnlyUntil`: owner's → anyone's on the phone (groundLoot.js:96-97). The worker decides the same thing on its own clock (index.js:4558-4565), so two clocks decide one edge. !!
  - walking within 20 px (a death pile), or the magnet holding (a kill pile): lying → asked (groundLoot.js:261-281)
  - 5 s with no answer: asked → may ask again (groundLoot.js:267-269)
  - loot_pickup_rejected: asked → one of the three buckets (wsClient.js:2414-2485)
  - loot_credit: asked → credited (wsClient.js:4258-4266) → gone after 0.5 s (groundLoot.js:41-44)
  - the worker's grant: every recipient claimed, or a death pile's first pickup → despawned (index.js:4647, 4838-4840)
  - the worker's tick at 60 s / 120 s → despawned, then loot_despawn → `_expired` on the phone (index.js:4486-4511; gameEvents.js:358-378)
  - the renderer, age > 30 s for a monster pile or now > `expiry` for a death pile, both on the phone's clock: cut out of `S.groundLoot` (effectsRenderer.js:10447, 10459-10463). The worker keeps the monster pile to 60 s. !!
  - a zone change, a respawn or a dungeon: the phone's list is emptied, then refilled by the next zone list (zoneTransitions.js:1135; respawn.js:64 then the Wheel arrival's zone_state, movement.js:850-870)
  - a room restart or eviction: every pile → lost (index.js:1433, memory only; the death was saved without the bag, index.js:3476, 3491) !!
  - a slime swelling at 0 HP: none → phantom (monsterCombat.js:268-281), which the phone credits to itself (groundLoot.js:377-436) !!
- Diagram edges:
  - `Nothing --monster killed--> KillPile`
  - `Nothing --player killed--> OwnerMinute`
  - `OwnerMinute --60 s on the worker's clock--> Anyones`
  - `OwnerMinute --!! phone clock a little fast: asks early, told 'not-recipient'--> HiddenOnThisPhone`
  - `KillPile --a recipient walks on--> Asked`
  - `Anyones --anyone walks on--> Asked`
  - `Asked --loot_credit--> Credited`
  - `Asked --refused: out of range or dead--> KillPile`
  - `Credited --0.5 s--> Gone`
  - `KillPile --worker 60 s (a death pile 120 s)--> Gone`
  - `KillPile --!! phone 30 s, worker still holds it to 60 s--> HiddenOnThisPhone`
  - `OwnerMinute --!! room restart or deploy--> Lost (the bag)`
  - `Nothing --!! a slime swells at 0 HP, phone only--> Phantom`
  - `Phantom --!! walked on: the phone credits itself--> Gone`

## Concerns, verified
- [CONFIRMED] R4-3 (divergence): a monster's pile leaves every phone at 30 s, while the worker keeps it, and would grant it, to 60 s.
  - Evidence: effectsRenderer.js:10447, 10459-10463 (`age > 30`, a splice of the list the pickup and the pet read), 10516 (`LOOT_LIFE = 30`); server/src/index.js:1439, 4499-4502; groundLoot.js:45 (60 s for a plain pile, never for remains).
  - How checked: read the cut and the worker's tick; tick.test.mjs pins the worker's 60 s.
  - Docs: the phone's 30 s is kept on purpose: item-drop.md:121-129 ("A monster's pile keeps its 30 s"), CLAUDE-HISTORY.md:4500, and the v2.3.2318 note ("Life is 30s", effectsRenderer.js:10513-10515). GDD-AS-BUILT.md:670 says "lasts 60 s". No note says why the worker keeps 60.
- [CONFIRMED] R4-3 (clock skew): every pile but a dropped item is aged on the phone's `Date.now()` against the worker's `ts` and `expiry`.
  - Evidence: pixiRenderer.js:583; wsClient.js:3944, 3979-3980; effectsRenderer.js:10447, 10459, 10522, 10555; groundLoot.js:36, 45, 106, 193; BroTown.jsx:7148.
  - What a fast clock does:
    - a clock d seconds fast cuts d seconds off every pile's life;
    - 30 s or more fast: no monster pile is ever seen;
    - 2 minutes or more fast: the player's own death pile is cut on its first frame, never drawn, never asked for, so the bag is lost for good;
    - 2.5 s or more fast: "RARE DROP!" never shows (`age < 2.5`, effectsRenderer.js:10271).
  - What a slow clock does: piles last longer on screen than on the worker.
  - Only a dropped item escapes this: its clocks go as time left (wsClient.js:3916-3922). The farm already measures the worker's clock (src/ui/mobile/farmBus.js:55, 62).
  - How checked: traced every comparison against `ts`, `expiry` and `ownerOnlyUntil`.
  - Docs: item-drop.md:170-172, "Not done": a death pile's clocks are the worker's own, so a phone whose clock is off sees its minute and its end that much off. Monster piles: none.
- [CONFIRMED] R4-3 (authority/opacity): the drawing pass removes piles from the game's own list.
  - The renderer splices `S.groundLoot`, the list the pickup, the magnet and the pet read. Its rules differ from groundLoot.js's (30 s for remains, which groundLoot.js keeps forever).
  - Five places on the phone decide a pile's end: groundLoot.js:36-45, the renderer (effectsRenderer.js:10094, 10459, 10600), stateCleanup.js:39, the refusal handler (wsClient.js:2449), loot_despawn (gameEvents.js:368).
  - Stale comments still describe the old death pile: "Preserve null recipients for death drops" (wsClient.js:3939); "A death drop carries no recipient list on the client" (groundLoot.js:252-258); "recipients=null ... TTL is the standard LOOT_EXPIRY_MS (60 s)" (index.js:4308-4310); "despawns after 60 s" (index.js:3465). The worker sends `[owner]` and keeps it 120 s (index.js:4422, 4431).
  - Docs: TRAPS §233 (docs/TRAPS.md:7849-7851): this same cut once hid the corpse's pickup bug.
- [CONFIRMED] R4-3 (leak, tiny): RARE DROP! is hidden at 2.4 s, not destroyed.
  - It keeps its picture until the pile goes (at most about 27 s more), and it is then destroyed with it.
  - Evidence: effectsRenderer.js:10276-10279, 10013.
  - Docs: TRAPS §192 (docs/TRAPS.md:6690-6711): "never only hidden". One small Text per rare pile, so memory is negligible.
- [CONFIRMED] N1-22 (divergence; wider than the finding says): a player standing on a death pile when it opens to them can be told 'not-recipient' and lose it.
  - The steps:
    - the phone opens the free-for-all on its own clock (groundLoot.js:96-97) and asks on the first frame a pile under its feet is open (groundLoot.js:261, 274-279);
    - the worker compares its own clock (index.js:4558-4565) and answers 'not-recipient';
    - the phone treats 'not-recipient' as a permanent no and sets `_expired` (wsClient.js:2414-2415, 2449), so the pile leaves this screen;
    - nothing re-sends it when the window really opens, because only a dropped item is told again (itemdrop.js:206).
  - It comes back only with the next whole list for the zone: a rejoin, a zone change or a roster re-send.
  - It takes a clock ahead of the worker's by more than the message's trip time (tens of milliseconds), not seconds.
  - Who is hurt:
    - a bystander waiting for a stranger's bag;
    - in No man's land, the dead player waiting out the killer's minute on their own bag (nomansland.js:826-828). That is an honest player losing their own bag.
  - Normally the owner is never refused, because they are the recipient all along (index.js:4422).
  - How checked: traced the ask, the gate and the bucket. mp-deathpile (tools/qa/mp/mp-deathpile.mjs) runs every phone on the worker's own machine, so its clocks agree and it cannot see this.
  - Docs: v2.3.2545 kept death piles on a 20 px trigger for fear of exactly this 'not-recipient' drop (groundLoot.js:252-258). item-drop.md:170-172 covers only "that much off".
- [CONFIRMED] N1-22 (divergence, trivial): a refusal does not say whether the hand or the pet asked.

- [CONFIRMED] G4-15 (guard-drift): "may I take this pile?" is written twice, once for the hand (groundLoot.js) and once for the pet vacuum (BroTown.jsx:7145-7205).
  - The missing safe-zone and 100 ms gates are harmless: the pet waits 500 ms (BroTown.jsx:7148).
  - The real drift is the v2.3.3476 corpse gate. The hand stands still while `S._dying` (groundLoot.js:52); the pet does not.
  - While dying, a pet out with you asks for your own death pile, which lies at your feet. The worker refuses 'dead' (index.js:4582). - [REFUTED] G4-15 (divergence): "the phone keeps a remains pile forever, so a missed loot_despawn leaves it on screen".
  - What stops it: the renderer cuts every pile that is not a death pile or a drop at 30 s, remains included (effectsRenderer.js:10459); and every rejoin's state_sync replaces the whole list (wsClient.js:1860).
  - groundLoot.js's "persist forever" (groundLoot.js:32-35, 45) is dead in practice. That also corrects V7's "remains piles never expire".
- [CONFIRMED] S1-9 (persistence): a room restart destroys every pile, including a dead player's whole bag.
  - The death saves the bag without its items at once (index.js:3476, 3491). The pile exists only in `this.loot` (index.js:1433).
  - So a deploy inside the pile's 2 minutes destroys the bag for good. The phone's rejoin list is simply empty, with no word to the player.
  - Docs:
    - handoff rule 11 (docs/ARCHITECTURE-HANDOFF.md:222-225) says memory is acceptable only when a wipe "loses nothing of value";
    - item-drop.md:66-69 accepts this loss for a dropped item, not for a death pile;
    - the code's note (index.js:4125-4128) treats in-memory piles only as safe for the id counter.
- [PLAUSIBLE] S1-9 (persistence, honest solo player): the same loss without a deploy.
  - When the last socket closes, the tick stops (index.js:6454) and nothing keeps the room awake: no alarms (handoff rule 12), and sockets use hibernation (index.js:4927).
  - A solo player who dies and puts the phone down may come back to a room rebuilt from scratch, with no pile.
  - Unproven link: how soon Cloudflare drops an empty room's memory. The repo says only "An empty room stops its tick ... and costs nothing" (docs/WORLD-ARCHITECTURE.md:309-311).
- [REFUTED] S1-9 (opacity): "a pile whose contributors all left or died before a DoT kill has an empty recipient list".
  - What stops it: the kill falls back to the killer when no contributor qualifies (server/src/combat.js:1903-1906).
  - An empty list does happen when only contributors under 5% remain (combat.js:1891-1899). That pile is unclaimable by design: "a share of 5% or more ... may take from the pile" (docs/GDD-AS-BUILT.md:668). It lies 60 s on the worker for nobody.

## What needs further thought
- How long should a monster's pile last? Every phone shows 30 seconds, and its last-call pulse counts to that. The worker keeps 60, and the game's own description says 60. One of them should change.
- Should a dead player's bag survive a server update? Today a deploy within two minutes of a death destroys the bag, and the rule for the server says anything of value must be saved. The choices:
  - save the death pile: one row per death, deleted when it is taken or ends;
  - keep the bag in the player's record until the pile is picked up;
  - accept it and say so in the game's description.
- Should the phone judge a pile's windows on its own clock at all? The dropped item already sends "time left" so that a phone with a wrong clock still agrees. Doing the same for every pile would end the early 'not-recipient' and the skewed 30 s.
- Making it explicit:
  - The worker owns one `phase` per pile ('owner', 'open', 'spent') and sends its windows as time left (the `_dropWire` shape, index.js:4291-4300), which the phone turns into its own clock once, on arrival.
  - One `mayAsk(pile, me, now, {dying, viaPet})` serves both the hand and the pet.
  - One remover (the pickup loop); the renderer only reads `_expired`.
  - Refusals carry `viaPet` and a "retry at", so the phone never guesses which of its asks was refused.
  - A death pile is stored if the owner wants it to survive deploys.
- Quick fixes vs design work:
  - One-line fixes:
    - treat 'not-recipient' on a death pile as a retry, not a drop, or send death piles' windows as time left;
    - add `S._dying` to the pet vacuum's gate;
    - destroy RARE DROP! at 2.4 s;
    - echo `viaPet` in loot_pickup_rejected;
    - correct the four stale comments.
  - Owner decisions:
    - 30 s or 60 s for a monster's pile;
    - the death bag across a restart;
    - whether the phone-made remains pile should exist at all (V7's G1-9; matching its "once per life" note would add more fake piles).
