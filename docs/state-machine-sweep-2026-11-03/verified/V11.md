# V11 · Changing place on the phone, the one loading screen, and the Wheel's ground helper
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Severity: medium (an honest player can lose their bag to monsters behind the dungeon's loading screen; a ground helper that fails to start, or whose start lists fail to load, leaves the world without ground, buildings or doors until a reload; the rest are glitches: wrong farm music, a blank arena floor, today's town shown for a moment)
- Who can trigger it: honest player: at a dungeon mouth with monsters about, tap Enter on a slow first load (up to 8 s blind and still hittable); on a computer, press E again during that load; take the Land Office's Travel to Farm (wrong music, every time); start the Wheel during a network blip. Modified phone only: step to the farm or town from any fight (the worker accepts it).
- Summary: Moving between places (into and out of dungeons, onto your farm, back after a death) is done by about nine pieces of code. Each clears its own list of leftovers, and five of them skip the shared list. One dark loading screen has five owners, and each removes it by its own rule. The dungeon's loading screen neither holds you nor shields you, so monsters can kill you behind it. If the Wheel's ground helper, or one of its start lists, fails to load, nothing tries again until you reload.
- Key files: src/networking/gameEvents.js:953-990, src/game/zoneTransitions.js:214-253, src/game/zoneTransitions.js:389-434, src/game/respawn.js:37-110, src/game/wheelTrial.js:282-323, public/tools/world/core/ground-worker.js:637-770

## The machine
- Entity, and where its state lives: the local player's place, `S.currentZone`, and the leftovers that belong to a place (loot, particles, arrows, slime orbs, bursts, `lockedTarget`, `npcs`, plus clearZoneLocalFx's list: the campfire, peers' fires, extraction, dash, whirl). Each way of moving writes these itself: zoneTransitions.js:852, 1221, 1506; wheelDungeons.js:261; respawn.js:64; gameEvents.js:915, 1064; FeedSeedPanel.jsx:184; BroTown.jsx:13548. The dungeon run lives in `S._wheelDungeonOpening`, `S._serverDungeon` and `S._dungeonBack` (gameEvents.js:953-990). The one veil is `_zoneLoadEl` (zoneTransitions.js:214), and five owners hold it: `S._zoneLoading`, `S._townArtHold`, `S._wheelTripVeil`, `S._wheelDungeonOpening` and `S._farmArtHold`. The ground helper lives in `_w`, `_initP` and `_info` (wheelTrial.js:44-46), and "the Wheel is loaded" is `_ready` (worldTrial.js:123). The helper's one-time facts are `swatches` and `objects` (ground-worker.js:112, 503-562), and its downloads are `dlJobs`, `dlLine` and `dlActive` (ground-worker.js:155-158).
- States:
  - In the Wheel: worker ready, `_ready` true, no veil.
  - Opening: at a dungeon mouth, veil up for at most 8 s. Still in the Wheel on both sides; not held, not shielded.
  - In an arena (`dungeon:<id>`), or cleared (the 30 s card).
  - Dead: 5 s, still in the place you died in.
  - Today's town on the trip: trip wanted, trip veil up, the town's art never fetched.
  - Gate loading: on the stairs, frozen, veil up, at most 15 s.
  - On the farm: first held (veil up, frozen, at most 12 s), then free.
  - Worker states: stopped, starting, ready, failed (kept as failed), and stopped again after 5 s away. Failed is final for the visit.
  - Retired: World View and the old lands; the client-run dungeon waves and depth tiers; the old worker's farm and local deaths.
- Moves:
  - Down the stairs: today's town → gate loading → Wheel (zoneTransitions.js:829-852). The full wipe is at 1133-1154.
  - Enter at a mouth: Wheel → opening (wheelDungeons.js:192-203 → gameEvents.js:953-976).
  - The opening finishes (loads done or 8 s): opening → arena (gameEvents.js:976-987 → `_enterArena` 914-943). There is NO clearZoneLocalFx and no lock clear here.
  - !! A second Enter during the opening (a computer's E): the worker ends the first run and opens a new one, and the first run's late hide lifts the veil (wheelDungeons.js:194-197; dungeon.js:299-311; gameEvents.js:978-979).
  - !! Killed during the opening: opening → dead. Nothing holds or shields you (BroTown.jsx:5479; dungeon.js:197-201).
  - Wheel run's door or clear: arena → today's town + trip (wheelDungeons.js:247-296). This is the fullest wipe.
  - Workshop door: arena → farm (zoneTransitions.js:1504-1568). It clears the fx and the lock.
  - !! Workshop clear: arena → farm (gameEvents.js:1054-1090). No fx clear, no lock clear.
  - Death: dead → today's town + trip (respawn.js:37-110). No clearZoneLocalFx and no loot wipe here; the trip's stairs wipe comes about 0.4 s later.
  - !! Land Office or Feed & Seed: Wheel → farm, held (BroTown.jsx:13545-13568; FeedSeedPanel.jsx:178-197). No clearZoneLocalFx. The Land Office also restarts the town's music (13567).
  - The farm's gate: farm → today's town + trip (zoneTransitions.js:1208-1316).
  - Worker start: stopped → starting → ready (wheelTrial.js:282-364). A load error leaves it failed (291-297, 329), and nothing retries until a stop 5 s away (worldTrial.js:524-537).
  - !! `_ready = true` is written by any preloadWheel that ends, without checking that its trip is still the current one (worldTrial.js:442).
- Diagram edges:
  - `Wheel --Enter at a mouth--> Opening (veil up, <=8 s)`
  - `Opening --loads done or 8 s--> Arena`
  - `Opening --second E on a computer--> Opening2 !! (first lifts the veil, unloads the shared floor)`
  - `Opening --monsters kill you behind the veil--> Dead !! (no hold, bag dropped)`
  - `Arena --door or clear (Wheel run)--> Town stop (trip veil)`
  - `Arena --clear (Workshop run)--> Farm !! (no zone-local wipe, no lock clear)`
  - `Dead --respawn after 5 s--> Town stop (trip veil)`
  - `Town stop --the opening's late hide--> Town shown !! (veil gone, flag still set)`
  - `Town stop --stairs, Wheel not loaded--> Gate loading`
  - `Gate loading --done or 15 s--> Wheel`
  - `Wheel --Land Office / Feed & Seed--> Farm held !! (no zone-local wipe; Land Office plays town music)`
  - `Farm --gate--> Town stop`
  - `Worker failed --stay, or die and come back--> Worker failed !! (only a reload retries)`
  - `Worker stopped (5 s away) --an old preload ends--> "loaded" with no worker !!`

## What needs further thought
- Should the dungeon's loading screen protect you, or come only once you are inside? Two ways: hold you still and have the worker stop monsters hitting you while you enter, or enter first and load behind a screen in the arena. Today you can lose your bag behind it.
- Should every change of place go through one door: one function that moves you, clears the leftovers, frees the art, sets the music and tells the worker? That would cover the farm trips, the dungeon's way in and out, and the respawn. Today there are nine hand copies, and the next new leftover will leak through some of them.
- When the Wheel's ground helper or one of its start lists fails to load, should the game quietly try again (and say so) instead of leaving the world bare until a reload?
- Making it explicit:
  - `changeZone(S, to, { why, at, veil })` in zoneTransitions.js, called by every way. It flips `S.currentZone`, runs ONE wipe table (clearZoneLocalFx grown to cover the arrays, the bursts and the lock), releases the left place's art, starts the music and sends the `move`.
  - The veil becomes a list of holders, `veil.hold(owner, name)` / `veil.release(owner)`. The element goes only when the list is empty, so no owner can lift another's.
  - The run becomes one record, `S._run = { phase: 'asked'|'opening'|'arena'|'cleared'|'leaving', zone, back }`. The Enter guard and the movement freeze read `phase`.
  - The ground helper becomes `{ gen, phase: 'stopped'|'starting'|'ready'|'failed', failedAt }`. preloadWheel writes `_ready` only for its own `gen`, and failed retries after a wait.
  - The worker's start fetches go through one `fetchWithin(url, ms, tries)`, and 'ready' says which lists came.
- Quick fixes vs design work:
  - One-line fixes:
    - drop `startZoneAmbient('town')` from the Land Office trip (BroTown.jsx:13567);
    - move the opening's `hideZoneLoadingOverlay()` below its "still mine?" check (gameEvents.js:978-979);
    - add `_wheelDungeonOpening` to the Enter guard (wheelDungeons.js:194) and to the movement freeze (BroTown.jsx:5479);
    - call clearZoneLocalFx and clear the lock in respawn.js, `_enterArena`, the Workshop clear and the two farm trips;
    - add a zone check to the campfire's reach test (BroTown.jsx:6026);
    - call `pump()` after a promotion (ground-worker.js:173);
    - add `!failed.has` to prefetchRound (ground-worker.js:244);
    - add a generation number for `_ready`.
  - Design work: what is fair while entering a dungeon (a worker-side "entering" state, or no screen before the arena); the one door for changing place; and how the worker's start retries, with what the player sees meanwhile.
