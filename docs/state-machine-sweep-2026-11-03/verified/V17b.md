# V17b · Pictures that stream in and out: monster looks, building sheets, other players' baked figures, and the per-frame figure fixes
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Severity: high (phones' memory and the screen: a pond with three anglers in different outfits on one screen keeps ~4 MB a second of pictures until an iPhone tab dies (R1-8); in the Wheel, monsters drawn without their art, or a dark screen, after an ordinary walk (R6-5); invisible, walk-through buildings after one failed download (R11-1). No items, gold or cheating involved)
- Who can trigger it: honest player: fish at a pond while two other players in different outfits fish on the same screen (R1-8); walk past the Water Caves' and the Poison Forest's monsters near the commons, step toward town and come back within about 10 s (R6-5); a network blip while a monster look or a building sheet downloads (R9-5, R11-1); stand near a player with drawings who cooks or lights a fire while your phone is short of memory (R3-5)
- Summary: Every picture the game streams moves through "not loaded, loading, ready, failed, freed", but each system keeps that in its own variables. Several moves are wrong or missing: a failed monster download is marked "ready" and drawn as a circle; two monster types sharing one picture set can lose it while one still says "ready"; a failed building sheet is never retried while you stand near it; and the fishing picture cache throws pictures away without truly freeing them, which can fill an iPhone's memory in about a minute.
- Key files: src/rendering/systems/entityRenderer.js:4999, src/rendering/wheelMonsterArt.js:148, src/rendering/zoneTextures.js:134, src/rendering/wheelObjects.js:493, src/rendering/systems/effectsRenderer.js:5356, src/rendering/systems/effectsRenderer.js:14571

## The machine
- Entity, and where its state lives: one streamed picture set (an "art record") per owner, its state spread over: the Wheel's monster looks, `_looks` look → {state, gen, t0, lastNear} (src/rendering/wheelMonsterArt.js:103), over each sprite module's memoised `loadPromise` and sheet tables (src/rendering/fishmanSprites.js:30-31, src/rendering/fireGoblinSprites.js:98-104) and zoneTextures' `_bundles` / `_crops` / `_gen` (src/rendering/zoneTextures.js:54-60); the Wheel objects' sheet pages, `_pages` page → {state, gen, wanted, palette, picture} and `_loading` (src/rendering/wheelObjects.js:315-316); other players' figure bakes `_peerFigs` / `_peerFigPending` / `_peerFigFailAt`, `_peerChopBakes` / `_peerChopPending` / `_peerChopFailAt`, `_peerInks` / `_peerInkPending` (src/rendering/systems/effectsRenderer.js:5340-5345, :5398-5403, :5119-5138); a peer display's held armoured frame `_peerLastMasked` / `_peerLastPD` with `_peerQueue` / `_peerPending` (src/rendering/systems/entityRenderer.js:3815-3817, :12880-12882); the fishing overlay LRU `_fishTopCache` (entityRenderer.js:4702); the harvest stand-in pool `_remoteSkillSprites` (effectsRenderer.js:14075); each figure's borrowed-transform marks `_jfx` / `_jumpY` / `_emo` / `_swimY` (src/rendering/rideFx.js:246-252, src/rendering/emoteFx.js:304-310, src/rendering/swimFx.js:208)
- States:
  - Absent: no record, nothing loaded.
  - Loading: a pending promise or a bake's pending flag; a Wheel monster or object is not drawn, a peer keeps their walking body.
  - Ready: every part arrived; drawn.
  - Ready-but-empty: marked ready with parts missing (a swallowed failure) or with its shared module unloaded under it; drawn as a stand-in, or with dead textures.
  - Failed: a rejected load; an object page stays undrawn, a peer figure waits 15 s; never reached by a monster look.
  - Held: a peer's last armoured frame shown while its bake is pending.
  - Freed: let go (idle, out of range, zone change); a gen bump drops loads still on their way (stale).
  - Evicted: pushed out of a capped cache, supposed to be freed.
- Moves:
  - a monster of the look within LOAD_R or the foreign box: Absent → Loading (wheelMonsterArt.js:257, :163-164)
  - every module promise settles: Loading → Ready (wheelMonsterArt.js:166-168), even when strips 404'd (zoneTextures.js:134, fireGoblinSprites.js:110): the move Loading → Failed or Partial is MISSING
  - loadLook rejects: Loading → Failed (wheelMonsterArt.js:173-175): unreachable, nothing rejects (src/rendering/monsterRecolor.js:157-160, effectsRenderer.js:2141)
  - no monster of the look in keep for FREE_AFTER 10 s: Ready → Freed, its module unloaded unless a sibling is in THIS tick's keep (wheelMonsterArt.js:148-150, :258-262): WRONG for a sibling still held in `_looks`, which stays Ready over freed art (:161, :259)
  - leaving the Wheel or another homes zone: all → Freed, gen++ (wheelMonsterArt.js:209-216); the art itself 400 ms later (src/game/zoneTransitions.js:60-64)
  - an object of the page near the view and a free slot (MAX_LOADING 2): Absent → Loading (wheelObjects.js:491-495)
  - Assets.load rejects: Loading → Failed (wheelObjects.js:348-352); while near it is only re-stamped (:493, :497): the retry Failed → Loading is MISSING
  - no object of the page within DROP_MARGIN for 2-4 s: Ready or Failed → Absent (wheelObjects.js:497-498)
  - a gathering peer drawn with no figure in their colours: Absent → Loading (effectsRenderer.js:5406, :5354); lands → Ready (:5415, :5361); throws → Failed for 15 s (:5417, :5138), but the drawings layer → Absent, retried next frame (:5362-5363): its failure clock is MISSING
  - nobody drew a figure for 15 s, or another needs room: Ready → Freed (effectsRenderer.js:14589-14609, :5442-5460)
  - a peer's armoured frame missing while one is held: Held, sent to the worker (entityRenderer.js:3836-3840); a bake made while the belt sheet loads is not cached (:3930), so it stays Held every frame with no deadline
  - fishing overlay miss: baked and inserted (entityRenderer.js:4999-5000); past 64 the oldest is Evicted with `ot.destroy(true)` (:5001): WRONG, its canvas stays in Pixi's Cache (docs/TRAPS.md:5449)
  - a peer leaves the room: their pool entry reaped (effectsRenderer.js:14571-14585): WRONG, `ent.gear` sprites left in gestureLayer
- Diagram edges:
  - `Absent --a monster or object near, or a peer drawn--> Loading`
  - `Loading --every part arrived--> Ready`
  - `Loading --a part 404s or a canvas is refused (swallowed)--> Ready` !! should be Failed or Partial
  - `Loading --Assets.load rejects (object page)--> Failed`
  - `Failed --still near (only re-stamped)--> Failed` !! no retry
  - `Failed --15 s clock (peer figure)--> Absent`
  - `Loading --drawings-layer bake throws--> Absent` !! retried next frame, no clock
  - `Ready --out of range or idle 10-15 s--> Freed`
  - `Ready --sibling look's free unloads the shared module--> Ready` !! over freed art
  - `Freed --wanted again--> Loading`
  - `Ready --left the Wheel or zone (gen++)--> Freed`
  - `Held --bake not cached (belt still loading)--> Held` !! no deadline
  - `Ready(LRU) --evicted past 64--> Freed` !! canvas kept in Pixi's Cache
  - `Ready(pool) --peer leaves the room--> Freed` !! gear sprites kept

## What needs further thought
- When a monster's pictures fail to download in the Wheel, which is better: its monsters invisible until a retry works (the preloading law, though they can still hit you), or drawn in a plain stand-in body? Today a failure silently gives the stand-in, and nothing retries.
- Should every streamed picture (monster looks, building sheets, other players' figures) retry on its own clock after a failure and give up after a time limit, as other players' figures already do (15 s)?
- The fishing picture is made once per animation frame per angler. Should it be made once per outfit instead, so a crowded pond cannot outgrow its cache?
- Making it explicit: one small shared "art record" helper for every streamed picture: `{ state: 'absent' | 'loading' | 'ready' | 'partial' | 'failed', gen, retryAt, holders }`. One `artLoad(rec)` reports 'partial' or 'failed' instead of swallowing, with a load timeout and a failure clock. A holder count per shared module or loader frees a module only when its last holder goes. One `artRelease(rec)` points sprites at Texture.EMPTY first and releases through releaseCanvasSource. The monster looks, the object pages, the peer bakes and the fishing cache would each keep one of these instead of their own flags.
- Quick fixes vs design work: one-line fixes: release an evicted fishing overlay's canvas through releaseCanvasSource (R1-8; this stops the leak, not the re-baking); destroy `ent.gear` in the peer-left reap (R4-15); give the drawings layer the figures' failure clock and PEER_FIG_LOAD_MS (R3-5); let a failed object page drop after a few seconds even when near (R11-1); in freeLook, count a sibling held in `_looks`, not only one in this tick's keep (R6-5); send `pose` again after a reconnect (R10-8). Need a decision: what a failed monster look shows (R9-5); keying the fishing overlay per outfit (R1-8); a deadline for a peer's held armour frame (R1-6); one figure-transform stage instead of four passes that patch and un-patch (R10-8).
