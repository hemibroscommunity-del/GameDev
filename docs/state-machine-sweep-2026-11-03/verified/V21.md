# V21 · "In a fight or not" on the worker: the fight clocks, and what a losing player may still do with their goods
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Severity: medium (cheating in No man's land: a modified phone keeps gold and bars the rule gives the killer, or hands them to a friend, so the killer's pile comes up short; no honest player loses their own things)
- Who can trigger it: modified phone only for every open road (the honest game opens those counters only at their buildings in town, where nobody can be hit under the rule, and its duel button always sends a 0 stake); an honest player can tap a dungeon mouth mid-fight and leave No man's land at once (S5-13), and can place banked points mid-duel (harmless)
- Summary: The worker answers "is this player fighting?" with about nine clocks (a hit taken, a hit dealt, the room's player-fight clock, a duel, the kill window, the stagger), each rule choosing its own clock and length. That mostly works, though copies disagree. The real gap: the "fight lock", meant to stop a losing player emptying their bag before the killer takes it, guards six roads; at least seven others stay open to a modified phone (forging armour, amulet crafting, the jackpot, duel stakes, arena stakes, gambling, gift trades).
- Key files: server/src/nomansland.js:234, server/src/armorforge.js:77, server/src/cadence.js:158, server/src/duel.js:191, server/src/cooking.js:593, server/src/index.js:3640

## The machine
- Entity, and where its state lives: one player's "fighting" status. On playerState (memory, rebuilt at every join, join.js:876): `ps.lastDamageAt` (a blow taken), `ps._lastDealtAt` (a blow dealt), `ps._nmlLastHitBy/_nmlLastHitAt` (nomansland.js:439-440, 1002-1007), `ps._staggerUntil` (pvpextras.js:272-280), `ps._arenaMatch`. On the room (memory, survives a rejoin, lost on a deploy): `_pvpHealClocks` {pvpAt, healAt} (cooking.js:637-668), `_duels` read by `_duelFor` (duel.js:93), `_pvpEngage` (pvpextras.js:160-180). Nothing is saved.
- States:
  - Quiet: no clock running.
  - Hit lately (under 2 s since a blow taken): mana refills at the slow rate (index.js:3641, 3791).
  - Monster fight (under 6 s since a blow taken or dealt): no HP trickle in the wilds, no safe-ground heal (index.js:3698-3699; safeheal.js:60-62). (`ooc`, 5 s taken, index.js:3640, is computed and read by nothing.)
  - Cooling (6-10 s): HP refills, but rest, the points reset and the one-bite rule still say "fight" (rest.js:86-90; cooking.js:608-622; pointsreset.js:81).
  - Provoking (under 10 s since a blow dealt, on a monster you hurt): it may follow you onto the safe ground (wheelzone.js:259-280).
  - Player fight (a duel, or under 10 s since a blow between players either way, ticks included): the fight lock (nomansland.js:247-252 via farmorders.js:195-199), the body lingers on close (linger.js:61-80), the one-bite rule (cooking.js:593-598). No HP regen in a duel or arena match (index.js:3676-3683).
  - Kill-attributed (5 s since a No man's land blow or its tick): a death now is that attacker's kill and costs the bag, the gold and the spares (nomansland.js:674, 689-795).
  - Staggered (1.5 s after being parried by a player): no swing, special, ability or burst; a dodge still goes, by design (abilities.js:606, burst.js:140, abilities.js:1175, combat.js:1224, 2219).
  - Dying (5 s), then respawn.
- Moves:
  - a blow reaches you (landed, blocked or dodged; a graced one never): Quiet → Monster fight (combat.js:449, 486, 605; the graces return first at :430-447)
  - you land a direct blow: → Monster fight (`_lastDealtAt`: combat.js:1482, 2505; abilities.js:888; burst.js:185; arrowblast.js:165; pvpextras.js:535)
  - !! your burn, root, splash, spark or thorn recoil hurts a monster: no stamp at all (`_applyMonsterDot` index.js:3329-3380; callers index.js:2697, combat.js:257, 1805, hits.js:270), so Cooling and the end of Provoking can start while your tick still bites
  - any blow between players, ticks included: → Player fight for both sides (combat.js:2510; pvpextras.js:536; cooking.js:659-664)
  - a duel accepted / resolved: → / ← Player fight (duel.js:207-210, 228-232)
  - a No man's land blow or tick: → Kill-attributed (nomansland.js:439-440, 1002-1007)
  - parried by a player: → Staggered (pvpextras.js:280)
  - time: each window lapses on its own length (6 s index.js:1095; 10 s data.js:539, rest.js:56, wheelzone.js:128, pvpextras.js:106; 5 s nomansland.js:147; 1.5 s index.js:1292)
  - respawn: both playerState stamps to 0, the room's clock kept (index.js:3542-3543)
  - !! rejoin: playerState rebuilt, a monster fight forgotten, the room's player clock kept (join.js:876, 1336; documented cooking.js:604-607)
  - deploy: everything gone (memory only)
  - acts that move value, and their gates in a Player fight (dying = the dying/dead/disconnected check):
    - LOCKED (`_bagLockedByFight`): Diego buys (shop.js:643), auction house list, buy, bid (store.js:568, 650), market order (market.js:299), trade2 confirm, both sides (trade2.js:229-234), farm plant and compost (farm.js:425), item drop (itemdrop.js:109); the farm order board asks the same test directly (farmorders.js:258, S7-2)
    - !! OPEN, turn forfeitable goods into something the loss cannot take: forge_armor, 5 bars into a piece in flight (armorforge.js:77-112; dying :84; no gear lock, no place); amulet craft, gold into the worn amulet, and amulet gem (amulet.js:123-207; dying :127; gear lock :138, :176); jackpot_deposit, gold into this week's tickets (cadence.js:158-190; dying :165); arena sponsor, gold into an escrowed stake (gladiator.js:440-470; no dying check); weapon gem and shield gem, a bag gem into a worn piece (weapongem.js:252-257; shieldgem.js:143-147); forge_weapon, harden_weapon, equip_request (gear.js:492, 706; hardening.js:241; dying and gear lock only; S7-12)
    - !! OPEN, move goods to a friend: duel stake (duel.js:141-215; dying :156, same zone :175; a third-party kill pays the partner, :219-226, 247-256); gift trade (trade.js:127-174; no dying check; S6-1)
    - OPEN, spite only (the victim loses the stake either way): Hall roll, Ace's coin flip, Ace's item wager (gamble.js:264-287, 226-262, 159-220; dying :267, :229, :162)
    - SOUND BY PLACE: bank_deposit (bank.js:212-260; the Bank's door :231, on the safe ground where no rule blow lands, nomansland.js:188, 296)
    - NOT GOODS: prog3_allocate (prog3.js:1401-1405, no checks at all); points_reset (pointsreset.js:78 dead, :81 `_anyFightAt`, :85 gap); ability_use (index.js:3257 dying; no stagger check, by design)
    - !! LEAVE THE FIGHT: dungeon_start (dungeon.js:294-297 alive only; a mouth wheeldungeon.js:162-165; no fight or duel check, S5-13)
- Diagram edges:
  - `Quiet --blow taken or dealt directly--> MonsterFight`
  - `MonsterFight --6 s with no stamp--> Cooling`
  - `Cooling --10 s since the last stamp--> Quiet`
  - `MonsterFight --only your tick still biting a monster--> Cooling` !! (ticks never stamp)
  - `MonsterFight --rejoin--> Quiet` !! (stamps live on playerState)
  - `Quiet --blow between players, or a duel accepted--> PlayerFight`
  - `PlayerFight --10 s since the last such blow, no duel--> Quiet`
  - `PlayerFight --parried--> Staggered`
  - `Staggered --1.5 s--> PlayerFight`
  - `PlayerFight --No man's land blow or tick--> KillAttributed`
  - `KillAttributed --death--> Dying (bag, gold, spares to the killer)`
  - `Dying --respawn after 5 s--> Quiet`
  - `PlayerFight --forge_armor, amulet craft, jackpot, duel stake, arena stake, gamble, gift--> PlayerFight` !! (goods leave the loss's reach; no lock)
  - `PlayerFight --dungeon_start at a mouth--> OutOfNoMansLand` !! (no fight check)

## What needs further thought
- Should every act that turns gold, bars or bag items into something a No man's land death keeps (worn gear, a just-forged piece, jackpot tickets, a duel or arena stake, a friend's purse) be refused in a fight with a player, as the six shop and trade roads are? The spec still calls forging "only rearranging the bag", a line written before the loss took your gold.
- Should a dungeon mouth refuse a player who is in a fight with a player, the way the linger keeps a disconnecting body in the world?
- Should a red skull's bag also be held in a fight with a monster? The lock watches player fights only, though the rule says a red skull killed by a monster loses everything it carries.
- Making it explicit: one `_fightState(pid, ps, now)` in one file returning {takenAt, dealtAt (stamped by every damage path, ticks included), pvpAt (the room's clock), duel, arena, staggerUntil, killBy}, with named windows (REGEN_QUIET 6 s, FIGHT 10 s, KILL 5 s) and three predicates (`inMonsterFight`, `inPlayerFight`, `inAnyFight`) that regen, safe heal, rest, reset, bite, lock, linger and provoke all call. For goods, one table naming each message that moves gold or bag items and its gate ('fightlock', 'place' or 'open by design'), checked once in the router before the handler, plus a test that fails any handler debiting coins or the bag without a row. No new messages: each refusal keeps its handler's existing answer.
- Quick fixes vs design work: one-line fixes are adding `_bagLockedByFight(pid)` to forge_armor, amulet craft and gem, jackpot_deposit, the duel accept when the stake is above 0, arena sponsor, the three gambles (and the gift lane, S6-1); deleting `ooc`; correcting wheelzone.js:256-258; making REST.QUIET_MS read PVP_HEAL.WINDOW_MS; a mirror test for the gambling numbers. Decisions for the owner: whether forging, hardening, gem-setting and equipping into worn slots count as "leaving the bag" (S7-12); whether a mouth refuses a player fight (S5-13); whether rest should follow `_anyFightAt` under `oneformula: false`; whether ticks should stamp `_lastDealtAt`.
