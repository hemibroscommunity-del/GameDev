# V7 · A world monster's life across its deaths and respawns (the worker's monster and each phone's copy)
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Severity: medium (a player can be left unable to hit a respawned blue slime that is attacking them; a ball from the monster's previous life can hit, even kill, a player about 19 s later with nothing drawn; the fake Remains piles and the free lifesteal heal are small. No items or gold are lost or minted on the worker)
- Who can trigger it: honest player: (1) kill a blue slime or snowman while its ball is in the air (easy with a bow) and stand still about 19 s; (2) melee-kill a blue slime during its 1.2 s lunge wind-up; (3) walk away from a slime another player killed about 2,600-2,800 px off, or background the phone for 20+ s right after a slime goes off, then come back to it; (4) be near any blue slime that swells; (5) take hits, let a friend kill the monster, then sword its next life
- Summary: A world monster dies and comes back as the same object. What it was doing (a thrown ball, a wind-up, a death swell) is wiped by hand-kept lists, and some slips through: a ball thrown just before death can hit you about 19 s later; a dying blue slime still lunges; your phone can keep a finished swell, so a respawned slime looks huge and cannot be hit; phones drop fake Remains piles; old damage still pays a lifesteal heal. The next new field will slip the same way.
- Key files: server/src/index.js:1977, server/src/telegraph.js:728, src/networking/wsClient.js:1297, src/game/monsterCarry.js:38, src/game/monsterCombat.js:268, server/src/combat.js:640

## The machine
- Entity, and where its state lives: one world monster `m` in `this.monsters['wheel']`. Its object and its id `wm-<home>-<k>` last the worker's life (wheelzone.js:347); death and respawn reuse both (index.js:1977-2063). Life: `alive`, `hp`, `respawnAt` (combat.js:1853-1874). Per-life side state on the worker: the ball `_projImpactAt/_projTargetId/_projTx/_projTy/_projFromX/_projFromY` (telegraph.js:672-688), the kit cast `_tgPhase/_tgUntil/_tgAim/_tgTarget/_tgRadius/_tgNextAt` (telegraph.js:387-511), the basic wind-up `_bw*`, the swell `_burstUntil/_burstR/_burstKiller/_burstSlot/_burstDone/_burstHitKiller` (telegraph.js:728-796), the burrow `_bur*`, a move `_mv*`, `dmgByPlayer`, `statuses`, `_kbDebt`, `_aggroOverride*`. On each player: the lifesteal ledger `ps.dmgFromMonster[m.id]` (combat.js:640-672). On each phone, a copy in `S.monsters`, kept for the whole visit; the tick updates it only within 2,800 px (wheelnear.js:1-50; tick.js:777-780 sends `alive` and `hp` every time). The copy holds `alive`, `curHp`, the swell `_burstFrom/_burstUntil/_burstScale`, the burrow `_burPhase/_burFrom/_burUntil/_invulnerable`, the pile flags `_lootDropped` and `_localRemnantDropped`, and its sound and drawing stamps.
- States:
  - Alive: idle or chasing; it may start a throw, a cast, a swing, a burrow or a move.
  - Winding up: a swing or throw (`_bwUntil`), a kit cast (`_tgPhase 'telegraph'`) or a move (`_mvPhase`); planted.
  - Ball in the air: `_projImpactAt` set, 650 ms for a slime or 900 ms for a snowman (index.js:1246-1258); runs alongside the other states.
  - Burrowed: a snowman's dig, pile or emerge (`_burPhase`), 2.4-4.2 s.
  - Swelling: a blue slime killed but still alive at 0 HP for 1.6 s (`_burstUntil`); it cannot be hurt and its kill waits.
  - Dead: `alive` false, `respawnAt` 18.75 s ahead on the Wheel; the tick skips it.
  - Respawned: back at its spawn with full HP, after a hand-written reset list.
  - Swollen on the phone: the copy holds a truthy `_burstUntil`, so it is drawn 3.5x and cannot be hit.
  - Fake pile dropped (phone): `_localRemnantDropped` or `_lootDropped` set on the copy.
  - Owed (each player): damage from this monster id still on file for a melee-kill refund.
- Moves:
  - throw wind-up ends: Winding up → Ball in the air, `monster_projectile` sent (telegraph.js:667-698)
  - a tick with `now >= _projImpactAt` on a LIVE monster: Ball in the air → landed, through the hit door's `snowball` row (index.js:2162-2236; hits.js:237-305)
  - target within CAST_RANGE and `now > atkCd`: Alive → Winding up (telegraph.js:475-511, called at index.js:2552); a stun or a parry cancels it (abilities.js:954-957; index.js:2924-2927)
  - killing blow on a blue slime: Alive or Winding up → Swelling (combat.js:1851-1852 → telegraph.js:728-752); it clears only the basic wind-up (telegraph.js:740). MISSING: the cast and the ball
  - killing blow on anything else: → Dead (combat.js:1853-1874). Nothing in flight is cleared; only `dmgByPlayer` (combat.js:2161)
  - 1.6 s pass: Swelling → blast → Dead (telegraph.js:758-796, run at index.js:2153, after the cast resolver at index.js:2135 and before the ball at index.js:2162)
  - `now >= respawnAt`: Dead → Respawned (index.js:1979-2061). MISSING: `_proj*`, `_kbDebt`
  - phone, swell event: → Swollen (gameEvents.js:1366-1371); the burst's execute ends it (gameEvents.js:1385-1386). MISSING: an end on its own clock, or on the tick's respawn edge (wsClient.js:1297-1310)
  - phone, a list replaces the copies: alive in both → the swell is carried (monsterCarry.js:38-46; wsClient.js:1806-1809, 4338). Wrong for a slime that died and came back in between
  - phone, every frame, a copy alive at `curHp <= 0`: → Fake pile (monsterCombat.js:268-281). Wrong under server loot
  - phone, the tick's alive → dead edge with no `monster_kill` first: → Fake pile (wsClient.js:1339-1363). Wrong under server loot
  - player hit by the monster: → Owed (hits.js:278); your melee kill refunds 90% and deletes (combat.js:661-672); your death, respawn or zone change clears it (index.js:3392, 3572; movement.js:818). MISSING: cleared when the monster's life ends

## What needs further thought
- When a monster dies, what should happen to things it has already started? A ball in the air could land on time (as the spec says for a target who walks away) or vanish, and a wound-up lunge could be cancelled. Today the ball lands 19 s late from the new monster and the lunge lands from the corpse.
- Should a phone ever make its own Remains pile in the world, now that the worker makes the real one? (Recommended: no. Any pile the phone makes there is a fake that the bag takes back.)
- Should a lifesteal heal count only damage from the monster's current life? (The spec says a kill of a monster that never hit you heals nothing.)
- Making it explicit: on the worker, a `m.life` counter bumped at each death, and one `_endLife(m, now)` called from the kill (and from the swell's start). It would end or drop everything in flight (ball, cast, wind-up, move, burrow, knockback debt) and be called again by the respawn, with a test that walks every `m._*` field the server writes. The lifesteal ledger would key on monster and life (or the kill clears every player's entry for that id). On the phone, one `resetLife(m)` beside the carry lists in monsterCarry.js, used by the tick's respawn edge. The carry would keep a swell only while the fresh copy still says `hp <= 0` (or sends `bu`), and `isIntangible` and the renderer would compare `_burstUntil` with the clock. One rule for piles: with `S._serverLoot` set, the phone makes none.
- Quick fixes vs design work: one-line fixes: clear `_proj*` and `_kbDebt` at death or in the respawn list (index.js:2042); end `_tgPhase` in `_startSlimeBurst` and the split start, or copy the move resolver's guard (monstermoves.js:471) into `_resolveMonsterTelegraph`; add `_burstFrom/_burstUntil/_burstScale` to the tick's respawn reset (wsClient.js:1297-1310) and a clock to `isIntangible`; skip `dropLocalRemnantOnce` and the tick's death-edge pile when `S._serverLoot` is set; delete every player's ledger entry for `m.id` on the kill. Decisions for the owner: the ball's fate at death; whether a freeze cancels a wind-up as a stun does; whether a parried cast takes its full cooldown (the comment) or 1.5 s (the code). Design work: one declared per-life reset on each side, instead of three hand lists that each new field must join. Also, mp-remnant should move off the retired map.
