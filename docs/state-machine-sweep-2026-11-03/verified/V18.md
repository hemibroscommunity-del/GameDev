# V18 · A character's look: the look stores, the stored record, the relay, the worn armour layer and the look picture caches
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Severity: medium (visual only: no items, gold or progress are at risk. Many customised players lose their own head overlay in busy scenes. A reconnect undoes an in-world shirt change. The armour picker can draw copper over iron. On a judge's computer, nine or more patterned shirts in view would make every printed shirt re-bake each frame, and that can drop whole frames.)
- Who can trigger it: honest player:
  - pick any non-default trousers or boots, then stand in a busy BroTown beside armoured players of other skin tones (your own head overlays get thrown out);
  - take the T-shirt off or on in the Hero page's Chest picker, then reconnect (a deploy or a network drop);
  - wear Iron Greaves and tap "Copper Greaves" in the Legs picker;
  - have one sprite download fail at the loading screen.

  Modified phone only: show any trait or armour layer to other players until its next join.
- Summary: A character's look lives in twenty stores on the phone, a record the server wrote once at creation and re-applies at every connect, and picture caches that bake a copy of each look, each cache with its own loading, ready, given-up and evicted rules. ## The machine
- Entity, and where its state lives: the look of every figure on screen (yours, each peer's, each townsperson drawn live), kept in three layers:
  - **Selection stores.** Module `_active` values plus `bt-*` localStorage: shirtCatalog.js:18, headwearCatalog.js:134, playerArt.js:209, patternCatalog.js:179, playerSkins.js:273-275. The worn armour layer is gearCatalog.js:47-68 (`bt-gear-v3-*`).
  - **The worker's record.** `char:<id>` (server/src/join.js:597-620) is written once, at creation. It is stamped onto the join frame (join.js:817-822) and onto every relay (join.js:574-584). The phone re-applies it at every state_sync (src/networking/wsClient.js:1605-1613 → src/game/characterRecord.js:220-242).
  - **Picture caches keyed by look:**
    - body sheets `_bodySheets` (playerSkins.js:309, no cap);
    - head overlays `_pickupHeadSheets` (:2408, cap 32);
    - gear sheets `_sheets` and the dressed-shirt LRU `_artSeen` (src/rendering/gearSheets.js:94, :807-809, cap 8);
    - five live head-piece recolour caches `_cache` (hairColorCatalog.js:118, hatColorCatalog.js:127, facialHairColorCatalog.js:82, eyewearColorCatalog.js:153, eyeStyleColorCatalog.js:148, cap 12 each; the shirt copy, shirtColorCatalog.js:97, has no caller);
    - their later-round timers (src/rendering/retryLooks.js:23-55; gearSheets.js:131-146).
- States:
  - Store: device value (after boot) / record value (after a join) / changed this session (only the shirt can change in the world).
  - Armour layer: matches the worn stat piece / diverged (a layer with no piece, or the wrong metal's art).
  - Cache entry, one of:
    - absent;
    - loading (held through a 2 s / 6 s retry backoff too);
    - ready;
    - given up (`[]`);
    - due again later (a later round);
    - evicted (its source freed at once, or 30 s later);
    - orphan (written back after it was evicted or dropped).
- Moves:
  - Page boot: localStorage → stores (shirtCatalog.js:19-22, no filter; headwearCatalog.js:135-142, filtered by the catalog; gearCatalog.js:56-58).
  - Join: the worker writes the record over every cosmetic key, the live armour and body-size keys included (join.js:817-819).
  - state_sync (every connect), in order:
    - record → stores, and a key the record lacks → its blank (characterRecord.js:220-242);
    - the store listeners re-prewarm the stand pose only (playerSkins.js:2837-2846);
    - a changed drawing drops every `/#art` sheet 500 ms later (playerSkins.js:2898, :2857-2878).
  - In-world shirt toggle: `st` and the gear shirt change (ItemDetailPopup.jsx:1130, :1273-1279). Nothing carries the change back to the record (join.js:597-620).
  - Armour echo: player_state `armor`/`legsArmor` → the layer is derived (wsClient.js:2818-2922, gearCatalog.js:186-195). Cosmetic writers run beside it (equipActions.js:315-326, ItemDetailPopup.jsx:1046-1056).
  - Cache entry moves:
    - a first draw or a preload: absent → loading (playerSkins.js:2301-2302, :2609; gearSheets.js:789; hairColorCatalog.js:191-192);
    - the bake lands: loading → ready (playerSkins.js:2085, :2567; gearSheets.js:727; hairColorCatalog.js:163);
    - a failed download: retry after 2 s, then 6 s. The returned promise waits for heads (:2570-2577) and gear (gearSheets.js:733-738) but NOT for bodies (:2086-2090);
    - the third failure: → given up; drawn again when its round is due: → loading (retryLooks.js:43-51);
    - over the cap: → evicted. Heads (:2437-2447) and hair etc. (hairColorCatalog.js:130-145) free the source 30 s later; the dressed shirt frees it at once (gearSheets.js:814-832);
    - a drawing edit: body sheets dropped and freed at once, head overlays freed after 30 s (playerSkins.js:2857-2878).
- Diagram edges:
  - `Absent --first draw / preload--> Loading`
  - `Loading --picture baked--> Ready`
  - `Loading --download fails (twice)--> Loading`
  - `Loading --third failure--> GivenUp`
  - `GivenUp --drawn and its round is due--> Loading`
  - `Ready --cap passed, oldest--> Evicted`
  - `Evicted --30 s later (heads, hair) / at once (dressed shirt)--> Freed`
  - `Loading --evicted or dropped, then the bake lands !!--> Orphan`
  - `Ready(your own head) --cap guard misspelt !!--> Evicted`
  - `Loading(body) --first failure, preload stops waiting !!--> GatePassed`
  - `DeviceLook --join / state_sync--> RecordLook`
  - `RecordLook --in-world shirt toggle--> ChangedThisSession`
  - `ChangedThisSession --reconnect !!--> RecordLook`
  - `ArmourMatches --Copper row tapped while wearing iron !!--> ArmourDiverged`

## What needs further thought
- Should an in-world shirt change survive a reconnect? If yes, the worker needs a way to update `st` in the record (or `st` should leave the record, as the armour layers did).
- Should a Chest or Legs picker ever offer art the player has no piece of (copper while wearing iron), or should those rows be the stat pieces only, retiring the cosmetic armour stash?
- On computers the look palette is off, so every look is a baked copy. Is 8 dressed shirts and 12 of each head piece the right cap for a crowd, or should a crowd fall back to the plain shirt and native colour instead of re-baking every frame?
- Making it explicit: one small `SheetCache` used by the body, head, gear and head-piece caches. It would have:
  - one state per key (absent, loading, ready, given up, evicted);
  - a generation number, so a bake that lands after its key was evicted or dropped throws itself away;
  - one retry policy whose promise always waits through the backoff;
  - one release rule (point holders at an empty texture, or wait 30 s);
  - a cap that never evicts a key touched in the current frame or owned by the local player.

  The armour layer would become a pure `wornLayer(rpg, shirt)` with no store for chest and legs. The record would get one apply step that skips keys this session changed on purpose (only `st` today).
- Quick fixes vs design work:
  - One-line fixes:
    - R7-2: build the guard's prefix with `_headSheetKey`;
    - R7-1: chain the body retry the way the head's is;
    - R8-11: put a try around each facing in hat, eyewear and eye style;
    - the picker: handle "a Copper row tapped while wearing a stat piece" in the stat branch;
    - add eyewear and eye-style "ready" promises to preloadTraits;
    - call `forget` in `_pickupHeadCap`;
    - gate the `_fxTrim*` records on `__btTrimVerify`;
    - delete InventoryPanel's dead 'equipped' block.
  - Decisions for the owner: the shirt-versus-record rule; what a crowd of dressed shirts should do (with a delayed release in the meantime); a cap for the body sheets; re-running the body preload when the record changes the look.
