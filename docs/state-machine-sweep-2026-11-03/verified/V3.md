# V3 · A player's connection on the server: joining, playing, the body left in a fight, a second tab taking over, leaving
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Severity: medium (cheating in fights between players, with no modified phone: a second tab gets a No man's land loser out with the bag the killer should take, and gives back up to 10 s of damage; a reload lets an arena fighter eat mid-match. No item or gold is duplicated or destroyed. Also a misleading screen on a failed join, and an owner's restore silently undone)
- Who can trigger it: honest player: open the game in a second tab and press Play during a fight (computer), or reload the page during an arena match and come back within 15 s; deploy/restart timing (a join that throws); owner only (kick then restore during a fight); modified phone only (a move saying `dead: true`; the join races, latent until the account check is switched on)
- Summary: Every player's connection to the server goes through a hidden life cycle: connecting, being checked, playing, leaving (or leaving a body in a fight for 20 seconds), and being replaced when the same character logs in again. The replacement skips the normal leaving steps, so a second browser tab rescues a losing player from a No man's land fight with their bag, and gives back up to 10 seconds of damage. A reload also lifts the arena's no-healing rule, and a failed join shows the wrong screen.
- Key files: server/src/join.js:739-774, server/src/index.js:6357-6458, server/src/linger.js:68-183, server/src/gladiator.js:389, server/src/index.js:5150-5161, server/src/admin.js:398-417

## The machine
- Entity, and where its state lives: one player's connection. On the worker: the socket's `session` in `this.sessions` (a Map, socket to session; `session.id` empty until join.js:774), the player's `this.playerState[id]` (built fresh at join.js:876; flags `lingering`, `_lingerUntil`, `disconnected`, `dying`, `dead`, `_regenDirty`/`_regenSaveAt`, `_arenaMatch`), `this._lingerBodies` (linger.js:93-94), the remembered place `this._lastPlaces` (lastplace.js:121-131), the duel's `away` clock (duel.js:260-268), the room's `tickInterval` (index.js:4937, 6454). On the phone: the refusal holders `_lgPending`, `_dvPending`... (wsClient.js:349-389) and `S._realtimeStatus`.
- States:
  - Opened: socket accepted, no player id yet; only `join` and `pong` are taken (index.js:4927-4937, 5077).
  - Checking: inside the join's checks (key, account check, freeze, linger); `session.id` still empty (join.js:674-747).
  - Refused: closed with a reason code and removed from the room at once (join.js:648-746; verify.js:90-94).
  - Joined: `session.id` set, a fresh player state read from storage, `state_sync` sent (join.js:774, 876, 921, 1726).
  - Lingering: socket gone, body kept up to 20 s where it stood, hittable (linger.js:68-97).
  - Superseded: an older socket of the same character, dropped by a newer join; its tab stops with "This account connected from another window" (join.js:757-772; wsClient.js:4646-4649).
  - Gone: state saved and removed, `player_leave` sent (index.js:6357-6458; linger.js:125-154).
  - Ghost body (latent): a player state with no session; reachable only through the account check's fetch gap.
- Moves:
  - socket upgrade: none → Opened; the tick starts with the first socket (index.js:4927-4937)
  - first `join`: Opened → Checking (index.js:5141-5155); a second `join` on a joined socket is closed 4011 (index.js:5141-5143)
  - a refusal: Checking → Refused, closed 4003 auth / 4004 frozen / 4014 verify / 4016 device / 4017 key / 4018 locked / 4010 linger_wait (join.js:648-746; verify.js:90-94)
  - all checks pass: Checking → Joined; the state is built from the place the phone names and the stored record (join.js:774, 876, 893, 921)
  - the join throws: → closed 4010 'join failed', nothing undone (index.js:5156-5161) — WRONG: 4010 is also the linger's code (linger.js:48)
  - the same character joins again while the old socket is live (second tab, reconnect over a half-open socket): old → Superseded WITHOUT webSocketClose; only the bot summary is written (join.js:757-772) — MISSING: the linger decision, the record flush (index.js:6370, 6385)
  - a close within 10 s of a hit between players, in the Wheel, not in a duel: Joined → Lingering (index.js:6370; linger.js:68-97)
  - any other close: Joined → Gone (index.js:6374-6446)
  - AFK 120 s, no join in 15 s, flooding: → webSocketClose, then removed (tick.js:165-219; flood.js:162-169)
  - kick / freeze / device or key put-out: → webSocketClose first, so a fight can linger (admin.js:72-76; devicelock.js:118-123)
  - linger time up, switch thrown, or died and respawned: Lingering → Gone, the body saved (linger.js:107-120, 125-154)
  - join from within 300 px of the body: Lingering → Joined, the body saved first (linger.js:174-178)
  - join from anywhere else: refused `linger_wait` + 4010 until the body has gone (linger.js:180-182; join.js:739-746)
  - the last socket closes: every linger ended, the tick stops (index.js:6451-6454)
  - owner's restore: refused only while a SOCKET is live (admin.js:407) — MISSING: a lingering body's state, which is saved over the restore when it ends (linger.js:150)
  - a reconnect into an arena match: the duel resumes (join.js:1598; duel.js:270-279) and the start message is sent again (gladiator.js:413-419) — MISSING: the no-healing flag, set only at activation (gladiator.js:389)
  - a deploy: everything in memory is gone, every socket closed on wake (index.js:1521-1527; handoff rule 11)
- Diagram edges:
  - `None --socket upgrade--> Opened`
  - `Opened --join--> Checking`
  - `Checking --a refusal and its close code--> Refused`
  - `Checking --checks pass, record read from storage--> Joined`
  - `Checking --the join throws (close 4010)--> Refused` !! the phone reads 4010 as "still in that fight"
  - `Checking --socket closes during the account check's fetch--> Ghost body` !! latent; nothing removes it
  - `Joined --same character joins from another tab--> Joined` !! skips the linger and the record flush
  - `Joined --close, no fight with a player--> Gone`
  - `Joined --close within 10 s of a hit between players--> Lingering`
  - `Lingering --join near the body--> Joined`
  - `Lingering --join from elsewhere (linger_wait, 4010)--> Lingering`
  - `Lingering --20 s up, or died and respawned--> Gone`
  - `Joined --AFK 120 s, flood, kick, freeze--> Gone`
  - `Lingering --body saved at its end, over an owner's restore--> Gone` !! the restore is overwritten

Evidence: join.js:739-746 (linger check sees no body: the old socket is still live), join.js:757-772 (old session deleted before its close, so its webSocketClose finds nothing: index.js:6358-6362, and `_lingerOnClose` at index.js:6370 never runs), join.js:876 (fresh state at the place named), wsClient.js:647 + BroTown.jsx:1064 (a fresh page names `z: 'town'`), lastplace.js:184-203 (a town join is taken as named). ## What needs further thought
- When the same character logs in from a second tab, or a relaunch, while their bro is in a fight with another player, what should happen? The same as a fresh launch today ("Your bro is still in that fight", wait until the body has gone), or should the new tab take the body over where it stands?
- In the arena, should a fighter who drops for a few seconds get a short grace when their next match starts, instead of an instant walkover?
- Before the account check (Turnstile) is switched on: a small change so a connection that closes, or sends a second join, while the check is running cannot leave a body behind.
- Making it explicit: one `session.phase` field ('open' | 'checking' | 'joined' | 'gone'), set at each step, with the join refusing unless 'open' and re-checking that its socket is still there after any non-storage wait. One `_playerLeaves(id, why)` that every exit goes through (close, AFK, flood, kick, put-out, reset, linger end and the takeover), owning the linger decision, the record flush, the duel/trade/party hooks, the caches, `player_leave` and the tick stop; the takeover calls it as a close (or keeps the live player object instead of reloading it). Per-player facts other systems need across a reconnect are derived from the room's records (the arena match from the duel's `arenaMatch` tag), not stored on the player object. Close codes in one table both sides read, with 'join failed' given its own code.
- Quick fixes vs design work: one-line or near: give 'join failed' its own close code (index.js:5160); flush `_regenDirty` (save the old state) in the takeover before the rebuild (join.js:768); make the restore also refuse while `this.playerState[pid]` exists, or end the linger first (admin.js:407); add `_achvForget` to `_lingerEnd`; let the arena's eat gates ask the duel record, or set `ps._arenaMatch` again in `_arenaOnRejoin`; re-check `this.sessions.has(ws)` after the account check's fetch. Design decisions: what a takeover during a fight should do; whether the linger should trust the phone's `dead`; a walkover grace in the arena; merging the three copies of "the player leaves" into one.
