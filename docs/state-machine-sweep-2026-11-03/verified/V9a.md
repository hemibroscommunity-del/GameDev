# V9a · Phone predictions the worker can refuse without telling it (quests and their giver, harvest swings, the guild ladder)
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Severity: medium (no gold or items are lost for good, but: an honest bow player loses harvests and is counted as bot-like; a phone shows gold, points, a handed-in quest or a felled tree the worker never agreed to; a second device shows the wrong guild rung and a button that is always refused; a modified phone takes Dungeon Delver's 300 gold without a dungeon)
- Who can trigger it: honest player: (a) fire the bow special at a monster that survives it, then start a harvest within about 2.5 s; (b) claim a guild rung, then play on another phone or switch characters and back; (c) tap Accept or Claim while the connection is dropping. Modified phone only: hand in mayor_3 without a dungeon. Deploy timing: a quest's phone check and worker objective out of step.
- Summary: The phone acts before the server answers: it marks a quest handed in and pays it, or fells a tree and adds XP, then tells the server. When the server says no, it usually says nothing, and on today's delta protocol the phone keeps its wrong picture until something else changes. Honest players reach this mainly through a bow volley's lingering arrows, which silently cancel a harvest started within 2.5 s. Separately, guild-ladder progress lives only on the device, and Dungeon Delver pays without a server check.
- Key files: server/src/quests.js:347, server/src/persistence.js:744, server/src/combat.js:1232, server/src/gathering.js:1381, server/src/guilds.js:67, src/game/quests.js:169

## The machine
- Entity, and where its state lives: one character's quests: the worker's `ps._quests[qid]` and `ps._questKills` (server/src/quests.js:147-156, :373, :442-444; restored server/src/join.js:1053), the phone's copy `S.rpg._quests` with its predicted coins, xp and achievementPoints (src/game/quests.js:25, :169-180; replaced whole from src/networking/wsClient.js:2936) and the `bt_rpg` cache (src/game/quests.js:55, :183). The giver's dialogue: `questPanel` plus QuestPanel's `stage`, `claimed` and `_claimRef` (src/ui/panels/QuestPanel.jsx:227, :268-291). One harvest attempt: the phone's `S._extraction` (src/game/lifeSkillRewards.js:239; status flipped at src/ui/BroTown.jsx:6812) plus the predicted `node.alive` and skill XP; the worker's `this.extractions[pid]` (server/src/gathering.js:1041-1058) and recovery clock `_harvestRecoverAt` (:1268-1274). The guild ladder: the phone's `rpg._guildProgress[skill]` (src/networking/gameEvents.js:1856) and the worker's `guild_claims:<pid>` (server/src/guilds.js:42-63).
- States:
  - available: the giver offers the quest (absent, or opened by the step before)
  - active: taken; the worker counts its objective
  - ready: phone-only, active and the phone's `check()` passes (src/data/gameSystems.js:8155)
  - phone-handed-in: the phone shows it paid and his next quest offered; the worker has not answered yet
  - turnedIn: the worker paid it
  - complete: declared (src/data/gameSystems.js:6590) and read, never written
  - harvest waiting: the hits land or the timer runs, then ready: the gesture cue is up
  - harvest struck: the phone has felled the node, added the XP and sent `node_strike`
  - harvest paid or refused: refused is silent, or (no-start/other-node only) the node is shown standing again
  - guild rung shown (the device's count) vs rung owed (the worker's count)
- Moves:
  - tap Accept: available → active on the phone first, then `quest_accept` (src/game/quests.js:25, :49-50); the worker does the same unless barred (server/src/quests.js:152-156)
  - tap Claim, or the auto reward: active → phone-handed-in, with gold, XP, +5 AP and the next quest opened (src/game/quests.js:169-180, :256); the worker pays after its gates (server/src/quests.js:335-347, :373, :442-451)
  - barred or no-trade refusal: the echo resends `_quests` and `coins` only (server/src/quests.js:155, :338, :357, :279-282)
  - silent refusal (dying or dead :310, no XP skill :330, not active :335, objective unmet :347; at accept :134, :152): nothing is sent, and v2 sends nothing later either (server/src/persistence.js:724-744). Missing move: phone-handed-in → active. It heals only when `_quests` or coins next change on the worker, or at a rejoin (server/src/join.js:862).
  - message dropped while the socket is down: the same phantom, until the rejoin's full snapshot (src/networking/wsClient.js:5519-5531)
  - tap a node: none → waiting, `extraction_start` (src/game/lifeSkillRewards.js:147-289); worker record (server/src/gathering.js:1015-1059)
  - hits done: waiting → ready (src/ui/BroTown.jsx:6812-6815; no death test)
  - gesture: ready → struck: `node.alive = false`, XP added, `node_strike` (src/game/lifeSkillRewards.js:529-546, :561-567, :617)
  - worker pays: record deleted, node dirtied, lifeSkills echoed (server/src/gathering.js:1406, :1442-1444)
  - worker refuses silently (dead, no tool, level, reach, too early, gone): nothing sent (server/src/gathering.js:1279-1320, :1354-1362). Missing move: the node shown standing, the XP taken back.
  - worker refuses no-start/other-node: the node is re-sent standing; the XP is not taken back (server/src/gathering.js:1381-1396)
  - bow chip `monster_damage` every 500 ms: the worker deletes the record mid-harvest (src/game/projectiles.js:1229-1241 → server/src/combat.js:1232). Wrong move: the phone is never told.
  - guild tap: the worker pays rung m and answers its index (server/src/guilds.js:60-67); the phone adopts index+1 (src/networking/gameEvents.js:1856); a refusal is a popup only (:1882-1886). Missing move: the rung at sign-in. A new device or a character switch starts at 0 (src/networking/charRoster.js:47-50, :610).

## What needs further thought
- When the server says no to something the phone already celebrated (a hand-in, a harvest), should it always say so, so the phone can undo it? Or should the phone wait for the yes before showing "Rewards claimed!" and the gold? The first keeps the game snappy; the second never shows a reward that is taken back.
- Should a bow volley's burn cancel a harvest at all? The sword lunge was changed to keep the harvest and refuse its own damage instead (server/src/abilities.js:1105-1125). The same choice is open for the arrow chips, and honest bow players are currently being counted as bot-like for it.
- Should the server tell each device the guild rungs at sign-in, so a new phone or a character switch shows the same ladder as the server pays?
- Making it explicit: give each predicted action one answer from the worker: `{ok}` or `{refused, why}`, with the authoritative fields always resent through `_resendPlayerState`. For quests that is a single `_questRefuse(session, why)` called from every `return` in `_handleQuestAccept` and `_handleQuestTurnIn`, resending `_quests`, `coins`, `achievementPoints`, `xp` and `prog3`. For strikes it is a single `_strikeRefuse(session, why, node)` that marks the node dirty (rate-limited as `_proofDirty` already is) and resends `lifeSkills`. The phone keeps one `S._extraction.phase` field, one `endExtraction(S, why)` door (death included), and one shared table of quest objectives that both `check()` and the worker read.
- Quick fixes vs design work: one-line fixes: call the echo at the four silent hand-in returns and add `achievementPoints` to it (quests.js:281); use `_resendPlayerState(..., ['lifeSkills'])` in the 'antibot-cap' branch; change the cook popup to 200; refresh the swipe layer's header comment. Small, each needing a cap and kill switch under the house rules: skip `_endExtraction` for `noKb` chips, or move it after the gates; dirty the node and resend lifeSkills on every strike refusal after the node lookup; send the guild claims with state_sync. Need a decision: give mayor_3 a worker objective that reads `lifeSkills.dungeonClears` (a new objective type, mirrored on the phone); whether the phone should wait for the worker before paying out; holding `check()` to `objective` by a test or a shared table.
