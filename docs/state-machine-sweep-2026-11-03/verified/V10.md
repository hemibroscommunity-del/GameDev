# V10 · The shield guard: your phone's copy, the server's copy, and other players' copies
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Severity: medium (an honest player's guard silently stops working for 3 s at a time while their screen still shows it raised, and the phone hides those blows' damage numbers as if they were blocked: they take full hits they think they are blocking and can lose a fight or die. The rest is small: strike and guard at once after a mid-lunge Block tap, and other players drawn with the wrong guard)
- Who can trigger it: honest player: raise the shield in a fight (tap Block, or right-click on a computer) and keep it up about 13 s, or absorb about 10 blocked hits; the server then breaks the guard for 3 s without the phone knowing, puts it back by itself, and repeats. Also honest: tap Block during a sword lunge; hold a guard while another player joins or reconnects (every phone after a worker deploy); lower a guard while a watcher is in a Wheel dungeon
- Summary: The shield exists twice. Your phone's copy is what you see: the raised arm, the lit Block button, the half-speed walk. The server's copy is what actually stops blows. When holding the shield empties your stamina, the server drops the guard for 3 seconds and never tells the phone. So the shield still looks raised while every blow lands, and the phone even hides those damage numbers. Then the server raises it again on its own. Other phones also lose track of your guard after a reconnect.
- Key files: server/src/movement.js:779-783, server/src/index.js:3749-3759, src/ui/BroTown.jsx:633, src/networking/gameEvents.js:3499-3517, src/game/shieldToggle.js:128-191, src/networking/gameEvents.js:2364-2376

## The machine
- Entity, and where its state lives: the player's guard, held in three places that are never reconciled.
  - The phone: `S._shieldUp`, `S._shieldAngle` (written only by src/game/shieldToggle.js:152-153 and 173; playerActions.js:680 is dead, never imported, BroTown.jsx:422). Also `S.shieldEnd` (BroTown.jsx:8299), the dead `S._shieldCdUntil` / `S._shieldAutoReleased` (BroTown.jsx:8302-8303), and the computer's `S._shieldKb` / `S._shieldMouse` (BroTown.jsx:10699-10712).
  - The worker: `ps.blocking`, `ps.ba` and `ps.blockStartT` (server/src/movement.js:779-783), and `ps._guardBrokenUntil` (server/src/index.js:3758, combat.js:2432, pvpextras.js:526). All of it is memory only: every join rebuilds it as down (server/src/join.js:876-880).
  - Each watching phone: `other._shieldUp` (src/networking/gameEvents.js:2373), set only by `player_shield` edges.
  - The link: the phone's flag rides every move packet as `blocking` / `ba` (src/networking/wsClient.js:6031-6045). The worker's flag goes to no phone, only to admin (server/src/admin.js:325), the monster AI rows (index.js:1947) and the lag-compensation history (tick.js:270).
- States:
  - Down: no guard on the phone and on the worker.
  - Up: the phone draws the arm and cone, walks at half speed (BroTown.jsx:5438) and sends `blocking: true`; the worker blocks blows inside ±60° of `ba` (index.js:2877-2885).
  - Parry window (worker only): the first 250 ms after the worker saw the guard go up (`blockStartT`, index.js:2904-2909).
  - Broken (worker only): 3 s (`GUARD_BREAK_MS`, index.js:1502); the worker refuses the phone's `blocking: true` (movement.js:780-781).
  - Cooling (phone only, unreachable): a 2 s grey Block button with a blue arc (ShieldButton.jsx:45, 849, 877); its only writer sits behind a client flag that is off.
  - Peer up / peer down: another phone's drawing of your guard, changed only by your `player_shield` edges.
- Moves:
  - tap Block (ShieldButton.jsx:857-860) or right-click (BroTown.jsx:10699-10712): Down → Up (shieldToggle.js:128-164); tap or right-click again: Up → Down (shieldToggle.js:187-191)
  - attack or special: Up → Down (playerActions.js:59, 120; monsterCombat.js:1437); roll, jump, swim, climb: Up → Down (dodge.js:88; jumpActions.js:73; BroTown.jsx:5291, 5575); death, duel end: Up → Down (wsClient.js:3156; gameEvents.js:4778). A daze, a zone change and a reconnect lower nothing.
  - every move packet: the phone's Up/Down becomes the worker's `blocking`; a down-to-up change stamps `blockStartT` (movement.js:779-783)
  - worker, stamina reaches 0 while holding (the regen tick, every ~660 ms: 5 a tick, no regen) or on a blocked player hit: Up → Broken (index.js:3749-3759; combat.js:2422-2433; pvpextras.js:520-527). A blocked monster hit only drains 10 (hits.js:331-341), and the next regen tick does the break.
  - worker, 3 s over and the next move still says `blocking: true`: Broken → Up with a FRESH parry window, no press (movement.js:779-783)
  - MISSING: the worker's Broken never reaches the phone, so the phone stays Up
  - DEAD: phone stamina 0 → Cooling (BroTown.jsx:633 `BLOCK_COSTS_STAMINA = false`, 8300-8306)
  - WRONG: the worker lands a blow, the phone is Up and the attacker is in its arc → the phone draws it as a block: no number, -15 stamina locally (gameEvents.js:3499-3517)
  - WRONG: a lunge in flight, then Block → Up, and the lunge still strikes on arrival (abilities.js:606-616; BroTown.jsx:5141-5160; worker has no gate, server/src/abilities.js:157-161)
  - reconnect: the worker's copy → Down, a break cancelled (join.js:876-880); the phone keeps Up and re-raises on its first move
  - peer: `player_shield {up}` from a sender in my zone → peer Up/Down (gameEvents.js:2364-2376); from a sender in another zone → dropped (gameEvents.js:254-258, 2366); my rejoin or theirs → peer rebuilt Down (wsClient.js:238-278, 1776-1794, 3740)

## What needs further thought
- When holding the shield runs your stamina out, what should you see? Today the server drops the guard for 3 seconds and then raises it again by itself, while your screen shows it raised the whole time. Should the shield visibly drop (the Block button greys out with a 3 s countdown) and stay down until you tap again? Or should it come back by itself, but with the 3 s shown?
- If you tap Block while a sword lunge is in the air, should the lunge still hit (as now: you get the hit and the guard together, which an earlier owner decision said must never happen), or should Block cancel the lunge's hit, or wait until the lunge lands?
- Should every player always see who is guarding (one bit per player on each update, a small cost)? Or is it fine that someone who just arrived, or came back from a dungeon, sees your guard wrong until you next toggle it?
- Making it explicit: give the worker one guard field, `ps.guard` = down | up | broken (with its end time and `blockStartT`), changed only by one function. The move handler, the regen tick and the three block sites call it, and it opens the parry window only on a real down-to-up press, never on the way back from broken. The worker tells the phone about the break: a short "guard broken, N ms left" field on `player_state`, behind a new cap and a lower-case kill switch. The phone's shieldToggle.js keeps the same three phases. The button, the arm, the walk speed and the move packet all read them, and the phone sends the `player_shield {up:false}` edge when a break lands so watchers agree. Watchers get the guard as one bit on the tick (both tick builders) and apply edges whatever their zone. The phone's own re-judging of landed blows is deleted for server zones.
- Quick fixes vs design work:
  - Quick, one or two lines each:
    - skip the fallback block in a server zone (an `!S._serverMonsters` test at gameEvents.js:3499, as v2.3.3300 did for the roll) (N2-14);
    - let `_peerInZone` gate only the sound in the `player_shield` case (R2-14, first concern);
    - drop the lunge's stashed strike when the guard goes up, or skip the strike in `_endDash` while `_shieldUp` (G2-2);
    - fix SHIELD_CD_MS to 3000 and the three stale comments (P3-3, G2-4).
  - Design, the owner's call:
    - telling the phone about the break, what the player then sees, and whether the guard comes back by itself (a new field, cap and kill switch; G2-4, P3-3);
    - repeating the guard to watchers on the tick (R2-14, second concern);
    - the parry window that re-opens after every break (decide together with the parry-window verifier; G2-4).
