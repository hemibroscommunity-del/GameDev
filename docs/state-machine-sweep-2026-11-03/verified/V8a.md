# V8a · Escrowed sales: a General Store order, an Auction House listing, an offer on it, the credit that pays each, and the phone's place and sell taps
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Severity: high (cheating: a modified page can file a cheap weapon under a dearer type or tier and take the gold of other players' resting buy orders, a known open hole; honest players: a double tap, or an old pick still lit on the General Store's sell button, lists a weapon they did not choose under another weapon's name, and may sell it on the spot; a restart at the wrong moment loses gold or goods in four places; an ignored offer's gold is held up to a week, not 48 h)
- Who can trigger it: honest player (double-tap or double-click "List for Sale"; tap it again after a refusal, a "Server error!" or a close and reopen with the old weapon still lit; on a slow or dropped connection, list from an item card, then close it or switch cards; make an offer on a shelf of more than 20 listings and get no answer) | deploy/restart timing (a General Store order that meets a resting one; an Auction House listing of a stack; an offer withdrawn, declined, expired or raised; any leftover healed only after 48 h with nobody opening the shop) | modified phone (a sell order's type and tier)
- Summary: When you sell or bid, the server holds your gold or goods in a saved record until the deal ends. Most steps heal after a restart, but four save in two halves, and a restart between them loses someone's gold or goods. Offers promise to return gold after 48 hours, yet only the first 20 listings are checked. The General Store's sell button has no 'busy' lock and remembers your weapon by its place in the list, so a double tap can sell a different weapon under the wrong name.
- Key files: server/src/market.js:327, server/src/market.js:101, src/ui/panels/buildings/ExchangePanel.jsx:618, server/src/store.js:1063, server/src/storeoffer.js:201, server/src/store.js:673

## The machine
- Entity, and where its state lives: three escrow records on the worker (GameRoom storage), each holding someone's gold or goods, plus the credit that pays them out.
  - An ORDER `mkt_order:<id>` (market.js:25, 408), with `expires` (341) and in memory `_mktIndex` / `_mktOrderCounts` (89-91) and `_mktLastSweep` (475).
  - A LISTING `store_listing:<id>` (store.js:789), with its markers `sale` (829, 917), `pendBid` (873), `releasing` (1003), `pendOffer` (storeoffer.js:168), plus `topBid`, `offers` and `expiresAt`; in memory `_stIndex` / `_stCounts` (store.js:429-431).
  - An OFFER, an entry `rec.offers[buyerId]` (storeoffer.js:186-191).
  - A CREDIT: `oplog:<opId>` stamps, pruned after 48 h (inbox.js:37, 189), and the mailbox `inbox:<pid>` (inbox.js:520), with `_opInFlight` in memory (246-253).
  - On the phone: BroTown state `mktSellItem` (a stash INDEX) and the kind fields (BroTown.jsx:1828-1866), `S._mktLastRefresh` (ExchangePanel.jsx:808), and the item card's shared `sellInFlight` ref and `sellBusy` (ItemDetailPopup.jsx:774, 783).
- States:
  - order escrowing: a crossing taker's gold or weapon, saved but named by no record or stamp
  - order resting: on disk and in the book; cancellable; expires after 24 h
  - order matching: out of the book, the credits paid one await at a time
  - order refunding: a cancel or an expiry, refund credit in flight
  - listing escrowing (stack): goods taken in one write, record not yet written
  - listing resting / bidding (`pendBid`) / bid standing (`topBid`)
  - listing buying (`sale`, `paid: false`) / accepted (`sale`, `paid: true`): settling, hidden from browse
  - listing releasing (`releasing`): a cancel or 7-day expiry handing everything back
  - offer pending (`pendOffer`, before the debit) / live (reply none or 'think') / refunding (gone from the record, credit in flight)
  - credit in flight / delivered (save and stamp in one run) / parked (mailbox and stamp; drained only at the next join) / stamp pruned after 48 h
  - crash leftover: a record with some stamps; healed only by the first shop request after a wake
  - phone pick: none / index i, kept across a refusal, a lost answer, a tab switch and a close
  - item card sending: one guard shared by every card; the outcome is listed, refused, or "no answer" (shown as a refusal)
- Moves:
  - Place, no crossing: escrow, save and record in one run: escrowing → resting (market.js:322-327, 408)
  - Place, crossing: taker escrow saved (market.js:327) → maker out of the book (358) → item leg (379) → gold leg (380) → price-improvement leg (382) → delete (384). Wrong: four or more writes.
  - Cancel: out of the book → refund → delete (market.js:443-451); expiry: at most once a minute on any market call (473-489)
  - Wake, order: ANY of settle:item / settle:gold / refund stamped → delete, else re-list (market.js:101-106). Missing: "pay the missing leg".
  - List a stack: take plus `:esc` stamp, awaited (store.js:673; inbox.js:684-701) → record put (store.js:789). Wrong: two writes. A weapon, gear or pet is taken and recorded in one run (store.js:691-692; storegear.js:448-473).
  - Buy now: `sale` put (store.js:829-830) → pay `:pay` (832) → settle: goods, gold, bids, offers (841; 936-967) → delete (848). `paid` is never written true.
  - Bid: `pendBid` put (873) → debit (875) → refund the outbid, put (881, 888-904); accept: `sale(paid)` → settle → delete (917-927)
  - Cancel or expire: `releasing` put (1003) → bid refund → offers → goods home (1018-1022) → out of the book (1023) → delete (1029)
  - Wake, listing: finish `releasing`; finish `sale` if paid or `:pay` is stamped, else re-list; promote `pendBid` / `pendOffer` if stamped, else drop (store.js:466-516)
  - Offer: (raise: refund 157 → put 163) → `pendOffer` put (169) → debit (171) → promote (177)
  - Offer withdrawn, declined or expired: record put (storeoffer.js:203) THEN credit (204-207). Wrong order.
  - Offer Yes: `sale(paid, offerSeq)` → settle → delete (storeoffer.js:256-264)
  - Offer expiry pass: only the first 20 listings in the book (store.js:1061-1066). Missing: the rest.
  - Credit: in flight → 'dup' (inbox.js:247); stamped → 'dup' (300); fits → live + save + stamp (321-328); else mailbox + stamp, no word to the player (331-335); drained only at the join (join.js:1533)
  - Prune: stamps older than 48 h, hourly while anyone is online (inbox.js:189; tick.js:416-418)
  - Phone: chip tap sets the pick and kind (ExchangePanel.jsx:587-594) → Place posts with no guard (618-688) → "ok" clears the pick (731); a refusal (699-700) or a lost answer (761-764) keeps it
  - Item card: "Put it up" (ItemDetailPopup.jsx:1552-1554); a card change clears the guard (798-806); the answer acts on whichever card is open (1555-1583); no timeout (storeApi.js:130-140)
- Diagram edges:
  - `Placing --no crossing order: escrow and record in one write--> RestingOrder`
  - `Placing --crossing: taker escrow saved, then credits one by one !!--> Matched (a restart between loses the taker's escrow)`
  - `MatchedLeftover --wake with ANY settle stamp !!--> Deleted (the unpaid leg is never paid)`
  - `RestingOrder --cancel or 24 h expiry: refund, then delete--> Gone`
  - `StackEscrowing --record put, a second write !!--> RestingListing`
  - `RestingListing --buy or accepted bid: sale marker, pay, settle--> Sold`
  - `RestingListing --cancel or 7-day expiry: releasing marker--> Released`
  - `PendingOffer --debit stamped, promoted--> LiveOffer`
  - `LiveOffer --withdraw / no / expiry: record first, credit second !!--> Refunded (a restart between loses the gold)`
  - `LiveOffer --48 h on a listing past the first 20 !!--> still held (up to 7 days)`
  - `LiveOffer --raised: refund, then record !!--> UnfundedOffer (a seller's Yes still pays it)`
  - `CrashLeftover --first heal after the 48 h prune !!--> heals the wrong way (re-list, or pay twice)`
  - `PhonePick(i) --double tap, or pick kept after a refusal or close !!--> the weapon now at i escrowed under the old name`
  - `ItemCardSending --card closed or switched !!--> guard cleared; the late answer acts on the next card`

## Concerns, verified
- [CONFIRMED] S6-3 (crossing, before the first credit): a General Store order that meets a resting one saves the taker's escrow, then pays each leg with its own await. A restart before the item leg loses the taker's gold or weapon, since no record or stamp names it, and the maker's order goes back on sale. Evidence: server/src/market.js:322-327, 358, 379-386. How checked: traced, then ran the real `_mktPlaceOrder` on a fake storage (SM/round2/v8awork/order.mjs: `SAVE rpg:buyer coins=380`, three awaited credits, then the delete). `_saveRpg` issues its put at once (persistence.js:229-262), and `_creditPlayerOnce` awaits a read before it writes (inbox.js:300). Who: deploy/restart timing. Docs: handoff rule 7 ("ONE synchronous run", ARCHITECTURE-HANDOFF.md:177) and TRAPS §184 state exactly this rule; v2.3.3294 fixed the trades but not this path. marketplace.md:31-41 covers only the delete-versus-credit order, and market.test.mjs:256-266 simulates only a lost delete.
- [CONFIRMED] S6-3 (between legs): a restart after the item leg and before the gold leg leaves an order the wake deletes on ANY stamp, so the seller is never paid. A taker's price-improvement refund is lost the same way, including after the gold leg. Evidence: server/src/market.js:101-106, 379-382. How checked: ran the real `_mktEnsureIndex` with only `settle:<id>:item` stamped. It deletes the record, and nothing retries the gold leg (order.mjs (d)). Who: deploy/restart timing. Docs: the v2.3.1184 comment (market.js:95-100) assumes every credit had landed.
- [PLAUSIBLE] S6-4 (with S6-3 and S6-5, late heal): the "paid" facts live only in 48-hour stamps, pruned hourly while anyone is online (inbox.js:37, 189; tick.js:416-418). The order book and the shelf heal a leftover only on the first shop request after a wake (market.js:195; store.js:541; storeoffer.js:138; storechat.js:176). A leftover healed after the prune can re-list a paid sale, or pay a release, a bid refund or an offer refund twice. A buy-now never even writes `paid: true` after its debit (store.js:829-841). Unproven link: 48 h with nobody opening the shop after such a restart. That is likeliest for the General Store, whose window is the only caller of /api/market. Who: deploy/restart timing plus a quiet shop. Docs: TRAPS §146 ("the oplog ... is not the record").
- [PLAUSIBLE] S6-3 / S6-5 (a partial book): both rebuilds set their in-memory book before the awaited list (market.js:89-92; store.js:429-431). A throw mid-rebuild leaves a partial book for the worker's life: orders invisible, never matched or expired, and the 10-order cap blind. Unproven link: a storage call throwing during a rebuild. Nothing is lost, since the records stay on disk for the next wake. Who: storage fault. Docs: none found.
- [CONFIRMED] S6-3 (soft expiry): the matcher never reads `expires`, and the sweep runs at most once a minute, so an order up to a minute past its 24 h can still fill. Evidence: market.js:350-354, 475. How checked: read. Harmless: the maker still holds the escrow and did want the trade. Docs: none.
- [CONFIRMED] S6-4 (parked while online): a weapon or gear credit to an online player whose bag is full goes to the mailbox with no message (inbox.js:331-335; `_gearCreditMail` only marks the ledger), and only the join drains it (join.js:1533 is the sole caller). A General Store buyer sees "Bought for ...G!" and no weapon until they free a slot and log in again. How checked: grep of every `_drainInbox` caller. Who: honest player with a full weapon bag. Docs: by design (rule 3; inbox.js:563-566).
- [REFUTED] S6-4 (an in-flight 'dup', then the first call throws): two credits with one opId do not overlap on any live path. Rule 9 keeps storage-bound events apart, every escrow caller awaits its credit, and the only un-awaited credits use unique ids (dailyrewards.js:833 `dq:<pid>:<day>:<i>`; nomansland.js:699 `nmlloot:<victim>:<now>:<seq>`). What remains needs a storage throw on top.

## What needs further thought
- When a sale is half done at a restart, should it always be finished? The Auction House writes a "sale in progress" note first and finishes the sale on the next start. The General Store writes no such note, and deletes the order if any part was paid. Copying the Auction House's note to the General Store is a contained change that alters nothing a player sees.
- Should the General Store name the weapon you sell by its id (as the Auction House names armour) and work out its type and tier itself, instead of believing the phone? That closes both the double-tap mislabel and the known cheat. Old weapons with odd tier names (the woodworking `ww_` tiers, old drop rarities) need a rule first.
- Should "paid" be written into each listing and order themselves, not only into the 48-hour journal (TRAPS §146), so a leftover found late still heals the right way? And is 48 hours still the promise for an offer's gold?
- Making it explicit: give each record one `phase` field:
  - an order: 'resting' | 'matching' | 'refunding';
  - a listing: 'resting' | 'bidding' | 'buying' | 'accepted' | 'releasing';
  - an offer: 'pending' | 'live' | 'refunding'.
  Write each next phase in the same synchronous run as the money it stands for: the maker's 'matching' with the taker's escrow, a stack's take with its listing, an offer's removal with its refund. For the last, use `_creditLiveTogether` when the buyer is online, otherwise write a 'refunding' phase first. Record each paid leg in the record too. Then one `may(rec, verb)` guard per kind replaces the scattered `sale || pendBid` checks, and one `converge(rec)` at wake finishes whatever phase it finds, every leg deduped by opId. On the phone, each window keeps one pending placement carrying an opId the worker stamps, with a timeout that ends in "not sure: check My orders". The pick is cleared on every answer and on open, and a late answer only touches the card it was sent from.
- Quick fixes vs design work:
  - One-line or few-line fixes: S6-6's expiry pass (test for offers before counting, or keep a cursor); S6-6's refund (credit, then write, as the raise path does); P1-7's double tap (an in-flight ref on Place, as the item card has); P1-7's old pick (clear `mktSellItem` on every answer and on open); M2-3 (an AbortController timeout in `post()`, and check the card's target before a late answer acts).
  - Small, contained changes: S6-5's stack listing (take the stack in the same run as the record, as the weapon branch does); S6-6's raise (refund and record in one write, or a marker first).
  - Needs a decision: S6-3 (a match note on the maker's order and a wake that pays the missing legs, as store.js's `rec.sale` does); P1-7's kind (a type and tier the worker works out; old tier names need a rule); S6-4 (paid facts in the records, TRAPS §146); M2-3's lost gear answer (the phone reading the worker's gear stash, the open item in gear-stash.md).
