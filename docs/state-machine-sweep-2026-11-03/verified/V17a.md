# V17a · The game's drawing engine: made, lost, rebuilt, and what each rebuild must bring back
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Severity: medium (no items or gold at stake. Unlucky timing during a black-screen recovery on an iPhone can leave a whole second drawing engine alive and never freed, the memory leak of which TRAPS §139 says "every black screen made the next one likelier", or keep the screen black for several seconds. A rebuild inside the world leaves every building without smoke, lamps, sparks or a waving flag for the visit. One failed download at login hides every health bar over heads until a reload.)
- Who can trigger it: honest player. (1) Any black-screen rebuild in the Wheel loses the building life, with no special steps. (2) One failed request for the bar picture at login. (3) The two-engine race needs timing: a rebuild whose new engine takes more than 5 s to start (pictures slow to come back), then a tab switch and return, or the watchdog's second dark strike, or a failed start's retry timer, before it starts. `?pixidelay` reproduces it on demand.
- Summary: The renderer is the engine that draws the game. It is made in the background, can die when an iPhone runs short of memory, and is rebuilt on a fresh canvas. Nothing tells an engine still being made that a newer one replaced it, so with bad timing two get made and one is never freed, or the wrong one draws. A rebuild also forgets the buildings' animations. A health-bar picture that fails to load once is never asked for again. The loading screen's report can only say "loaded".
- Key files: src/ui/BroTown.jsx:4001-4008, src/ui/BroTown.jsx:8401-8421, src/rendering/pixiApp.js:724-749, src/rendering/wheelObjects.js:387-416, src/rendering/systems/entityRenderer.js:188-217, src/rendering/preloadAnimations.js:519-541

## The machine
- Entity, and where its state lives: the page's one Pixi renderer and its canvas. Its state is spread across these places:
  - the React canvas epoch `glEpoch` (src/ui/BroTown.jsx:1574-1577), which keys the canvas element (:12768-12773);
  - `pixiRef.current`, `window._pixiRenderer` and `window.__pixiActive` (:4003-4008, :4105, :8415-8420);
  - the module's `_appRef` (src/rendering/pixiRenderer.js:207, :315), plus about a dozen module handles set at birth (:324-376);
  - window globals: `__btLastGlRebuild` (BroTown.jsx:4200, :4204), `__btGlLostAt` (src/debug/crashTrap.js:309; reset at BroTown.jsx:4138), `__btPixiInitFails` (:4024, :4037, :4114), `__btGlRebuildOwed` (:4163-4164), `__btBlackRetry` (:4091, :4096) and `__btCanvasFallback` (:4021);
  - the watchdog's strike count `S.__wdDark` (:8889).
  
  The art a rebuild must bring back lives in wheelLife's `_tex` (src/rendering/wheelLife.js:130), the bar art's `_hudBarLoadStarted` and `_hudBarTex` (entityRenderer.js:187-188) and the witnesses `_w` (src/rendering/canvasWitness.js:71). The loading-screen gate is the promise `introWaitRef.current` (BroTown.jsx:12223, :12323), with `__btPreloadSteps` (pixiRenderer.js:102-104) and `__btPreloadReport` (preloadAnimations.js:541).
- States:
  - none: no renderer (page start, or right after a cleanup)
  - making: `initPixiRenderer` pending; it waits for the pictures to come back (up to 8 s, picsOnce.js:209-215) and then for `app.init` (pixiApp.js:724, :729)
  - live: assigned to `pixiRef` and drawing
  - failed: the start was refused; it is retried up to 8 times on a fresh canvas, with a wait of 1 to 15 s
  - lost: the graphics context died (crashTrap stamps `__btGlLostAt`)
  - rebuild window: the 5 s after a rebuild; a second call inside it owes one rebuild, and only if the canvas was lost again
  - purged: 2 or more witnessed pictures read back empty, so the page reloads instead of rebuilding
  - superseded (never declared): an engine still being made whose canvas is no longer the page's. This is the missing state.
  - per rebuild in the Wheel: the building life is made or freed
  - bar art: unasked, loading, loaded, or failed (null for the session)
  - gate: loading wave 1, then wave 2, then baked, uploaded and done; the intro lifts at the latest at 20 s
- Moves:
  - the effect runs with no renderer: none → making (BroTown.jsx:4001-4002)
  - the start resolves: making → live, assigned without any check (:4002-4008)
  - the start is refused: making → failed, and the epoch is bumped after a backoff, at most 8 times (:4103-4124). The timer is never cancelled.
  - WebGL fell back to canvas, or the context has no stencil: live → bump (:4020-4036, :4049-4057)
  - the context is lost: live → lost, then `_rebuildRenderer` 2.5 s later (crashTrap.js:309-314)
  - `_rebuildRenderer` outside the 5 s window: if 2 or more witnesses read empty, the recovery reload (BroTown.jsx:4192-4201); otherwise stamp and bump (:4204-4214)
  - `_rebuildRenderer` inside the window: owe one rebuild if lost since (:4145-4171)
  - the resume probe: `__pixiActive === false` → `_rebuildRenderer` (src/networking/wsClient.js:5071-5072). This is also true during any start that follows a cleanup.
  - the watchdog: the second dark strike → `_rebuildRenderer`; the fourth → a recovery reload (BroTown.jsx:8897-8918)
  - a bump while live: the cleanup destroys the assigned renderer (:8415-8420), then a new effect run → making on a new canvas
  - a bump while making: the cleanup finds nothing to destroy, and the old start lands later and takes `pixiRef` and the module handles (MISSING supersession)
  - a rebuild in the Wheel: the life textures are freed (pixiRenderer.js:926 → wheelObjects.js:802 → :410). Only the way in makes them (:392, called only from src/game/worldTrial.js:429), and the rebuild keeps the trip entered (src/rendering/systems/tileRenderer.js:2024), so they are never made again.
  - bar art: one load on first ask (entityRenderer.js:190-217); a failure means null for the session
  - gate: wave 1 → wave 2 → report (preloadAnimations.js:519-541) → bake → uploads → programs (pixiRenderer.js:138-181)
- Diagram edges:
  - `None --effect run--> Making`
  - `Making --start resolves--> Live`
  - `Making --start refused--> Failed`
  - `Failed --backoff timer (8 tries)--> Making`
  - `Live --context lost / watchdog / resume probe--> RebuildAsked`
  - `RebuildAsked --inside 5 s--> OwedRebuild`
  - `OwedRebuild --window ends, lost again--> RebuildAsked`
  - `RebuildAsked --2+ pictures emptied--> RecoveryReload`
  - `RebuildAsked --epoch bump, cleanup destroys--> None`
  - `Making --epoch bump--> Superseded !! (nothing destroys it)`
  - `Superseded --lands later--> Live !! (takes pixiRef if last; the other is never freed)`
  - `Live(Wheel) --rebuild--> LiveWithoutBuildingLife !!`
  - `BarArtLoading --one failed request--> BarArtMissing !! (never asked again)`
  - `GateLoading --a group fails--> Done ("fulfilled") !!`

## Concerns, verified
- [CONFIRMED] U1-3, R5-1 scenario A (race): an engine still being made is never told it was replaced. If the epoch is bumped while a start is pending, the cleanup destroys nothing. Both starts then land and assign, the last one wins `pixiRef`, and the other is never destroyed. Evidence:
  - src/ui/BroTown.jsx:4002-4008 assigns with no `canvas === canvasRef.current` check; the only such check is in the 5 s probe (:4073).
  - BroTown.jsx:8415-8420 destroys only an assigned renderer.
  - The start waits up to 8 s plus `app.init` (pixiApp.js:724, :729; picsOnce.js:209-215).
  - Callers that bump during a pending start: the resume probe on `__pixiActive === false` (wsClient.js:5071-5072; false since the previous cleanup, BroTown.jsx:8419) once more than 5 s have passed; the watchdog's single strike-2 call (:8898); and the uncancelled backoff timers (:4031-4033, :4121-4123).
  - Everything after `app.init` is synchronous: createPixiApp's tail and buildScene (pixiApp.js:735-749, :188), then initPixiRenderer's handles (pixiRenderer.js:315-376). So a start that lands last has already re-pointed `_appRef`, the prewarm, palette, water and crumble handles and picsOnce's `_renderer` before any caller could refuse it.
  - The orphan stays subscribed to look changes (src/rendering/systems/effectsRenderer.js:4462-4476) and stays in `_liveTileRenderers` (tileRenderer.js:216, :290).
  
  How checked: read every bump site and the cleanup, and the awaits inside the start. No StrictMode (src/main.jsx:58). No suite or phone test covers a pending start. Docs: none on a replaced start. TRAPS §139 (docs/TRAPS.md:5468-5487) describes exactly this leak for a renderer that is not destroyed.
- [REFUTED] R5-1 scenario B (race): "backToMenu during a pending start". backToMenu is reached only from the character creator (BroTown.jsx:2294-2303, :12566), where the renderer effect does not run (:3563). No in-game code sets bootPhase (grep: no writers outside BroTown.jsx's boot and door code). The only in-game road is joinTown throwing (:12037), a rare error path on which the join itself has already failed (the auto-join does not run twice, :12014-12015). How checked: grep of every `setBootPhase`/`backToMenu` writer. Docs: v2.3.1869 note (:3529-3561).
- [CONFIRMED] R11-23 (stuck): after a black-screen rebuild in the Wheel, every building is drawn without its life for the rest of the visit. Evidence:
  - src/rendering/pixiRenderer.js:926 → wheelObjects.js:802 → wheelObjectsFree :410 → wheelLife.js:187-191.
  - The new WheelObjects (pixiRenderer.js:517) draws life only if `wheelLifeReady()` (wheelObjects.js:544).
  - The only maker is wheelObjectsWarm (:392), called only from worldTrial.js:429 on the way in. The rebuild keeps the trip entered (tileRenderer.js:2024; wheelGround.js:474-490), so the way in never runs again.
  - The objects themselves come back, because their index reloads lazily (wheelObjects.js:108-112).
  - landAuras is not affected: it is freed only when the worker stops (src/rendering/landAuras.js:255).
  
  How checked: grep of every caller of `wheelLifeWarm`, `wheelObjectsWarm` and `wheelLifeFree`. QA misses it: mp-palette rebuilds with `?nolife` (tools/qa/mp/mp-palette.mjs:264-265, :388-390), and mp-wheellife and mp-flagwave never rebuild. Docs: wheelLife.js:27-32 says "made once a visit".
- [CONFIRMED] R1-3 (stuck): one failed request for the bar art is never asked for again, so the bars stay missing for the session. Evidence:
  - The latch is entityRenderer.js:188-191, and each of the six loads is `.catch(() => {})` (:192-217).
  - With `barFrame` null: the node bars hide (:877), the peer bars return (:11946), the monster bar is never put in (:10931-10932), and your own bar's alpha is 0 (:18475-18479).
  
  How checked: read every reader of `_hudBarTex`; nothing resets the latch. node_modules is absent, so Pixi's own retry was not checked, but the latch blocks any retry. Docs: TRAPS §253 and docs/specs/retry-looks.md ("never given up for the session"). Its "Not yet asking again" list (retry-looks.md:56-58) names only `_loadPopupIcon`; this loader is unlisted.
- [CONFIRMED] R5-6 (guard-drift): `__btPreloadReport` can never say "rejected". Evidence:

  - Checked with a node script in the same shape (round2/v17awork/report-shape.mjs): a rejecting group reads "fulfilled".

## What needs further thought
- Questions for the owner:
  - When the game rebuilds its graphics after a black screen, should an engine still being made be cancelled and thrown away the moment a newer one starts? Today it is not, and nothing tests that case.
  - Should a rebuild bring back everything the way into the world made: the buildings' smoke, lamps and flag? Today only a trip out and back does.
  - Should the "ask again, never give up for the session" rule the looks follow cover the health-bar art and the loading report too, so a failed download shows up and heals?
- Making it explicit: one small renderer-life module holds `phase` (none, making, live, failed, rebuilding, reloading) and a generation number.
  - `begin(canvas)` hands the start a token. createPixiApp checks the token right after `app.init`, before its synchronous tail and before initPixiRenderer's handles. A start whose token is stale destroys its app and returns nothing.
  - Every door (crashTrap, the resume probe, the watchdog, the 5 s screen probe, the backoff timers) calls one `requestRebuild(reason)`. It owns the 5 s window, the owed rebuild and the witness check.
  - The cleanup cancels that run's timers.
  - An `onRendererBuilt` hook lets systems put their art back (wheelLife, the baked-body upload).
- Quick fixes vs design work:
  - Near one-liners:
    - WheelObjects calls `wheelLifeWarm()` when `this.life && !wheelLifeReady()`. It is synchronous and "safe to call again" (wheelLife.js:137-139).
    - preloadAnimations records the true status: catch inside the record, not before `allSettled`.
    - The bar loader resets its latch on a failure, with a `retryAt` as `_ensureTraitLoaded` has.
  - Small but placement matters: the supersession guard has to sit inside createPixiApp or initPixiRenderer. BroTown's `.then` is too late, because a stale start that lands last has already re-pointed the module handles.
  - Needs a decision and a phone measure: whether the eight kept canvases go through keepAsImage or get witnessed, and whether the witness set needs widening after v2.3.3702. This is the spec's own open question: does Safari empty small canvases?
