# V1 · What a phone says about itself on every step, and the protections the server builds on it
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Severity: high (cheating: a tampered page can make itself untouchable by monsters and by other players while it still attacks, and so take other players' bags and gold in No man's land and wagered duels; honest players only meet the low ones: a second death after closing the game mid-death, a shield tapped twice parrying)
- Who can trigger it: modified phone only for every high concern (the game is a web page and these are plain JSON fields, so "modified" needs only the browser's developer tools or a socket script like the bot lab's); honest player for the low ones (die, then swipe the game away or lose signal within 5 s; tap the shield off and on; equip a spare in a losing fight)
- Summary: On every step a player's phone tells the server things about itself: 'I'm dead', 'my shield just went up', 'I'm cooking', which area it is in, and at login which size of world it draws. The server turns each into protection (monsters and players leave that player alone; a fresh shield parries) without checking it, so a tampered page can become untouchable while still attacking and take others' loot in No man's land. A forged skill level is a free full heal; closing the game mid-death brings a second death.
- Key files: server/src/movement.js:741-796, server/src/index.js:1940-1948, server/src/hits.js:244-252, server/src/movement.js:815-834, server/src/wheeltables.js:49-53, server/src/grids.js:823-849

## The machine
- Entity, and where its state lives: one player's `playerState[id]` on the worker (GameRoom). Written by the PHONE on every accepted `move` (movement.js:704-797): `ps.dead` (movement.js:796), `ps.blocking` + `ps.blockStartT` (movement.js:779-783), the shield's facing `ps.ba` (movement.js:793-795), the harvest code `ps.ex` + `ps._exAt/_exX/_exY` (movement.js:741-752), `ps.z` and with it `ps._zoneEntryGraceUntil` (movement.js:721, 815-834); at the join `ps._wheelSizes` from the phone's `ws` (join.js:848, 886); on `stats_update` the legacy tracks `ps.weaponSkills` / `ps.defenseSkill` (grids.js:823-842). Written by the WORKER beside them: `ps.dying` / `ps.respawnAt` (index.js:3388-3391, 3535-3537), `ps._guardBrokenUntil` (index.js:3758; combat.js:2432), `ps._respawnOwed` (index.js:3596, 3606), the fight clock (`_pvpHealClocks`, farmorders.js:195-199). Of all this only the skill levels and the hp 0 written at a death (index.js:3491) are saved; the rest is memory, lost on a reconnect or a deploy.
- States:
  - alive: the worker has the player up and the phone says `dead: false`.
  - claims-dead: the worker has the player alive but the phone says `dead: true`; no monster, player or No man's land rule reaches it (illegal, reachable).
  - dying: the worker's 5 s death (dying + dead + respawnAt); the phone's own move may still say alive (TRAPS §233).
  - respawn-owed: the respawn ran with no socket to hear it; a flag nothing ever reads back.
  - shield down / raised (parry open 250 ms, index.js:1291) / held (5 stamina a regen tick) / broken (3 s worker latch, index.js:1502).
  - harvesting at a node (record + `ex`) / cooking or lighting a fire (`ex` cook|fire, shielded 120 s within 120 px of the stamp, index.js:1415-1420).
  - zone: a hub, a combat zone, the Wheel, or any `dungeon:<1-32 chars>` id, live or not (data.js:423); entry-graced 500 ms (index.js:1478).
  - in step / out of step with the room's Wheel size, fixed at the join for the whole session (wheeltables.js:49-53).
  - legacy skill track level 0-100 per weapon kind and defence, only ever rising; each rise fills hp, stamina and mana (grids.js:844-849).
  - in a fight with a player (a duel, or a hit between players in the last 10 s): the bag's ways out are locked; forge, hardening and weapon equip are not.
- Moves:
  - move with `dead`: any -> claims-dead or alive, with no look at the worker's own view (movement.js:796)
  - a blow takes hp to 0: alive -> dying (index.js:3383-3391); the tick finds hp 0 and not dying: -> dying, cause 'stuck-at-zero' (index.js:3527-3529)
  - 5 s pass: dying -> alive in town, full pools (index.js:3530-3541); with no socket: -> respawn-owed (index.js:3596, 3606), then lost with the object (linger.js:134; index.js:6397; join.js:876)
  - the socket closes inside the 5 s: the dying phase is gone; the next join restores hp 0 with dying false (join.js:1001, 1337-1338) and the sweep kills again (index.js:3527)
  - move `blocking` false->true, guard not broken: down -> raised, stamp = now (movement.js:779-782); `blocking: false`: -> down, stamp 0 (movement.js:783); 250 ms: raised -> held (index.js:2908)
  - stamina 0: held -> broken, in the hold drain (index.js:3753-3759) and on a player's blocked hit (combat.js:2427-2433); MISSING on a monster's blocked hit, which clamps at 0 and leaves the shield up (hits.js:333-337)
  - move `ex` null -> cook|fire: stamp the time and place (movement.js:744-748); `ex: null`: stamp 0 (movement.js:749-751); any attack: stamp 0 and record deleted (gathering.js:974-981; combat.js:1232, 2225; abilities.js:620; arrowblast.js:164; burst.js:182)
  - move naming another valid zone: zone changed, 500 ms of zero damage for any non-hub zone, a zone snapshot sent (movement.js:815-881); a dungeon id needs only its shape (movement.js:381-385, 408-411)
  - join: in or out of step by the phone's `ws` list (join.js:848, 886; wheeltables.js:57-62)
  - `stats_update` with a higher track level: level raised, all three pools full (grids.js:823-849); no prog3 branch, unlike the T1 stats (grids.js:731)
  - `forge_weapon` / `harden_weapon` / `equip_request` during a fight with a player: allowed (gear.js:492-500, 706-711; hardening.js:242-247), while the bag's other ways out refuse (nomansland.js:247-252)
- Diagram edges:
  - `Alive --move dead:true (phone) !!--> ClaimsDead (no monster, player or NML reaches it)`
  - `Alive --worker blow to 0 HP--> Dying`
  - `Dying --5 s, socket there--> Alive in town`
  - `Dying --5 s, no socket !!--> RespawnOwed (never replayed)`
  - `Dying --game closed inside 5 s, next join at 0 HP !!--> Dying again (second death)`
  - `ShieldDown --move blocking:true--> Raised (parry 250 ms)`
  - `Raised --250 ms--> Held`
  - `Held --move false then true, no gap !!--> Raised (parry re-armed)`
  - `Held --stamina 0 (regen tick or a player's hit)--> Broken 3 s`
  - `Idle --move ex:cook--> CookShielded 120 s`
  - `CookShielded --attack + ex:null + ex:cook in one bundle !!--> CookShielded`
  - `InWheel --move z:dungeon:<any>, then z:wheel !!--> InWheel + 500 ms zero damage`
  - `Joining --ws without the room's size !!--> OutOfStep all session (no monster, no NML)`
  - `Track L --stats_update L+1 on prog3 !!--> Track L+1 + full HP, stamina, mana`

- [CONFIRMED] S1-2 / S3-3 (authority): a phone that says `dead: true` on its moves while the worker has it alive drops off every monster's list and out of every player-hit check, while its own blows on monsters still land; around its own attack on a player it flips the claim off and on inside one bundle. Evidence: movement.js:796 (the write); index.js:1941 (the monster roster, which feeds the sticky pick index.js:2278 and the nearest pick index.js:2334-2356); combat.js:2321 (the player-hit loop); nomansland.js:256, 376 (No man's land level 0, target refused); telegraph.js:437, 458, 492; monstermoves.js:521, 593; monsterstatus.js:379, 510; firetrail.js:231; against combat.js:1210 (a monster attacker is asked `dying` only) and combat.js:2215 / nomansland.js:372 (a player attacker is asked `dead`, which the bundle sets false for that part: index.js:5050-5056). ## What needs further thought
- Should the server stop believing the phone about being dead, its shield's timing, cooking, its area and its world size, the way it stopped believing `dodging` in v2.3.3292 (movement.js:42-62: the phone's word counts only inside a roll the server charged for)? Every high concern here is that same mistake: the phone's word turned straight into protection.
- When a player closes the game in the five seconds after dying, should the next launch replay the death (today: a second death screen, and a duel's or dungeon's kept bag goes on the ground), or just stand them up in town at full health?
- In a losing fight in No man's land, may a player still forge, harden or swap which blade they hold, and so keep that value from the winner?
- Making it explicit: the worker keeps one record per player for these phases, say `ps.guardState = {life: 'alive'|'dying', shield: {phase: 'down'|'raised'|'held'|'broken', since, downSince}, activity: {kind, since, x, y, by: 'server'}, entryGraceUntil, inStep}`, changed only by one function, `_phaseSet(ps, event, now)`. The move's fields become requests that function may refuse: `dead` is ignored (the worker's dying is the truth; every target gate asks `life`), a raise re-arms the parry only after a minimum time down and never while dazed, a cook shield starts from the cooking handler (which knows the fire) and not for a few seconds after an attack, the entry grace is stamped only on a zone entry the worker placed, an out-of-step join is refused with a reload reason, and the skill-track restore is skipped under prog3.
- Quick fixes vs design work: one or two lines each: skip the restore under `ps.prog3` (grids.js:844); break the guard at 0 stamina in `_hitStopped` as the player path does (hits.js:336); refuse a raise while dazed or frozen (movement.js:780); require a minimum time down before a raise re-arms the parry (movement.js:782); refuse a cook/fire re-arm for a few seconds after `_endExtraction` (gathering.js:980); delete or rehome the dead `_respawnOwed` (a Map on the room keyed by player id would survive the object); a small sweep: the target gates ask `ps.dying` instead of the phone's `ps.dead` (index.js:1941, combat.js:2321, nomansland.js:256 and 376, telegraph.js, monstermoves.js, monsterstatus.js, firetrail.js, pvpextras.js, pvplunge.js). Need the owner's decision: the zone-flip grace and the minted dungeon ids (server-placed zone entry, or accept a dungeon id only for a live instance, which the code avoided because a deploy wipes instances: movement.js:350-369); what to do with an out-of-step page (refuse the join, or let it in but not attack); the second death on a rejoin; the fight lock on forge, harden and equip; retiring the old-client all-round block (`ba` missing) now that every page sends it.
