# V8b · Trades and wagers between players: the gift, the trade window, the arena bet, the daily spin's pot and Ace's coin flip
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Severity: medium (an honest player's trade window can freeze until a reload; a server update in the wrong millisecond can lose an arena stake, hand a paid-for weapon back to its seller, or pay a spin pot twice; a modified phone can empty a losing bag past the PvP fight lock)
- Who can trigger it: honest player: have a trade window open when the phone drops its connection or the server updates (the window then cannot be closed until a reload); tap "Open trade" twice, or Trade on anyone's card mid-trade (the window vanishes while the trade lives); flip with Ace again within 2 s (told "not enough gold") | deploy/restart timing: the stake, a trade with weapons, the spin pot, the season mail-out, a quest weapon, the arena crown's mail | modified phone only: the gift trade in a losing fight, decline-then-accept | owner only: the season switch's empty "season over" note
- Summary: Players move value in five ways: a one-way gift, the two-sided trade window, an arena bet, the daily spin's pot and Ace's coin flip. Each has a hidden stage spread over the server and the phone. Verified: a server update at the wrong moment can lose a bet, return a traded weapon after its price was paid, or pay a spin pot twice; an open trade window can freeze on the phone after a dropped connection; and the old gift trade skips the fight lock.
- Key files: server/src/trade2.js:329, src/networking/wsClient.js:1647, server/src/gladiator.js:465, server/src/dailyrewards.js:592, server/src/trade.js:127, src/ui/panels/AceFlipPanel.jsx:227

## The machine
- Entity, and where its state lives: one transfer of value between two players, or between a player and the house.
  - Gift: `_pendingTradeOffers` 'sender>target' -> {offer, ts, id}, worker memory (server/src/trade.js:114-122).
  - Trade window, worker: `_trades2` sessions {state, ready, confirmed, changedAt, ts, offers, pets, weapons} and `_t2Invites` 'from>to' -> ts, memory (server/src/trade2.js:133-163); weapon escrow rows `trade2wpn:<pid>:<seq>`, storage (server/src/trade2.js:579).
  - Trade window, phone: ONE `trade2` React slot (src/ui/BroTown.jsx:2070) and the receipt watcher `S._t2DoneWatch` (src/networking/gameEvents.js:4984).
  - Arena stake: `arena_stake:<tid>:<mid>:<pid>` and its oplog stamps in storage; the bracket in memory (server/src/gladiator.js:463-467).
  - Spin pot: `daily_rewards:<pid>.sp` {open, pot, k, run} (server/src/dailyrewards.js:647) and the payment stamp `spinpot:<pid>:<run>` (:588).
  - Ace: the phone's `aceFlipBus` {open, pending, result, note} (src/ui/mobile/aceFlipBus.js:19-47); the worker rolls and pays in one event (server/src/gamble.js:226-262).
- States:
  - gift offered: a 2-minute offer the target may accept (server/src/trade.js:116-125)
  - trade invited: one side asked; the other's phone shows a request card (server/src/trade2.js:170-172)
  - trade open/offer: both edit; any edit clears both Ready and both Accept (server/src/trade2.js:182-194)
  - trade open/review: both Ready; Accept allowed 2.5 s after the last edit and not in a player fight (server/src/trade2.js:225-234)
  - committing: items, gold and pets in one write, then each weapon in a write of its own (server/src/trade2.js:329-367)
  - done/receipt: the phone holds the receipt 4.2 s (src/ui/panels/TradeWindowPanel.jsx:769)
  - cancelled: declined, expired, disconnected, busy, insufficient (server/src/trade2.js:113-122, 136-138, 436-459)
  - phantom (phone only): a window for a trade the worker no longer has
  - stake escrowed -> settled into the pool | refunded by the join sweep (server/src/gladiator.js:489-538, 758-775)
  - pot closed -> open (pot, doubles) -> paid | lost; an open pot is paid at the day's end (server/src/dailyrewards.js:426-444, 631-668)
  - flip idle -> pending -> settled | "refused" after 6 s, whatever the reason (src/ui/panels/AceFlipPanel.jsx:227-236)
- Moves:
  - trade2_open toward B: none -> invited (server/src/trade2.js:170-172); B opens back within 60 s: invited -> open (server/src/trade2.js:144-166)
  - Ready from both: offer -> review; Back: review -> offer, your Accept KEPT (server/src/trade2.js:203-212)
  - any edit (items, gold, weapon, pets, a pet renamed): open -> offer, readies and accepts cleared (server/src/trade2.js:182-194, 496-503, 512-523, 588-594, 614-621)
  - both Accept: review -> committing -> done (server/src/trade2.js:235-376); phone: done-watch -> receipt -> closed (src/networking/gameEvents.js:4937-4999, src/ui/panels/TradeWindowPanel.jsx:769)
  - cancel, 5-min idle, disconnect, invite 60 s: -> cancelled, both told (server/src/trade2.js:113-122, 419-459)
  - WRONG: a second trade2_open while trading: the phone's window -> closed, the session stays (server/src/trade2.js:136-138, src/networking/gameEvents.js:5022)
  - MISSING: server update, or a drop the worker sees first: session -> gone, phone window -> phantom, never cleared (server/src/trade2.js:12, src/networking/wsClient.js:1641-1647)
  - WRONG: restart during a weapons commit: half done; the join sweep refunds the weapons (server/src/trade2.js:356-367, 650-668)
  - MISSING GUARD: gift accept: offered -> settled with no fight check (server/src/trade.js:127-174)
  - WRONG: stake: debit in one write, record in the next (server/src/gladiator.js:465-467)
  - WRONG: spin Take: paid in write 1, closed in write 3 (server/src/dailyrewards.js:583-594, 674-675)
  - WRONG: Ace flip: pending -> idle "not enough gold" after 6 s for every silent refusal, the 2 s cooldown included (src/ui/panels/AceFlipPanel.jsx:227-236, server/src/gamble.js:237)
- Diagram edges:
  - `None --trade2_open--> Invited`

  - `Review --both Accept, 2.5 s, no fight--> Committing`
  - `Committing --one write: items, gold, pets--> Done`

  - `Offer --cancel, idle 5 min, disconnect--> Cancelled`

  - `GiftOffer --!! accept in a player fight, no lock--> Settled`

## What needs further thought
- Should the server still accept the old one-way gift trade, now that every phone uses the trade window? Turning it off, or giving it the fight lock, closes a way to empty a losing bag in a player fight.
- When a trade window loses its server (an update or a dropped connection), should the phone simply close it with "The trade was cancelled", or should the server keep the trade for a short grace period so it can carry on?
- The "server update in the middle of a payment" cases (arena bet, a trade with weapons, the spin pot, the season mail-out, a quest weapon, the crown's mail) each need an update to land in a few milliseconds, but each loses or doubles value, and the project's rule is one write. Fix all of them now, or only the weapon trade and the spin pot, which move the most?
- Making it explicit: for the trade window, one session record with a declared `stage` (invited -> offer -> review -> committing -> done | cancelled(reason)) and one transition function every handler calls; every message names its subject (the session id, or the invite's two ids); the phone keys its slot by that subject, drops it on every state_sync as it already does the party (src/networking/wsClient.js:1641-1647), and the worker answers a command for a missing session with a 'gone' cancel. For the payments, one helper that issues the payment, its stamp and the record saying "paid" in one synchronous run (the duel's `_escrowDebitGoldTogether`, the daily quests' `_creditLiveTogether` beside `_drSave`), used by the stake, the pot, the weapon deliveries, the season mail-out, the quest weapons and the mail drain. For Ace, an attempt id sent and echoed, and a "no answer yet" state instead of a guessed refusal.
- Quick fixes vs design work: one line or close: clear `trade2` and `_t2DoneWatch` on state_sync, and answer trade2_cancel for a missing session; `_bagLockedByFight` in `_interceptTrade` (or retire the gift lane with a live flag); the stake through `_escrowDebitGoldTogether`; a 2 s cooldown on Ace's Flip button (or a reason on that refusal); the worker's `now` on trade2_state for the countdown; trade2_invite stopping the receipt watcher; opening Ace's dialog clearing the last result; a stamp on the season's freeze grant. Needs a decision or real work: the weapons inside the trade's one write (stash room and the mailbox must be read before the commit); the pot paid with `_creditLiveTogether` beside `_drSave`; the season mail-out and the quest's unfit weapons written with their records; the achievement put moved from `_achvGrant` into each caller's batch; 'busy' split into "you are already trading" (send your window back) and "they are"; whether the gift lane lives on.
