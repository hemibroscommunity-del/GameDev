- Area: server
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Why: the ledger was designed to be cleared on a zone change, and the world is now ONE zone, so damage from a monster's earlier life is still on file when you melee-kill its next life.
- Key files: server/src/combat.js:640, server/src/combat.js:661, server/src/hits.js:278, server/src/movement.js:818, server/src/index.js:1977
- Entity: one player (ps), keyed by monster id
- State lives in: `ps.dmgFromMonster[monsterId]` (combat.js:642-643; runtime only, never saved)
- States: per monster id: none, owed (damage taken, not yet refunded), refunded/forfeited
- Transitions:
  - any landed (or graced) monster blow through the door: none/owed → owed += taken (hits.js:278); fire patches and the heap count too (mid = patch.mid / 'heap', firetrail.js:239, heapburn.js:76)
  - your melee killing blow on that id: owed → refunded 90%, entry deleted (combat.js:661-672, from combat.js:2005)
  - your bow/staff/dot killing blow: entry KEPT (combat.js:664 returns before the delete)
  - someone else's kill of that id: entry kept
  - slime blast hit you as its killer: owed → forfeited (combat.js:2001-2003)
  - your death / respawn / zone change: all → none (index.js:3392, index.js:3572, movement.js:818)
- Guards that read it: _applyMeleeLifesteal (combat.js:661-672); pvpextras _pvpKillHeal (player twin)
- Counterpart/linked: client's old applyMeleeLifesteal (spec says it was to be removed); docs/specs/lifesteal-server.md; S4-9 kill resolution
- Concerns:
  - [divergence] (high confidence, mechanism) Monster objects keep their id across lives (the respawn reuses `m`, index.js:1977-2061) and in the Wheel nobody changes zone (movement.js:818 is the only bulk reset short of dying). So: a Wheel monster hits you for 300, a friend (or your own bow) kills it, it respawns 18.75 s later, and your sword kills the new one without being touched: _applyMeleeLifesteal finds 300 owed and heals 270. The spec's own rule "Player kills a monster that never hit them -> No refund" (lifesteal-server.md:121) and its "Cleanup on zone change ... so stale monster IDs don't linger" (:106-112) both assumed zones; the Wheel removed that boundary. Evidence: combat.js:664/671, movement.js:818, index.js:1977-2061. Docs: lifesteal-server.md:106-125 (open question at :169 already asked about despawn hygiene).
  - [leak] (low confidence) Entries for every monster that hit you without your melee kill stay until you die; dungeon monster ids are unique per run (`dm-<inst>-...`, wheeldungeon.js:197), but entering/leaving a dungeon is a zone change, so the growth is bounded by the Wheel's ~384 ids. Small.
- Making it explicit: key the ledger by monster LIFE (an `m.life` counter bumped at respawn) or clear every player's entry for `m.id` inside _resolveMonsterKill.
