## S5-2 · Duel machine (challenge → accept → active → resolved), with its wager escrow and away clocks
- Area: server
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Why: A duel survives one side walking into another zone, and nothing ends it but the 10-minute clock, so for up to 10 minutes both players get no HP regen and the hider can win the wager by hp-fraction tiebreak.
- Key files: server/src/duel.js:103-214, server/src/duel.js:228-258, server/src/duel.js:284-338, server/src/index.js:6431-6439, server/src/index.js:3676-3688
- Entity: a pair of players (room-wide maps on the GameRoom)
- State lives in: `this._duelChallenges` Map 'challenger>target' → {wager, ts} (server/src/duel.js:120,133); `this._duels` Map id → {status 'active'|'resolved', a, b, wager, startedAt, expiresAt, away{pid:deadline}, arenaMatch?} (server/src/duel.js:79-91,207); `this._pvpConsent` pair key → expiry (server/src/duel.js:210, shared with arena and threat); storage `duelEscrow:<id>` (server/src/duel.js:198); oplog stamps `duel:<id>:a|b`, `duelpot:<id>`, `duelrefund:<id>:a|b`
- States: none → challenged (2 min TTL) → active → (away: one or both players on a 15 s forfeit clock) → resolved {kill | death | forfeit | timeout}; escrow: held → paid | swept-refund
- Transitions:
  - duel_request / duel_wager_request: none → challenged; a re-send overwrites the pair's challenge (server/src/duel.js:124-135)
  - duel_decline: challenged → none (server/src/duel.js:137-140)
  - tick: challenged → none after CHALLENGE_TTL (server/src/duel.js:285-288)
  - duel_accept (fresh challenge, neither in a duel, same zone, wager ≤ shown, both pay): challenged → active; escrow and stakes in one write (server/src/duel.js:143-213)
  - arena activation builds an active duel directly (server/src/gladiator.js:383-388)
  - socket close: active → away[pid] = now+15 s, and the consent pair is cleared (server/src/index.js:6435,6439; server/src/duel.js:260-268)
  - rejoin: away[pid] deleted, consent re-registered for 10 min (server/src/join.js:1598; server/src/duel.js:270-280)
  - tick: away clock expired → resolved 'forfeit', earliest leaver loses (server/src/duel.js:301-310)
  - tick: now > expiresAt → resolved 'timeout', higher hp fraction wins, coin flip on a tie (server/src/duel.js:328-335)
  - death: resolved 'kill' (cause pvp:<opponent>, no pile/wipe) or 'death' (server/src/duel.js:219-226; server/src/index.js:3417)
  - resolve: duel deleted, consent pair deleted, duel_end broadcast, pot credited fire-and-forget then escrow deleted (server/src/duel.js:228-257)
  - join-path sweep: an escrow >10 min old with no live duel → refund both, unless `duelpot:` is stamped (server/src/duel.js:345-364)
  - deploy: all three maps wiped; escrow survives for the sweep (handoff rule 11)
- Guards that read it: `_duelFor` (server/src/duel.js:93) is read by the HP regen gate (server/src/index.js:3676), the PvP eating throttle (server/src/cooking.js:597), linger (server/src/linger.js:78), ride (server/src/ride.js:104), farm orders (server/src/farmorders.js:196), heap burn (server/src/heapburn.js:67), clan war and bounty (server/src/clans.js:383; server/src/threat.js:211), arena activation (server/src/gladiator.js:378); damage gate `_pvpAllowed` reads `_pvpConsent` (server/src/index.js:3115-3127)
- Counterpart/linked: client DuelRequestPanel / InspectPlayerPanel and `S._inDuel`/`S._activeDuel` (src/networking/wsClient.js:5258,5308); S5-3 (the arena rides this machine); threat.js writes the same `_pvpConsent` keys (server/src/threat.js:164-165,179-180; see S5-19), so a duel's resolve deletes a threat consent between the same pair (server/src/duel.js:232) and a death clears every pair (server/src/index.js:3438); relay types (server/src/relay.js:46-49)
- Concerns:
  - [stuck] (medium) Two players duel, one walks through a door into another zone (a dungeon, the farm). The accept checks the zone, but a duel under way is never ended by a zone change. combat.js only lets a swing land on players in the attacker's zone, so the duel cannot be won and runs to its 10-minute clock. For those 10 minutes `inDuel` turns off both players' HP regen, in town and outside it (server/src/index.js:3683,3688), eating is throttled (server/src/cooking.js:597), riding and farm orders are refused (server/src/ride.js:104; server/src/farmorders.js:196), the bag is fight-locked (shop, market, auction, trade, drop and planting refused: server/src/nomansland.js:247-252 via server/src/farmorders.js:195-199), and neither can duel anyone else. Evidence: server/src/duel.js:172-174 ("a duel already under way survives one side zoning out, which is what the disconnect/forfeit clocks are for"), but the forfeit clock starts only in webSocketClose (server/src/index.js:6435), never on a zone change; no zone hook calls the duel. Docs: v2.3.1973 note (server/src/duel.js:52-78) bounded the old "forever" to 10 min; none found on zoning out.
  - [authority] (medium) Hit-and-hide: in a wagered duel, land one blow, then step into a zone the opponent cannot reach. Both players' regen is frozen, so at the 10-minute clock the hider's higher hp fraction wins the pot (server/src/duel.js:329-334). Evidence as above; a dungeon door does not refuse a dueller (server/src/dungeon.js:294-297 checks only that the player is alive; see S5-13), and a party leader's start pulls a dueller in too (server/src/dungeon.js:363-392).
  - [race] (low) A plain duel_request overwrites a pending wager challenge between the same pair (a single slot per pair), so an accept echoing the old shown wager is refused when the new stake is higher. The guard is deliberate (server/src/duel.js:180-188; TRAPS §152, v2.3.3168).
  - [persistence] (low) A deploy wipes `_duels` mid-duel. The wager is refunded only after the record is 10 min old, on a join, at most once every 5 min (server/src/duel.js:347,354). By design (handoff rules 6 and 11).
- Making it explicit: a `DUEL_PHASE` enum on the record with one `_duelLeave(pid, why)` exit that a zone change, a disconnect and a death all call, so zoning out starts the same forfeit clock as a disconnect.

- Area: server
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Why: The "no healing in a match" flag `ps._arenaMatch` lives on playerState, which every reconnect rebuilds, so a fighter who drops and comes back (the case v2.3.3713 handles) can eat fish, Garden Stew and shop heals for the rest of the match.

- Transitions:

  - the 8th entry, or the gather deadline with ≥2: gathering → running round 1; overflow refunded fire-and-forget (server/src/gladiator.js:284,320-321,338-356)

  - deploy: everything in memory voided; join-path sweeps refund entries and stakes older than 10 min unless the pot or stake stamps show a payout (server/src/gladiator.js:735-775; server/src/join.js:1600-1601)

- Counterpart/linked: client `S._arenaMatch` set from /status polling (src/ui/BroTown.jsx:9066-9116) and arena_match_start (src/networking/gameEvents.js); legacy client result POST (src/networking/gameEvents.js:4566-4587, answered and ignored at server/src/gladiator.js:133-137); S5-2 (duel), S5-4 (sponsorship pool); spec docs/specs/arena.md, docs/specs/arena-area.md
- Concerns:
  - [persistence] (high) The healing gate does not survive a reconnect. `ps._arenaMatch` is written only at activation (server/src/gladiator.js:389). A socket close deletes playerState (server/src/index.js:6397); a duel never lingers (server/src/linger.js:78); every join builds a new playerState object (server/src/join.js:876); and the rejoin hook re-sends arena_match_start without setting the flag again (server/src/gladiator.js:413-419). So a fighter whose socket drops within the duel's 15 s grace comes back with no `_arenaMatch`. Then `eat_request` (server/src/cooking.js:162), the Garden Stew (server/src/cooking.js:697) and the shop healFish (server/src/cooking.js:738) heal them, held back only by the PvP throttle of one bite every 15 s (server/src/cooking.js:616-623; server/src/data.js:538-540), about twelve fish over a 3-minute match. HP regen and safe-heal stay gated because they also ask `_duelFor`. The hub stamina top-off (server/src/index.js:3829) also reads only `_arenaMatch`, but it runs only in the 'town', 'worldview' and 'farm_home' zones (server/src/index.js:3658), so in the Wheel it matters only at the farm. The same happens after a second-tab takeover (server/src/join.js:758-768 evicts without a close, and line 876 rebuilds the state). Turning airplane mode on and off is enough. Evidence: as above; arena.test.mjs:617 checks only the re-sent message. Docs: docs/specs/arena.md:40-45 ("Healing disabled during matches ... set/cleared by activation/resolution"); index.js:3670-3675 explains why the regen gate derives `inDuel` rather than trusting a flag on ps.
  - [race] (medium) Walkover with no grace. A pending match activates the moment the round before it ends (server/src/gladiator.js:616-640), or at the gather deadline. A fighter with no playerState at that instant, for instance an iPhone tab put away while the other semifinal finished (a socket close deletes playerState, server/src/index.js:6397), loses by walkover at once (server/src/gladiator.js:371-373): eliminated, entry spent, no 15 s grace. The same blip one second later, inside an active match, would get the duel's 15 s grace (server/src/duel.js:260-268). Evidence: as cited. Docs: docs/specs/arena.md:29-30 ("Offline player at activation = walkover"), which is deliberate; the gap between "offline" and "dropped for a few seconds" is not discussed.
  - [guard-drift] (medium) The same question, "is this player in an arena match?", is answered two ways: by `ps._arenaMatch` in cooking.js and at index.js:3829, and by `_arenaMatch || _duelFor` in regen, safeheal and heapburn. The two answers disagree exactly in the reconnect case above.
  - [stuck] (low) Matches are activated with no zone check, unlike the social accept (server/src/duel.js:175). A fighter who walked into a dungeon during the 60 s gather gets a match nobody can win by fighting, decided by the 3-minute hp tiebreak (server/src/gladiator.js:366-399). Docs: docs/specs/arena.md:46-49 ("Venue v1: town + consent pairs ... no teleport"), so it is a known limit of venue v1.
  - [race] (low) A fighter waiting between rounds may accept a social duel (`_interceptDuel` has no arena check, server/src/duel.js:146). Their next match is then deferred as "busy" and decided by the timeout tiebreak (server/src/gladiator.js:374-378). It is self-inflicted and cannot deadlock the bracket, by design (docs/specs/arena.md:27-30).
  - [stuck] (low) A fighter who drops between rounds within 10 s of the last match's blows lingers (server/src/linger.js:68-97; they were not in a duel at the close). Their next match then activates against the still body with no duel `away` clock, so it runs to its kill or the 3-minute clock instead of a 15 s forfeit.
- Making it explicit: derive "in an arena match" from the duel record (`this._duelFor(pid)?.arenaMatch`) in one helper, as index.js already does for duels, and drop the ps flag; and keep the bracket's phase as one `status` with the match's `pending|active|won` as a field rather than the `winner`/`active` pair.

## S5-4 · Arena sponsorship stake (placed/escrowed → settled pool | swept refund)
- Area: server
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Why: The stake's debit (with its stamp) and its `arena_stake:` record are two awaited writes, so a restart between them takes the gold with no record for the sweep to refund. This is the split that v2.3.3185 closed for duel wagers and arena entries but not for stakes.
- Key files: server/src/gladiator.js:440-476, server/src/gladiator.js:489-538, server/src/gladiator.js:758-775, server/src/inbox.js:648-679
- Entity: one spectator's stake on one arena match
- State lives in: storage `arena_stake:<tid>:<mid>:<sid>` {tid, matchId, sponsorId, targetId, amount, ts} (server/src/gladiator.js:463-467); oplog `arenastake:` (debit), `arenastakeloss:`, `arenastakewin:`, `arenastakecut:`, `arenastakerefund:` (server/src/gladiator.js:465,512-521,770); the match's `winner` (server/src/gladiator.js:600)
- States: none → escrowed (gold taken, record written) → settled-lost (stamped) | settled-won (credited) → record deleted; or escrowed → orphaned (tournament voided by a deploy) → refunded by the sweep
- Transitions:
  - arena_sponsor (running, open match, not your own, 10..5000, no record yet): none → debit+stamp (server/src/gladiator.js:465) → record put (server/src/gladiator.js:467) → ack and board broadcast (server/src/gladiator.js:468-475)
  - match resolved: fire-and-forget settle lists the match's records, stamps the losers, credits the fighter's cut, credits the winners, deletes the records and tells each sponsor (server/src/gladiator.js:489-537)
  - join-path sweep (once per 5 min, stake older than 10 min, tournament not live): refund unless a win or loss stamp exists, then delete (server/src/gladiator.js:758-775)
- Guards that read it: the stake record is the one-per-match check (server/src/gladiator.js:464); `m.winner` refuses late stakes (server/src/gladiator.js:456); the sweep's `_opSeen` pair (server/src/gladiator.js:767-768)
- Counterpart/linked: client PartyPanel spectator betting, gated on `caps.sponsor` (docs/specs/sponsorship.md:29-31); legacy `arena_bet` relay stays cosmetic (TRAPS §1); S5-3 bracket
- Concerns:
  - [persistence] (medium) Restart between the debit and the record. `_escrowDebitGold` commits the coins and the `arenastake:` stamp and returns (server/src/inbox.js:665-671); only then is `arena_stake:` put, in its own awaited write (server/src/gladiator.js:467). A restart between the two (every server merge restarts the room) voids the tournament (in memory). The sweep only walks `arena_stake:` records (server/src/gladiator.js:763), so this stake is never refunded, and a retry is answered "no-tournament". Evidence: server/src/gladiator.js:465-467. Docs: docs/specs/inbox-escrow.md:66-72 lists duel wagers and arena entries as moved to one-run writes (v2.3.3185), but stakes are not on that list; docs/specs/sponsorship.md:41-42 says "record persisted before the ack", which holds, but not before the debit commits.
  - [persistence] (low) A settlement that throws part-way (after the losers are stamped, before the winners are paid) costs the pool. The sweep then refunds the unstamped winners their stake only. This is deliberate: "a crash part-way costs a payout, never makes one twice" (server/src/gladiator.js:486-488).
- Making it explicit: put the stake record in the same synchronous run as the debit (`_escrowDebitGoldTogether([{id, amount, opId}], [[key, rec]])`, as duel.js:196-198 does).

## S5-5 · Sparring session and its clones as fake players (none → clones out → cleared)
- Area: server
- Verdict: watch
- Why: A clone is a full `playerState` entry with no socket, so every system that walks `playerState` must remember to skip `_spar` on its own (at least 14 places do); a new walker that forgets will treat a clone as a player.

- Concerns:
  - [opacity] (medium) The "this is not a real player" rule is spread over at least 14 skip-checks in 10 files. Any walker of `playerState` that does not check `_spar` treats a clone as a player: an area hit, a count, an event's rankings, a party lookup, the arena's `_arenaTiebreak`. Evidence: the list above; `_pvpAreaPlayers` relies on `_pvpAllowed` to refuse them (server/src/pvpextras.js:234-242). Docs: spar.js header (server/src/spar.js:35-40).
