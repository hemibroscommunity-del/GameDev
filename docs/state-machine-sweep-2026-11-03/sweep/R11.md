## R11-1 · The Wheel objects' sprite-sheet pages (wanted, loading, ready, failed, dropped)
- Area: rendering
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Why: A page that fails once is never asked for again while you stay near it, so its buildings and trees stay invisible and walk-through; a stalled read holds one of only two load slots with no timeout.
- Key files: src/rendering/wheelObjects.js:315-355, src/rendering/wheelObjects.js:483-500, src/rendering/wheelObjects.js:225-279, src/rendering/wheelObjects.js:401-423, src/rendering/png8Worker.js:35-58

- Transitions:

  - the way in (worldTrial preloadWheel, behind the overlay): every page round the arrival -> loading, ignoring MAX_LOADING, waited on at most PAGE_WAIT_MS 9 s (:387-398)
  - worker read ok: loading -> ready, kept as colour numbers (:331-332, :339-347); read fails while the rec is current: -> loading-as-picture (:333-336)
  - load settles but `_pages.get(k) !== rec || rec.gen !== _gen` (the Wheel left or rebuilt meanwhile): -> stale, sheet freed (:343, :367-370)
  - load rejects: loading -> failed (:348-352)
  - not in the keep set (DROP_MARGIN 1000) for DROP_MS 4 s and not loading: ready|failed -> absent; its sprites destroyed first, shadow textures released, then the sheet (:496-499, :772-780, :371-381)

- Counterpart/linked: R11-2 (break visuals use texOf), R11-4 (footprints), R11-23 (building life, freed with the pages), wheelGround.js's piece streaming (R11-7, the same rule), src/game/wheelTrial.js wheelOnStop, the QA scenario mp-wheelobjects (asserts `failed === 0` on arrival only: tools/qa/mp/mp-wheelobjects.mjs:109)
- Concerns:
  - [stuck] (medium confidence) A failed page is never retried while the player stays near it: a rec that exists is only re-stamped (`if (rec) { rec.wanted = now; continue; }`, src/rendering/wheelObjects.js:492-493), and a rec in the keep set never drops (:497), so only walking more than 1000 px past the view for 4 s clears the 'failed' rec. Until then every object on that sheet is undrawn (:521) AND stops nobody (:605-606) — a phone on a flaky network can walk through the town's buildings. No retry timer (compare standIns.js RETRY_MS 15 s, which R8 found). Evidence: :348-352, :491-499. Docs: none found.
  - [stuck] (low confidence) No timeout on a page read: readSheet's promise settles only on the worker's answer or stopPngWorker (:248-256), and png8Worker.js fetches with no AbortController (src/rendering/png8Worker.js:35, :44, :49). A stalled fetch keeps `_loading` raised; two of them fill MAX_LOADING (:73, :494) and no new page loads for the rest of the visit (the way-in warm bypasses the cap, the walk does not). Browsers do eventually fail a stalled fetch. Docs: none found.
  - [race] (low confidence) The picture path can unload a sheet a newer rec is about to use: rec A is loading via Assets.load(url) (palette drawing off, or its numbers failed to read), the Wheel is left or the renderer rebuilt and the page asked for again before A settles; rec B's Assets.load(url) gets Pixi's same cached promise, A's handler runs first, finds itself stale and calls Assets.unload(url) (:343, :367-370), and B then marks ready a sheet that is being destroyed (Pixi v8's Spritesheet.destroy empties `textures`, so the draw at :527-528 finds no frame and draws nothing) while the 'ready' rec still hands its objects' footprints to the walk test (:605-606): invisible walls until the page drops.   - [leak] (low confidence) wheelObjectsWarm's 9 s race timer is never cleared (:396); harmless.
- Making it explicit: a per-page record with a declared `{loading, ready, failed(retryAt)}` and a read timeout, plus a per-url owner count for the picture path.

## R11-2 · A Wheel object's hit, break and mending as drawn (shake, rubble, fade back)
- Area: rendering
- Verdict: watch
- Why: The renderer learns of hits, breaks and mendings only through a 64-event buffer that drops its OLDEST entries, and a slime blast pushed from the socket handler can overflow it, leaving rubble under a mended object or a broken one vanishing with no rubble.
- Key files: src/rendering/wheelObjects.js:636-675, src/rendering/wheelObjects.js:464-469, src/game/wheelBreak.js:83-86, src/game/wheelBreak.js:166-183, src/game/wheelBreak.js:200-240
- Entity: each Wheel object (by object index) on this screen; the rules half is src/game/wheelBreak.js (client-only by the owner's wish: "on the client side you could destroy buildings", src/game/wheelBreak.js:5-9)
- State lives in: `_shakes` (src/rendering/wheelObjects.js:445), `_fadeIn` (:446) and each sprite's `_mendA` (:715-716), the sprite's presence in `sprites` (:430, :517, :649), WheelShatter `heaps` (src/rendering/wheelShatter.js:224), `_blockers` (:434); driven by wheelBreak's `_dmg`, `_broken`, `_events` (src/game/wheelBreak.js:51-53)
- States: whole, shaking (220-700 ms after a hit), broken (no sprite, a heap flying then lying, no footprint), mending (heap fading 1.2 s, sprite fading in 1.6 s), whole; plus "smouldering" for a building (buildingFire.js, R10's slice)
- Transitions:
  - 'hit' event: whole -> shaking (src/rendering/wheelObjects.js:640-644); shake ends by its own clock or when the sprite goes (:688-696)
  - 'break' (count reaches the material's hp): shake and fade cleared, sprite destroyed, heap cut from its picture, debris burst queued, footprint filtered (:645-668); the footprint is ALSO dropped synchronously by the onWheelBreak listener (:452-456, src/game/wheelBreak.js:64-70)
  - every frame while `isWheelBroken(i)`: no sprite is made (:517)
  - 'repair' (REPAIR_MS 3 min up and the player clear of it, src/game/wheelBreak.js:256-285; or at once when more than MAX_BROKEN=24 are broken, :177-181): heap starts fading (src/rendering/wheelShatter.js:351-356), sprite fades in (:670-674, :710-717), footprints rebuilt (`_blockAt = null`, :455, :673)
  - leaving the Wheel or a black-screen rebuild (WheelObjects.destroy): everything whole at once (:793-800, src/game/wheelBreak.js:296-298); a new worker answer also resets it (sameWorld, src/game/wheelBreak.js:72-81)
- Guards that read it: draw (:517), footprints (:607), wheelPropRec / wheelPropArt for marks and chips (:999-1007), hit routing in combatHelpers/projectiles/visualSystems (`_broken.has`, src/game/wheelBreak.js:120)
- Counterpart/linked: src/game/wheelBreak.js (another slice owns the rules), R11-3 (heaps), R11-4 (footprints), buildingFire.js (`_fireEvent`, :678-684), peers' shots in src/game/visualSystems.js:462, slime blasts from src/networking/gameEvents.js:1421 via src/game/slimeBlast.js:23
- Concerns:
  - [combo] (medium confidence) `push` keeps at most 64 events by dropping the oldest (src/game/wheelBreak.js:83-86) and the renderer drains once a frame (src/rendering/wheelObjects.js:468). A slime blast runs from the socket handler (src/networking/gameEvents.js:1421) and strikes up to BLAST_MAX=40 objects in one call (src/game/wheelBreak.js:200-240), each pushing 'hit' + 'break', plus a 'repair' for the oldest broken object once more than 24 are broken (:177-181) — ~96 events for 40 trees, more with older rubble about. Several slimes dying in one frame, or blasts while the tab is hidden (no rAF, the socket still delivering) do the same. A dropped 'repair' leaves that object's heap lying for good (a heap goes only on remove or the HEAP_PX cap, src/rendering/wheelShatter.js:338-356) under the object drawn whole again (`isWheelBroken` is false, :517), with no fade; a dropped 'break' makes the object vanish with no shards or burst; a 'repair' whose 'break' was dropped fades a never-destroyed sprite in from alpha 0 (:670-674, :715). Docs: none found (CLAUDE-HISTORY.md:575-579 describes the break; nothing on the buffer).
  - [persistence] (low confidence, deliberate) Breaks live in memory on this screen only and a black-screen rebuild mends everything at once (destroy -> resetWheelBreak, :800) — the owner asked for client-side breaks (src/game/wheelBreak.js:5-9); noted, not a bug.
- Making it explicit: let the renderer read `_broken` (the truth) each frame and diff it against its own heaps and sprites, instead of trusting a lossy event stream for anything but the shake.
