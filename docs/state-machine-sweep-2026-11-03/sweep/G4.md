## G4-1 · A quest's status: offered, taken, handed in (the client's prediction of it)
- Area: wire (both)
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Why: the phone marks a quest taken or handed in (and pays gold, XP, points and opens the next step) before the worker answers, but only two of the worker's six refusal paths send the truth back, so a refused hand-in can stay "paid" on a v2 phone.
- Key files: src/game/quests.js:20, src/game/quests.js:121, server/src/quests.js:130, server/src/quests.js:306, server/src/quests.js:279
- Entity: one player's `rpg._quests[questId]` (and the gold/XP/points the hand-in pays)
- State lives in: `rpg._quests[qid]` (client src/game/quests.js:25, :169, :179; server server/src/quests.js:156, :373, :443), `rpg._questKills[qid]` (server-only writer, server/src/quests.js:216), the QuestPanel's `_claimRef` once-only latch (src/ui/panels/QuestPanel.jsx:271), `stage` 'talk'|'act' (src/ui/panels/QuestPanel.jsx:227)
- States: absent, available (opened by the step before), active, [complete: declared in QUEST_STATUS, written by nobody], turnedIn; plus the phone-only "ready" (active and its check() passes)
- Transitions:
  - tap Accept in the giver's dialogue: absent/available → active, locally first, then `quest_accept` (src/game/quests.js:25, :50); worker: same move after `_questBarred` (server/src/quests.js:152-156)
  - tap Claim (or the auto reward): active → turnedIn locally, coins += gold, xp += xp, AP += 5, `_compStats.questsCompleted++`, next → available (src/game/quests.js:169-180); worker: same after its gates (server/src/quests.js:373, :442-444)
  - worker's player_state: whole `_quests` replaced (src/networking/wsClient.js:2936)
  - refusal echo (barred, trade missing): worker resends `_quests` + `coins` (server/src/quests.js:155, :338, :357 → :281)
  - character reset / new character: map cleared (server persistence, outside this slice)
- Guards that read it: QuestPanel `_canTurnIn` = status 'active' + questObjectiveDone (src/ui/panels/QuestPanel.jsx:301); getNpcQuest (src/data/gameSystems.js:8417-8456); deriveQuestLog (src/ui/mobile/sheet/questModel.js:58); journalStatus (src/game/questJournal.js:139); questRoute `_routeQuests` (src/game/questRoute.js:73); isZoneUnlocked (src/game/questRoute.js:573); worker `_questBarred` / `_questObjectiveMet` (server/src/quests.js:245, :290)
- Counterpart/linked: server/src/quests.js (the authority); G4-2 tracked quest; G4-3 the road; daily quests (dailyrewards.js `_drOnQuestTurnIn`, server/src/quests.js:454); achievements Storyteller (`_achvOnQuest`, server/src/quests.js:448)
- Concerns:
  - [divergence] (medium confidence) The worker refuses a hand-in SILENTLY when the player is dying/dead/disconnected (server/src/quests.js:310), the XP skill is missing (:330), the quest is not active on the worker (:335) or the objective is not met (:347); only `_questBarred` and the trade refusal echo (:338, :357). In protocol v2 a refusal changes nothing, so no delta goes, and the phone keeps its prediction: quest turnedIn, +gold, +5 AP, +XP, the next step "available" and offered by the giver (getNpcQuest reads the local map), until some later change to `_quests`/`coins` or a reconnect. The objective-unmet path is the live one: the phone's per-quest `check()` (gameSystems QUEST_CHAINS) and the worker's declarative `objective` are different code, and mirror-audit holds gold/xp/next/after/trade/gives but not the objective itself (server/test/mirror-audit.test.mjs:348-380). The spec says every refusal echoes ("A refusal changes nothing and sends `_quests` and `coins` back", docs/specs/quests.md:91-93): the code does not. Evidence: server/src/quests.js:279-282, :347. Docs: docs/specs/quests.md:91.
  - [divergence] (medium confidence) Even the echo is partial: it resends `_quests` and `coins` only (server/src/quests.js:281), so the predicted `achievementPoints` +5 (src/game/quests.js:173) and `xp` (src/game/quests.js:171) stay on the phone after a barred or trade refusal. `achievementPoints` feeds the Achievements page via noteServerAp only from the echo (src/networking/wsClient.js:2939-2945), so the phone's own +5 sits in `rpg.achievementPoints` until the next real AP change. Evidence: src/game/quests.js:171-176. Docs: none found.
  - [race] (medium confidence) `wsOut` drops a message silently while the socket is not OPEN (src/networking/wsClient.js:5346), but acceptQuest/turnInQuest predict unconditionally once `S.channel` exists (src/game/quests.js:49, :156). A hand-in tapped during a reconnect shows QUEST COMPLETE, the banner, the flying coins and the next quest; the worker never heard it, and the reconnect's full snapshot quietly puts the quest back to active and takes the gold back. Evidence: src/game/quests.js:156-188, src/networking/wsClient.js:5345-5347. Docs: none found.
  - [guard-drift] (low confidence) The stored status 'complete' is declared (src/data/gameSystems.js:6590) and read as "ready" by deriveQuestLog (src/ui/mobile/sheet/questModel.js:58), the journal (src/game/questJournal.js:141), questJournalModel (src/ui/mobile/sheet/questJournalModel.js:59, :65) and QuestStepNudge (src/ui/mobile/QuestStepNudge.jsx:33), but getNpcQuest has no branch for it and SKIPS it to the giver's next quest (src/data/gameSystems.js:8445-8453), and the road ignores it (src/game/questRoute.js:73). Nobody writes 'complete' today (grep), so this is dormant: a worker that ever stores it makes the giver offer the next step instead of the hand-in. Docs: none found.
  - [authority] (low confidence) `_compStats.questsCompleted` and `totalGoldEarned` are counted by the phone's prediction, refused or not (src/game/quests.js:174-176), and published as the profile's `quests` (src/ui/BroTown.jsx:8207). Display only. Docs: none found.
- Making it explicit: one `questStep(status, event)` table shared by the client prediction and the worker, plus a rule "every quest refusal echoes `_quests`, `coins`, `achievementPoints`, `xp`" in one helper called from every `return` in `_handleQuestTurnIn`/`_handleQuestAccept`.
