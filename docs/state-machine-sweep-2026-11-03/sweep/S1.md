## S1-1 · A world monster's life and behaviour loop (_tickMonsters)
- Area: server
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Why: a ranged monster's ball in the air is never cleared on death or respawn, so it lands from the previous life on the first tick after the respawn; the respawn reset is a hand-kept list of a dozen phase fields.
- Key files: server/src/index.js:1977, server/src/index.js:2162, server/src/index.js:2429, server/src/telegraph.js:672, server/src/combat.js:1853
- Entity: one server monster `m` in `this.monsters[zoneId]` (the Wheel's ~190 and the old zones')
- State lives in: `m.alive`/`m.respawnAt` (index.js:1853-1858, combat.js:1853/1874), `m.targetId` (index.js:2430, 2609), `m._aggroOverrideTarget/_aggroOverrideUntil` (combat.js:254-255, 1559, 1802; abilities.js:966; arrowblast.js:192), `m._projImpactAt/_projTargetId/_projTx/_projFromX` (telegraph.js:672-688), `m._bwUntil/_bwKind` (telegraph.js), `m._tgPhase`, `m._burPhase`, `m._burstUntil`, `m._mvPhase` (other slices' resolvers), `m._attackingUntil`, `m.atkCd`, `m._stunUntil` (abilities.js), `m._kbDebt` (combat.js:1622, abilities.js:930), `m._wanderTx/_wanderTy/_wanderPausedUntil` (index.js:2640-2672), `m.variant` vs `m.spawnVariant` (index.js:2064-2082)
- States: dead-waiting-respawn, resting (Wheel, nobody near), resolving-a-phase (telegraph / move / basic wind-up / burrow / slime fuse), ball-in-flight (orthogonal), sticky-aggro, chasing, winding-up (throw or swing), leashed-home, wandering, wander-paused, cc-frozen; variant base -> transformed
- Transitions:
  - first tick with a player in the zone: the zone's list spawned (index.js:1877-1883; the Wheel's ~190 via wheelzone.js `_wheelSpawnMonsters`), kept for the worker's life except a dungeon's (dungeon.js:938); a zone nobody stands in is not ticked at all (index.js:1886-1892), so its clocks and balls wait
  - kill (any damage path): alive -> dead, respawnAt = now + _monsterRespawnMs (combat.js:1853, 1874; noRespawn keeps 0 for dungeons)
  - tick, now >= respawnAt: dead -> alive at spawn, hp full, and a hand list of phase fields reset (index.js:1977-2048)
  - tick, Wheel rest test: awake -> resting (index.js:2066-2068; wheelzone.js:301 reads _projImpactAt/_tgPhase/_bwUntil/_burPhase/_mvPhase)
  - first damage on a mummy: variant mummy -> skeleton, spd changes, monster_transform (index.js:2064-2082); respawn reverts (index.js:2040-2046)
  - a resolver owns the tick: _resolveMonsterTelegraph / _resolveMonsterMove / _resolveBasicWindup / _resolveBurrow / _resolveSlimeBurst each `continue` (index.js:2135-2153)
  - ball lands: now >= _projImpactAt -> cleared, hit through _monsterHit 'snowball' (index.js:2162-2232)
  - sticky override expires or its target is extracting/sheltered: override cleared (index.js:2269-2290)
  - Wheel chase leash or safe ground: chasing -> no target (index.js:2413-2425)
  - player within aggro: -> chasing, targetId set (index.js:2429-2470); in band: throw wind-up (index.js:2496-2524); burrow/move/telegraph start (index.js:2533-2554); in reach: swing wind-up (index.js:2560-2581)
  - nobody in aggro: chasing -> wander, targetId null, _tgLost stamped, _kbDebt dropped (index.js:2608-2613); leash pull-back, pick leg, arrive -> pause (index.js:2630-2675)
- Guards that read it: `!m.alive` skip (index.js:1977), `ccMoveMult > 0` on every start (index.js:2121-2122, 2533-2577), `!m._projImpactAt` one ball at a time (index.js:2497; telegraph.js:672), `_extractionShielded` / `_wheelSheltered` on the target (index.js:2280, 2398), aggro picks from playersByZone which drops dead/disconnected/spar (index.js:1939-1949)
- Counterpart/linked: the phase resolvers in telegraph.js, monstermoves.js, combat.js (burrow, slime burst; slices S3/S4); wheelzone.js rest test and separation (S3); tick.js monsterWire sends `tg` and the `_tgLost` edge; the client draws monster_projectile and monster_transform (src/networking/gameEvents.js)
- Concerns:
  - [stuck] (high confidence) A ball in the air when its thrower dies is never cleared: the kill path (combat.js:1853-1874) and the respawn reset (index.js:1979-2048) both leave `_projImpactAt/_projTargetId/_projTx/_projFromX` set, and the `!m.alive` branch `continue`s before the impact check (index.js:1977-2050 vs 2162). On the first tick after the respawn (18.75 s on the Wheel), `now >= _projImpactAt` is true and the old ball resolves: it hits the old target if they stand within 40 px of the old aim point (camping a spawn is the ordinary farming case), with no `monster_projectile` drawn for 19 s, reported `from` the spawn point. Every fodder (slime) kind throws (index.js:1263-1280), so this is live on the Wheel. The respawn list clears the same class for `_bwUntil` (v2.3.2215), `_tgPhase` (v2.3.3634) and `_burstUntil` (v2.3.2224) on purpose; the ball was missed. Evidence: telegraph.js:672-688, index.js:2162-2232, grep shows no other writer of `_projImpactAt`. Docs: docs/specs/monster-aggression.md:165 ("must land even if the target walked out"), nothing on the thrower's death.
  - [race] (medium confidence) The impact check sits AFTER the five resolvers that `continue` (index.js:2135-2153), so a ball in the air waits while its thrower is in a swing wind-up, a kit's telegraph or a snowman's burrow: it lands after the 900 ms the phone drew (the phone's ball has already burst), tested against the frozen aim point. A snowman throws, then drops to half and burrows: the ball hangs for the whole burrow. Evidence: index.js:2135-2162, index.js:2533. Docs: monster-aggression.md says impact is "resolved from the tick loop ... outside and ahead of the aggro branch", not ahead of the resolvers.
  - [combo] (low confidence, retired zones only) The sticky override (10 s, combat.js:1803) is not cleared on respawn; a respawn shorter than 10 s (spawnscale.js:222-228, only an old zone with 16+ players; the Wheel has no ZONES row so it always uses 18.75 s) brings a fresh monster back with a 1200 px pull on its killer. `_kbDebt` also survives a respawn and is repaid on the first chase (index.js:2453-2459, cleared only on aggro loss at 2613).
  - [opacity] The respawn reset is a manual list of ~25 fields across six systems (index.js:1979-2048), each added after its own incident (v2.3.1709, 2215, 2221, 2224, 3120, 3513, 3634, 4011); a new phase field on a monster has no mechanical check that it is reset.
- Making it explicit: one `_resetMonsterLife(m)` that every phase system registers its fields with (or a single `m.phase` slot plus a `m.inFlight` list), called from both the kill and the respawn, with a test that walks every `m._*` field written in server/src.

## S1-2 · A player's death and respawn (dying, dead, respawnAt, the owed respawn)
- Area: wire (both)
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Why: the worker skips a "dead" player using the phone's own word (`ps.dead`, written from every move), the owed-respawn replay can never fire because the join replaces the object it is written on, and the dying phase is not saved, so a close mid-death comes back as a second death.
- Key files: server/src/index.js:3383, server/src/index.js:3511, server/src/movement.js:796, server/src/join.js:876, server/src/join.js:1877
- Entity: one player's `playerState[id]` on the worker; the phone's `S._dying` / `S._deathStart` (src/networking/wsClient.js:3149, src/game/respawn.js)
- State lives in: `ps.dying` (worker only: index.js:3388, 3535; join.js:1337; spar.js:527, 592; devtools.js:370), `ps.dead` (index.js:3389, 3536, but ALSO the phone's claim on every move: movement.js:796), `ps.respawnAt` (index.js:3391, 3537; join.js:1338), `ps._deathKeepsBag` (index.js:3490, 3560), `ps._deathShield` (index.js:3443, 3570), `ps._lootRecovered` (index.js:4326, 3571), `ps._respawnOwed` (index.js:3596, 3606; read join.js:1877)
- States: alive, dying (dead + respawnAt, 5 s), respawned-in-town, respawn-owed (no socket heard it), stuck-at-zero (hp 0 but not dying), lingering-and-dying (linger.js), spar-down (spar.js)
- Transitions:
  - a blow takes hp to 0 (combat.js _applyDamage callers): alive -> dying, pile + wipe + player_died (index.js:3383-3502)
  - tick, hp <= 0 and not dying (any other source, a record restored at 0): alive -> dying, cause 'stuck-at-zero' (index.js:3527-3529)
  - tick, now >= respawnAt: dying -> alive in 'town', full bars, second wipe unless _deathKeepsBag (index.js:3531-3584)
  - no socket, or the send throws: -> respawn-owed (index.js:3596, 3606)
  - join: dying cleared, respawnAt 0 (join.js:1337-1338) on a freshly built object (join.js:876)
  - the phone: player_died -> `S._dying` (wsClient.js:3149); player_respawned -> applyLocalRespawn (wsClient.js:3242); watchdog after 20 s with hp > 0 (BroTown.jsx:5011-5013)
  - a move: `ps.dead = !!msg.dead` at any time (movement.js:796)
- Guards that read it: `ps.dying` (combat.js:1209 attacker, index.js:3254 abilities, index.js:4580 pickup), `ps.dead` ALONE: the monster roster (index.js:1940), the PvP target loop (combat.js:2321), No man's land's target (nomansland.js:376), the linger (linger.js:73), a kill's share (combat.js:1894), trapping.js:380; both: ~40 handlers (guilds.js:37, trade2.js:126, bank.js:224 ...)
- Counterpart/linked: the phone's death screen and watchdog (src/game/respawn.js, src/ui/BroTown.jsx:4992-5014); relays `player_died_to_monster` / `player_respawned` draw peers' corpses (relay.js:263, 268); linger (S1-4); spar.js; No man's land's `_nmlOnDeath` (slice S5); the death pile (S1-9)
- Concerns:
  - [authority] (high confidence) `ps.dead` is the phone's word (TRAPS §233), yet three server gates skip a player on it alone: the monster aggro roster (index.js:1940), the PvP target loop (combat.js:2321) and No man's land's target check (nomansland.js:376). A modified phone that sends `dead: true` on its moves is never chased or swung at by a world monster and cannot be hit by another player, while its own blows on monsters still land (combat.js:1209 asks only `dying`). TRAPS §233's rule ("ask `ps.dying` as well") does not help here: these gates need `ps.dying` instead of the claim, or the move must not be allowed to set `dead` while the worker has the player alive. Evidence: movement.js:796, index.js:1939-1941, combat.js:2321, nomansland.js:376. Docs: TRAPS §233, docs/specs/item-drop.md:173 ("Other places that ask only `ps.dead` were not changed").
  - [divergence] (high confidence) The owed respawn (v2.3.1822) is written on the old player object (index.js:3596, 3606) but every join builds a NEW `playerState[msg.id]` unconditionally (join.js:876) before it reads `_respawnOwed` (join.js:1876-1877), and the flag is not saved (not in persistence.js). The close path deletes the object anyway (index.js:6398). So the replay never fires; only the phone's 20 s watchdog (BroTown.jsx:5011) rescues a frozen death screen. combat-lifecycle.test:1205-1236 checks only that the flag is set, never that a join replays it. Docs: the comments at respawn.js:15 and BroTown.jsx:5000 say "the real fix is on the worker".
  - [persistence] (medium confidence) The dying phase is memory only: `_handlePlayerDeath` saves hp 0 (index.js:3491), and a close inside the 5 s window deletes the state before the respawn runs (index.js:6398; a dying body is never lingered, linger.js:73). The next join restores hp 0 (join.js:1001, `_keepBarFullness` keeps 0) with dying false (join.js:1337), and the stuck-at-zero sweep kills the player a second time (index.js:3527, cause 'stuck-at-zero'): a second player_died on a fresh launch, every death hook run again (_nmlOnDeath, war, bounty, pile). For a red skull, No man's land's "killed by a monster" rule takes the whole bag including the tools the first death kept (nomansland.js:67-97). Docs: v2.3.1562's comment names "a state restored from storage mid-death" as handled by a second death; no doc weighs the second death's hooks.
  - [opacity] Two flags mean "dead" with different owners (dying: worker; dead: worker and phone), and ~60 gates pick one or both by hand (grep `ps.dead` / `ps.dying` in server/src).
- Making it explicit: one worker-owned `ps.life` = 'alive' | 'dying' | 'down-spar', the phone's claim kept apart as `ps.claimDead` for drawing only, the respawn debt kept on the session (or in the last-place record) so a join can read it.

## S1-3 · A game socket's life on the worker (open, joined, closed) and its close codes
- Area: wire (both)
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Why: a join that throws closes with 4010, the code the linger refusal later took, so the phone shows "your bro is still in that fight" and retries every 5 s; "one join a socket" is keyed on `session.id`, which is set only after the join's awaits, one of which is a network fetch.

  - DO wake: every hibernated socket closed 'stale on wake' (index.js:1521-1527)

- Counterpart/linked: the phone's onclose machine (src/networking/wsClient.js:4560-4700: 4003, 4004, 4005, 4006, 4009, 4010, 4015, 'superseded by reconnect'); flood.js (slice S4) for the budget and the address limits; join.js (S2); linger (S1-4); the AFK clock (S1-8)
- Concerns:
  - [divergence] (high confidence) Close code 4010 means two things. index.js:5160 (v2.3.2438) closes a join that threw with 4010 and its comment says the phone "sees an unknown code and reconnects on its own backoff"; v2.3.3260 then made 4010 the linger refusal (linger.js:48) and the phone now takes ANY 4010 as one (wsClient.js:4612-4617): it shows LingerScreen ("Your bro is still in that fight") and retries on a fixed 5 s (LG_WAIT_DEFAULT, wsClient.js:390) with no backoff. A join that keeps throwing (a storage fault, a bad record) is a misleading countdown every 5 s forever. flood.test §7, origin.test and verify.test §7 each check their own code against 4010 but nothing checks index.js:5160. Docs: docs/specs/one-socket.md "Close codes" lists 4010 as the linger only.
  - [race] (low confidence) "One join a socket" (index.js:5141) is tested on `session.id`, which _handleJoin sets only at join.js:775, after its awaits. The account check's `_verifyToken` is a network fetch (verify.js:78, reached from join.js:517 for a new character and botsig.js:489), which opens the input gate (handoff rule 9), so a second `join` on the same socket with another id runs beside the first; the first id's player state then has no session and stays in the room (the exact v2.3.3274 bug). Needs a modified client and two account-check tokens. Evidence: index.js:5134-5143, join.js:674-683, 775.
  - [leak] (low confidence) The tick loop stops only in webSocketClose (index.js:6454). The kick, the reset and the device refusal call webSocketClose first (admin.js:72-75, persistence.js:564-565, devicelock.js:118-122), but the join's own refusals remove a never-joined session directly (join.js:652, 691, 700, 710, 716, 728, 744; verify.js:93). If that socket was the room's only one and the runtime never delivers its close (a peer that ignores the close frame), an empty room keeps its 22 ms interval and cannot hibernate (the cost tick.js:180-190 describes). Evidence: grep `this.sessions.delete(ws)`.
  - [watch] `webSocketError` calls webSocketClose without a catch (index.js:6460); an error there is an unhandled rejection.
- Making it explicit: `session.phase` = 'open' | 'joining' | 'joined' | 'closing', set first thing in each step (the 'join' case refuses unless 'open'); one `_dropSession(ws, code)` that every refusal, eviction and sweep goes through and that owns the tick-stop check; close codes in one shared table that the phone reads too.

## S1-4 · The body left in a fight (linger)
- Area: server
- Verdict: needs-thought
- Why: the linger exists to stop "close the game to escape a No man's land fight", but it lives only in webSocketClose, so a second tab or a relaunch whose join evicts the still-open socket skips it (and reloads HP up to 10 s stale), and a phone's own `dead: true` turns it off.
- Key files: server/src/linger.js:68, server/src/linger.js:159, server/src/index.js:6369, server/src/join.js:739, server/src/join.js:755
- Entity: a leaving player's `playerState[id]` and its entry in `this._lingerBodies` (Map id -> {until, session})
- State lives in: `ps.lingering`, `ps._lingerUntil`, `ps._lingerZ` (linger.js:85-87), `this._lingerBodies` (linger.js:93-94, 126, 164), `ps.disconnected` (linger.js:130), the fight clock `_pvpHealClocks` (cooking.js, read linger.js:62)
- States: playing, lingering (standing, hittable, no socket), lingering-and-dying, resumed (taken back by a near join), gone (saved, player_leave)
- Transitions:
  - close within PVP_HEAL.WINDOW_MS of a player hit, in the Wheel, not dying/dead/duel/spar, someone else in the room: playing -> lingering, guard dropped, velocity zeroed (linger.js:68-97, called index.js:6369)
  - tick sweep: time up, the switch thrown, or died and respawned (z changed): lingering -> gone (linger.js:107-120, tick.js:362)
  - join from within RESUME_PX in its zone, alive: lingering -> resumed, quiet end (linger.js:174-178)
  - join from elsewhere: refused, `linger_wait {ms}` + close 4010 (linger.js:181-182, join.js:740-746)
  - last session closes: every linger ended (index.js:6451-6453)
  - deploy: memory only, every linger lost (linger.js:31-32)
- Guards that read it: `_lingerOnClose` preconditions (linger.js:72-84), `_lingerOnJoin` (linger.js:163-182), getAllPlayerData and nearjoin include the body (index.js:6604-6605, nearjoin.js:172, 267), webSocketClose skips its leave steps while lingering (index.js:6374, 6393, 6444-6445)
- Counterpart/linked: the phone's LingerScreen and `_lgPending` (src/networking/wsClient.js:384-396, 1380-1388, 4609-4617); the 4010 collision (S1-3); the death machine (S1-2); No man's land (slice S5); lastplace.js (S2)
- Concerns:
  - [stuck] (high confidence, as an escape) The same-id eviction (join.js:755-773) removes the old session BEFORE closing it, so its webSocketClose finds no session and `_lingerOnClose` never runs; the linger check at join.js:739 sees no body because the old one is still live. Then join.js:876 builds a fresh state at the place the new join names, and lastplace.js takes a fresh page's "out of the Wheel" place as it is (lastplace.js:29-33). So a player losing a No man's land fight who opens a second tab (desktop tabs share one id: CLAUDE.md §5 Identity), or relaunches before the old socket's close reaches the worker, is in town with the bag at once. Evidence: join.js:739-773, 876; index.js:6358-6369. Docs: docs/specs/pvp-fights.md §6 promises "coming back any other way waits until the body has gone"; nothing mentions a takeover.
  - [authority] (high confidence) `_lingerOnClose` refuses a body with `ps.dead` (linger.js:73), the phone's own claim from its moves (movement.js:796; TRAPS §233): a modified phone sends one move with `dead: true` and closes, and the body leaves at once.
  - [persistence] (high confidence) The same takeover also skips webSocketClose's flush of the coalesced pools and HP (index.js:6385-6386, the v2.3.1619 "snapping backwards" fix), and the new join reads the character from storage (join.js:921, persistence.js:56), not from the live object it just replaced (join.js:876). HP above 25% and every pool are saved at most every REGEN_SAVE_MS, 10 s (persistence.js:186-227), so a takeover mid-fight gives back up to 10 s of damage taken and stamina spent (a free heal on top of the escape), and an honest phone whose half-open socket is superseded on its return loses up to 10 s of regen. Evidence: join.js:755-773 (only `_botfpFlush` runs for the old session), persistence.js:218-227.
  - [leak] (medium confidence, small) `_lingerEnd` repeats webSocketClose's leave steps by hand and has drifted: it never calls `_achvForget` (index.js:6419 has it, linger.js:125-154 does not), so each lingered player who does not come back keeps an `_achvCache` entry for the life of the worker (achievements.js:272-280); the next join reuses it as "the newest truth" (achievements.js:285-289).
  - [opacity] Two copies of "the player leaves" (index.js:6393-6420 and linger.js:125-154) plus a third partial one in persistence.js:534 for a reset; a new per-player cache has to be forgotten in each.
- Making it explicit: one `_playerLeaves(id, {quiet})` used by webSocketClose, `_lingerEnd` and the eviction; run the linger decision and the dirty-pool flush on the takeover too (the evicted session counts as a close), or let a takeover keep the live object instead of reloading it.
