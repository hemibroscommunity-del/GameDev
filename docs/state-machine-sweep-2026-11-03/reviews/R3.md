# R3 review · the owner's reality and completeness

- Overall: The diagnosis is sound and the Wave 1 fixes are well sourced. But the Thursday section looks only at code, and the cheapest, most valuable work before the expo is operational.
  - **The venue's one internet address.** Judges on one venue network share one address. That address may hold only 16 places and open only 20 sockets a minute (flood.js:76-77). The 17th judge reads "BroTown is full right now. Every spot in the world is taken" (RoomFullScreen.js:122-124), and that screen's Try now has V4's confirmed race. The room itself holds 120 (index.js:1045).
  - **Deploys during judging.** They have hurt the owner before: v2.3.1718 (buildWatch.js:2-4) records a judge who "didn't share the same world" after four client deploys in an hour.
  - **Shared expo computers.** They open V5's two-tab road and V15's double roster lookups against the address's 20 account checks a minute.
  - **Of T1-T5:**
    - keep T4: confirmed here, one line, judge-visible;
    - fix T1's proof, which cannot fail at 1366x768, and its scope;
    - make T2 a one-number hedge. The judges' Windows and Mac machines draw shirts through the 8-slot bake. This Linux QA box draws them through the look palette, so no desktop test has run the judges' path;
    - move T3 and T5.
  - **Add R6-5.** It is one token, and it can blank the world on the judges' quest path.
  - **The decision list.** It needs the expo decisions first, #12's map-card half moved up (it gates T1), and about a dozen missing questions.
  - **Wave 1.** Several items bundle three or four systems each, against the one-system rule. Three verified files' quick fixes (V2, V8b, V15) and VA's results have no home.
  - **Wave 3.** It should start as ratchet tests, not refactors.
