- Overall: Shrink the Thursday list to two client-only PRs: T1 with a proof that actually fails today, and T4 with the two phone tests that assume today's behaviour fixed in the same PR. Add the two things the plan does not have: an expo-day merge freeze, and a check of the per-address limits.   - W1.4's "refund: credit, then write" recreates the dupe that V8a shows on the offer-raise path.
