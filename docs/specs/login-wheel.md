# The login wheel (v2.3.4295)

Owner, 2026-11-05: "I added a 'free daily spin' you can see the gold amounts on the wheel ... Take the sound effect that has the largest sound wave in the file to use for the full spin. This will be once you login to the game for the day. One per account (one account meaning one account that holds 10 characters - not each character)."

- **Odds = the art.** Segment angles measured off `assets/ui/daily-spin/wheel-bulbs.png` (degrees clockwise from the top): 100 Gold 176.4 (49.0%), 200 82.8 (23.0%), 300 50.0 (13.9%), 500 25.8 (7.2%), 1,000 16.1 (4.5%), 10K 8.9 (2.5%). Average spin about 464 gold. Table: `server/src/loginspin.js` `LOGIN_SPIN.SEGS`, mirrored in `src/game/loginSpinWheel.js` (held together by `server/test/loginspin.test.mjs`).
- **Account** = the device id the join carries (a device holds up to ten characters); no device id falls back to the character. Record `lspin:<d:device>` `{day, pid, idx, coins, paid}` (v2.3.4393: + `nm`, `lv`, who spun, for the Leaderboards' row of a pot paid after they left: `docs/specs/leaderboards.md`). **Day** = UTC day (`dailyrewards.js dayOf`, the same as every daily thing).
- **Double or nothing** (owner, 2026-11-05: "Keep the double or nothing going until user chooses yes or no ... as many times as they want"): the spin's prize is a POT the worker holds in the record; `loginspin_double {k}` rolls a fair 50/50 (a win doubles, a loss empties it) and `loginspin_collect {k}` pays; `k` is the round index, so a double tap or replay is answered again, never rolled again. A pot another double would carry past 100,000 (the daily spin's `POT_MAX`) is paid by itself. An open pot is collected, never lost: 90 s idle, and at the next ask of any character of the account. Wire answers: `loginspin_result {idx, base, pot, k, open, can}`, `loginspin_round {win, lost, collected, replay, ...view}`.
- **Art/sound (double):** `public/ui/daily-spin/double-{wheel,pointer,plate,yes,no}.png` cut from the owner's mock-up and 50/50 wheel (`assets/ui/daily-spin/double-*.png`); `daily-double-win.mp3` (the slot machine) on a double, `daily-double-lose.mp3` (the buzzer) on nothing.
- **Order:** roll and stamp in one synchronous run, save, credit through `_creditPlayer` (opId `loginspin:<acct>:<day>`, source `dailyspin`), a pot is paid by clearing `open` (saved), then the credit, then `paid` (saved); a crash between leaves `paid:false` and the next ask pays it (same opId).
- **Wire:** client `loginspin_ask {}` -> `loginspin_state {avail, day}`; `loginspin_go {opId}` -> `loginspin_result {idx, coins}`. Both answers in `PRIVILEGED_EVENTS`; the asks in wsClient's send allowlist. Cap `caps.loginspin`; kill switch `loginspin: false` (live flags).
- **When:** `LoginSpinWindow` (mounted in GameApp) asks once the world is up (`loadingGate.worldReady`), opens when the worker says a spin waits and `onboardingPace.onboardingBusy()` is false.
- **Art/sound:** `public/ui/daily-spin/{wheel,pointer,title}.png` (256 colours; the pointer cut out as its own layer, its hole in the wheel filled from the rim); `public/sfx/loot/daily-spin.mp3` is 1.6-5.75 s of the owner's recording (its loudest, longest spin). Sources in `assets/ui/daily-spin/` and `assets/sfx-source/2026-11-05/`. `D915EA1B...png` (the plain rim) is kept as `wheel-plain.png`, unused.
- **Where (owner):** a FREE DAILY SPIN button on the top bar's right (the title plate scaled to the bar, a gentle glow, only while today's spin is unused; `ZoneHeader.jsx`), and the window large and centred. SPIN and YES work only on the Wheel's safe ground (the minimap plate's "safe": `wheelzone.js _wheelSafeAt`; NO always collects); elsewhere the button reads "Spin in a safe area". The worker enforces it (`loginspin_state {safe:false}`), the client only explains.
- **Tests:** `server/test/loginspin.test.mjs`, `node tools/qa/mp/run.mjs loginspin`, UI review state `login-spin`.

- **The feel** (owner: "it's supposed to be a dopamine hit"): a wind-up (the wheel eases back 10 deg), a fast launch, 7 turns and a 4.4 s strong ease-out, the pointer flicking as each divider passes (a Web Animation), a small overshoot and settle, then a glow, rays, a burst of coins (10 to 56 by the prize; 10K is a shower), and the gold popping and counting up before it flies to the Gear tab. Double or nothing: a drum-roll pause (0.75 s) before the little wheel, the slot machine and a gold glow on a double, a red flash, a shake and the buzzer on nothing. "Exactly 50/50 every spin" is always on that window (the worker's roll is `rand < 0.5`, no streak, no bias). Transforms and Web Animations only, at most 60 coins each removed when it ends, nothing running after the window closes; `prefers-reduced-motion`: no wind-up, flicks, coins or dimming.
- **Lights dimming and brightening** (owner: the lit and plain wheels): each wheel is the lit picture over its plain-rim twin (`wheel-plain.png`, `double-wheel-plain.png`), turning together, the lit one fading 1 -> 0 -> 1 every 2.6 s (a 1.3 s ease each way). The plain double wheel (a white-backed picture) is cut with the lit one's alpha; it is not quite the same drawing (the artist re-drew it), so the pair is lined up by the rim, not pixel for pixel. Under reduced motion only the lit one shows. All ten pictures are 256 colours and decoded only while the window is open.
