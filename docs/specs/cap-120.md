# The player cap is 120 (v2.3.3205)

> Owner, 2026-10-13: *"I think 120 is a good cap limit for now."*

`MAX_PLAYERS` in `server/src/index.js` goes from 60 to 120. The live flag
`max_players` can still only lower it (`_roomCap()`, `join.js`), so the
operator can hold the room to fewer without a deploy. The chat moderation's
ring of recent speakers is twice the cap (`CHATMOD.SEEN_MAX` 120 -> 240).

## Why 120 holds

**The room's thread.** With #887-#891 (near searches by squares, the paced
tick, steady movers, the safe ground's move diet) the load test
(`tools/qa/load/load.mjs`, the real runtime, phone-shaped bots, 30% fighting)
keeps the room smooth -- 33 ticks a second or more -- to about 145 players:

| Players | 100 | 120 | 125 | 150 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ticks a second | 41.3 | 39.0 | 38.8 | 32.2-33.8 |
| the thread busy | 71% | 80% | 83% | 93-95% |

(120 from the memory run below, with the debugger attached; the others from
the night's ramp, `docs/specs/move-diet.md`.)

**Its memory.** Cloudflare holds each Durable Object to 128 MB, and a room
over it is reset -- everyone in it disconnected. `tools/qa/load/heapprobe.mjs`
reads the room's JavaScript heap through the local runtime's debugger while
the load test runs (every 15 s, a full garbage collection every other sample):

| Players | Kept after a full GC | At the most in use between GCs | Reserved by V8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 60 | 6.6-7.4 MB | 38 MB | up to 54 MB |
| 120 | 8.3-9.4 MB | 44 MB | up to 88 MB |
| 150 | 8.9 MB | 56 MB | up to 92 MB |

What the room really keeps is under 10 MB; the rest is garbage between
collections, which V8 clears sooner as it nears its limit. Not counted here:
the runtime's own buffers for each socket's compression (about 300 KB a
socket for zlib, ~36 MB at 120), which live outside the JavaScript heap.

**The phones.** A phone's download was the old reason for 60 (about 4 KB/s per
moving player near you); that was counted unzipped, and on the wire it is
about a thirteenth (v2.3.3199): at 120 players about 12 KB/s.

## v2.3.3240: the 2 s look relay was missing from these numbers

The load test's bots never sent `track`, a real phone's whole look every 2 s.
The worker relayed every one to every other player as a message of its own.
With it counted (`load.mjs --track`), the room stopped being smooth at about
105 players: 25.8 ticks a second at 120. So the 120 above held only for bots.

`docs/specs/look-relay.md` relays a look only when it changes. With the looks
counted, that is 38.0 ticks a second at 120 and 31.1 at 150. So 120 holds once
that change is live.

## What 120 does not change

- One room is still the whole world. Past ~145 players it slows (fewer ticks a
  second); more players need the world split across several rooms
  (`docs/WORLD-ARCHITECTURE.md` §11).
- A full room still answers `room_full` and the phone retries every 5 s
  (`docs/specs/room-full.md`).

## Tests

`server/test/roomfull.test.mjs` checks the cap under test is the shipped one
(120) and that a flag can never raise it.
