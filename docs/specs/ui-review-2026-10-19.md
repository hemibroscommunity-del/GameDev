# The first ChatGPT review, checked and fixed (v2.3.3481)

The owner, 2026-10-19, sending ChatGPT's notes from the review kit
(docs/specs/ui-review.md, v2.3.3460): "Here are the notes from the review
session." With them came 13 sheets of icons ChatGPT drew, "Most of them are
not useful, but there may be icons that represent an improvement or get added
where no previous icon existed."

## How the notes were read

ChatGPT answered in 11 chats, one per packet of the kit (p01 the door and the
creator, through p11 harvesting and fighting). Its notes were about 180
findings, plus its views on each section's "Still open" items.

None was acted on as written. Each was checked against the code on main first,
one helper per two packets, each with its evidence (file and line), a verdict,
the exact fix, the tests that read the words and a size:

| Verdict | Count | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| REAL | about 100 | true on today's main: fixed here |
| NEEDS OWNER | 44 (some the same item in two packets) | a design choice, a feature or a trade-off: asked, not done |
| FALSE | 9 | the screen was right (often a control's bigger invisible ring the automatic check had missed) |
| CAPTURE ARTIFACT | 9 | the screenshot tool's mistake, not the game's (fixed in the tool where it could be) |
| DECIDED | 9 | already an owner decision in docs/UI-DESIGN-NOTES.md, working as designed |
| ALREADY FIXED | 1 | fixed on main after the screenshots were taken |

What this round taught about using ChatGPT this way:

- **It reads a screenshot well and a rule badly.** Several of its fixes said
  something new and untrue, for example that the Bank protects worn items
  (not for a red skull a player kills), or that the jackpot takes a 10% cut
  (the worker takes none). The copy here is the code's rule, not ChatGPT's
  wording.
- **Half its "small target" findings were already fixed.** The kit's
  automatic check had measured the drawn button, not its invisible tap ring
  (fixed in v2.3.3460).
- **The screenshot tool caused some findings.** The tutorial's GEAR UP card
  showed on screens where the tool meant to skip the tutorial. The bag was
  photographed from the Auction House's landing, five across. A laptop shot
  showed the skills overview, not the skill. Those are mended in
  `tools/qa/ui-review/states.mjs` (below).

## The owner's decisions in this round

- **"Remove cookhouse fish game."** The Cookhouse's timing bar (COOK! in the
  green), its raw-fish rows and its burnt-fish sound are gone. That game was
  the page's own: the worker never knew the fish was cooked. One line says
  where fish are cooked now: at a campfire lit from a log in the bag, which
  the worker settles. Meals, brews and the cooked-fish Eat rows are unchanged.
- **"Do woodworking."** A bow's or a staff's hardening is gated on
  Woodworking, `floor(level / 5) >= the weapon's tier index`, and a sword's
  stays on Blacksmithing (server/src/hardening.js, `hardenIsWood`). The
  worker answers "Need Woodworking Lv N for this tier". The Woodworker's
  hardening line says the same against `caps.hardenwoodskill`. Kill switch
  `hardenwoodskill: false` puts everything back on Blacksmithing.
- **"Achievement points are something I'd like in the game ... an arena winner
  should be included in that."** The arena always promised its champion
  "500 AP", and no worker ever paid them. Now gladiator.js `_arenaCrown` pays
  `ARENA.CHAMPION_AP` 500 in the same credit as the gold (payload `ap`;
  inbox.js adds it to `achievementPoints` in the payment's one write) and puts
  `ap` on the champion's record. The arena window, the champion box, the Post
  Office and the chat name the AP only where it was paid (`caps.arenaap`,
  `champion.ap`, the credit's `ap`). Kill switch `arenaap: false`. A wider plan
  for achievements is a separate question for the owner.
- **"Keep blur."** The dialogue backdrop's 2 px blur stays, the one exception
  to the no-blur rule (docs/UI-DESIGN-NOTES.md).
- **Icons.** From the sheets, the owner picked land scenes for all eight land
  banners, the arena's helmet, the trade request's handshake, gold quest pins,
  the jackpot's sack of gold, the Pet House, the prize wheel on the free daily
  spin and the treasure map on an empty Journey (docs/specs/owner-icons.md).
  They kept today's world-map compass. The leg armour waits on their answer.
  Gems have no picture yet: ChatGPT's eight did not follow the game's
  shapes, so docs/ART-WISHLIST.md "Gems" has a prompt that does.
