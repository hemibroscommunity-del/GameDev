## 1. Body parts flashing under the armour

### How a body under armour is drawn

The body under the armour is not the plain body. Each frame is baked: the
plain body trimmed so nothing of it pokes out round the plate and greaves
(the masked body, `_maskedBodyCache`, baked in a worker). A frame the worker
has not baked yet is drawn with the plain body under the armour for a frame
or two (v2.3.2904's choice, entityRenderer `_localMaskedFrame`). That is the
flash: skin at the plate's edges and trousers under the greaves, there and
gone.

So the loading screen bakes every frame you can be drawn in before it lifts
(`prewarmMaskedBodyFrames`). Two kinds of frame were missed.

### a. A drawn player's west, northwest and southeast

West, northwest and southeast are drawn by flipping east, northeast and
southwest. A player with any drawing or pattern (a tattoo, a print on the
trousers, a pattern on the trousers or shoes) is drawn there from a body
picture of its own, with the drawing put on flipped so it reads the right way
round (`localBodyArt(true)`, v2.3.1940). The body pictures themselves were
baked behind the loading screen (playerSkins `preloadBodyAll`), but the
masked frames are keyed on the body picture, and both prewarm passes baked
only the unflipped one.

So every frame of a drawn, armoured player turned west, northwest or
southeast was baked the first time it came up, and flashed. Every session,
logged in fresh or not.

### b. The first running frames of a full set

A full set's running frames are baked with the chain belt (maskedBake.js).
The belt is not one of the gear slots the loading screen loads, so its sheet
loads the first time something asks for it. A frame baked before it arrives
goes out without its belt and is not kept (`beltPending`), and the prewarm
never baked it again. On the test phone that was the first two frames of the
northeast run.

### What changed

- **Both prewarm passes bake the flipped body too**, for a drawn player in the
  three flipped facings (entityRenderer `_prewarmArts`). The loading bar counts
  them (`planPrewarmProgress`). `?flipbake=0` is the game as it was.
- **A full set waits for the belt's five sheets before it is baked**
  (gearSheets.js `preloadBeltSheets`, through the new `gearSheetLoad`, which
  can also wait for a sheet another caller started). Only with both pieces on,
  or a set one equip away that has both: restored to 256 px frames, the five
  sheets are about 32 MB of canvases a player who never wears both does not
  need.

## 2. The cook's frames during the gesture

Each gesture cycle (a flick of the pan up and the settle back) played the
cook's whole 24-frame loop. That loop holds about five tosses of the pan.
At the quick pace the gesture is built around (500 ms a cycle) that was 21 ms
a frame, three times the 60 ms the same strip plays at in the wind-up before
it, and faster still for a quicker hand.

Now a cycle plays a third of the loop and the cycles walk on through it
(gesturePose.js `gestureLoopFrame`, `COOK_FLICKS_PER_LOOP` 3): at the quick
pace that is 62 ms a frame, the strip's own speed. A slower hand is slower,
as the owner asked in v2.3.2760 ("animate at the speed you perform the
gesture"). The wind-up and other players' cooks are unchanged.

## 3. The whirlwind's catch, in its colour

v2.3.3499 marked each monster the wind-up will catch with rings on the ground
at its feet, which the monster's own body stands over. Now its whole body is
drawn in the whirlwind's amber too, flashing:

- the monster's own picture is drawn again over it in ONE COLOUR, each
  pixel's light and shade kept (its brightness, lifted so a dark monster still
  comes out a full colour), at 0.9: a caught monster is an amber one you still
  recognise, its own colours a tenth;
- it flashes by brightness: from the ring's own amber (0xff9a3c) to a light
  gold (0xffcc5c) and back, so it reads amber at every moment of the flash;
- a flash every 400 ms as the wind-up starts, every 200 ms as the strike
  comes: about three and a half in the one-second wind-up;
- the monster's own tint (a land's stage colour, its hit flash) is untouched
  underneath; while its red hit flash is on, the amber is a third as strong,
  so the hit still shows.

### Not a tint

The first cut multiplied each caught monster's tint toward the amber. A tint
can only take colour away: a blue slime came out a dark olive, and a red fire
goblin stayed red. The second cut drew a flat amber shape over the monster,
fading between 0.3 and 0.8. At its weakest a blue slime read grey (blue mixed
toward orange passes through grey), and at its strongest it was a flat blob
with nothing inside.

So the amber is drawn by a sprite program of its own
(`src/rendering/fillBatch.js`): Pixi's own sprite program, with each pixel's
colour replaced by its brightness before the sprite's tint paints it. The
sprite uses the monster's own frame, so no picture is made, and nothing is
loaded. It is drawn in the frame's own pass (no filter: TRAPS §221), and its
program is built behind the loading screen. `?fill=0` leaves it out (the rings
stay).

effectsRenderer works out who is caught, as before, and leaves it on the state
(`S._whirlCatch`); entityRenderer draws the amber over each caught body on the
next frame (`_whirlGlow`, src/game/whirlCatch.js). The rings stay.

Measured (`mp-whirltint`, the monster drawn by the game's own renderer): a
blue slime is 0% amber as itself, 100% of its body amber in the flash's
trough and at its peak, about half its shading kept; a fire goblin 41-44% as
itself, 100% caught. Pictures: `whirltint-close.png` and
`whirltint-close-ember.png` (each monster as itself, in the trough, at the
peak), and `whirltint-windup[-ember].png` in the world.

## 4. The Points window's last point

The window's count started at 1. With one point left it was already at its
most, so [+] was greyed: the last point could only be spent with Spend, past
a [+] that looked switched off.

Now the count starts at 0 and [+] adds each point, the last one too. Spend
says "Add points with +" until something is added, then "Spend n points". At 0
the window still shows what one point buys. After a spend the window stays, as
since v2.3.3522, its count back at 0.

### The per-stat cap (a question, not changed)

**Removed in v2.3.3578**, the owner's answer: "Remove stat cap." See
docs/specs/no-stat-cap.md.

Each stat can hold at most **your level** in points, and the four damage stats
(Power, Luck, Special, Element) **twice your level**
(server/src/prog3.js `_handleProg3Allocate`, `min(cap, level x lvlBound)`; the
phone greys [+] at the same number).

- It came with the points system as "§6-C": you can go defence-first, but
  you cannot outrun your level.
- When stats moved onto a curve (v2.3.2680), the owner said of Defense, Dodge
  and Resist: "keep per level limit on those 3". The damage stats got 2x.
- The curve already makes each point worth a little less than the one before,
  so piling everything into one stat pays less a point. The cap is a second
  guard against the same thing.
- What it stops, on a level 10 character's 27 shared points: all 27 in Dodge
  is a 71% dodge against monsters at your level. The cap holds it to 10 points,
  53%. Dodge and Defense together never let less than 10% of a hit through
  either way.

Options for the owner: remove it (the curve and the 10% floor keep any one
stat from breaking the game), or one rule for every stat, twice your level.
Nothing is changed until the owner picks.

## 5. The Goo Grotto behind a tree?

The owner's screenshot from earlier the same day (the one of the missing head
and legs) was taken in BroTown. Its minimap shows a landmark dot north-west of
the bro, at the end of a path, and on screen in that direction there is only a
big apple tree.

That dot is not the Goo Grotto. It is **Prospector's Circle**, the commons'
landmark (plan.js: "a ring of eight weathered standing stones on a grassy
knoll"). It is on the maps and has a path to it, but nothing has been drawn
for it yet, so the spot is empty grass.

The Goo Grotto is out in the Verdant Wilds, at the end of their first
stretch. Nothing stands over its mouth: test-world-core holds every dungeon's
mouth clear of every object and resource (placing.js `MOUTH_CLEAR`), and the
phone's picture shows it in the open (tools/qa/mp/out/spot-grotto-*.png).
