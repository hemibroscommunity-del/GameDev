# The menus batch of the 2026-10-19 playtest (v2.3.3489)

The owner played the game on their phone on 2026-10-19 and sent a list of
notes. This is the "menus" part of that list: eight notes about the touch
buttons, speech bubbles, the quest log, the life skills page, the maps, the
shop windows and the Points window. Each section below says what the owner
wrote, what was wrong, what changed, and which tests check it.

Other parts of the same list (sounds, the Blacksmith, combat feel, jumping,
targeting and traps, death effects, land auras) are separate pieces of work
and are not touched here.

## 1. The Block button's picture, and Whirlwind closer to Special

The owner: "Block button needs a new icon. Whirlwind action button needs to
be placed closer to melee special attack (too far away)."

**Block.** The button drew the 64 px shield sprite from the world, stretched
to the button's size: blurry, and not one of the owner's ability pictures
like the other buttons. It now wears the owner's round gold shield, cut from
their UI sheet (`assets/icons-source/owner-2026-10-19/brotown-rpg-ui-asset-sheet-1.png`,
the box 293,856 to 416,983) by the house cutting tool
(`tools/ui/cut-owner-icons.py`, a new `disc` piece that trims the shield
to its round face), 160 px like every ability picture, with a baked grey
copy for while it waits (`public/ui/controls/abil-block.webp`,
`abil-block-grey.webp`). Never a CSS filter on a control (TRAPS §42). Both
pictures load on the loading screen with the other ability pictures
(`ABILITY_ART_URLS` in `src/ui/panels/controlSkin.jsx`), so the button never
appears empty.

**Whirlwind.** Special and Whirlwind sat on a diagonal "half a button" apart
(v2.3.3106), the owner's earlier ask "enough space between them for not
accidentally pressing the other one". The diagonal stays; only the distance
changed: their centres are now 1.2 buttons apart (`RCTL_PAIR_AIR` 0.2 in
`src/ui/panels/ShieldButton.jsx`):

| | before | now |
|---|---|---|
| upright (390 x 844) | 77 px between centres | 57 px, 9 px of air between the rings |
| sideways (844 x 390) | 86 px | 64 px, 10 px of air |

The left thumb's cluster shares a different number (`LCTL_THUMB_FRAC`), which
did not change. Every button stays on the screen and clear of the others
(`mp-btnlayout`'s clearance matrix, which measures this pair by its own
rule: more than 8 px of air between the rings, and never overlapping).

Tests: `mp-abilslot` (126 passed), `mp-btnskin` and `mp-btnlayout` (410
passed), `server/test/small-icons.test.mjs` (23: the picture is the owner's,
160 px, warmed, and the old stretched shield is gone from the button).

## 2. Townsfolk speak in white bubbles, players in dark ones

The owner: "Make Npc dialog boxes white. Keep players the darker color to
differentiate."

It is Halloween week (`src/game/season.js`), so every speech bubble was the
pumpkin-purple Halloween bubble, townsfolk and players alike, and outside the
week every bubble was white. Now the colour says who is talking
(`src/game/bubbleSkins.js`, read by `effectsRenderer.js`'s bubbles):

- a townsperson's bubble (its key starts `npc:`) is always the white one with
  black words;
- a player's bubble is dark with light words: the Halloween purple during
  Halloween week, and a Lantern Slate slate the rest of the year (the raised
  `#2B3940` into the panel `#202C32`, words `#F7F2E7`, a faint light edge).

Tests: `server/test/season.test.mjs` §5 (whose bubble is whose, both seasons,
30 passed), `mp-halloween` (both phones: a player's bubble purple, the
townsfolk's white), `mp-chatbubble`, `mp-chatfont` (10 passed in the week and
with `?halloween=0`; it now finds your own bubble by your id and measures its
words on either colour). `docs/specs/halloween.md` says the same.

## 3. Track a quest, and the gold star follows it

The owner: "I tapped track quest in quest menu but no golden stars were in
minimap (I had two active quests - one from beast master and the other from
mayor bro)."

Three things were wrong:

1. **Tracking changed nothing on the maps.** "Track this quest" wrote one
   setting that only the quest list read, to sort itself. The minimap's star,
   the world map's gold road and the pinned quest card never asked.
2. **Most of the two givers' quests led nowhere.** The star only knew three
   kinds of place: a land ("Bring 4 Snowman Remnants from Frost Ridge"), a
   resource (the fishing, the ore) and a hand-in. Beastmaster Bro's four
   quests and Mayor Bro's "Visit 3 buildings", "Kill 5 monsters" and "Clear
   any dungeon" were none of those, so with any two of them active there was
   no star at all: exactly the owner's case.
3. **A quest with nowhere to go hid the one behind it.** The old code stopped
   at the first "do it anywhere" quest and answered "nothing" if it named no
   resource, so "Spring 5 traps", taken before "Bring 5 Ore", blanked the
   ore's road too.

What changed:

- `src/game/trackedQuest.js` holds the tracked quest for everyone who reads
  it (kept in memory, as the minimap asks every frame; another tab's change
  is heard). The quest page's Track button writes it, as before.
- `src/game/questWays.js` says where each quest leads. Each quest (or each
  step of a quest with steps) has a **way** in the quest table
  (`src/data/gameSystems.js`, beside `zone` and `node`):
  - Box Traps, and Set and Spring's first step (carry a trap): the
    Woodworker's door, where traps are made;
  - Set and Spring's other steps, A Trapper's Eye, Your Own Beast, and the
    Mayor's Into the Wild (kill 5): where the nearest land's monsters begin
    (its level 1s, the same spot a quest naming a land leads to);
  - Welcome Home (visit 3 buildings): the nearest building with a door you
    have not been through (the halls and the shut Hotel do not count, as the
    quest does not count them);
  - Dungeon Delver: the Land Office's door (your farm, and its Dungeon
    Workshop, are behind it);
  - a quest ready to hand in: its giver, as before.
- The order: the tracked quest first; then, as before, a quest ready to hand
  in or one naming a land; then the rest in the order they were taken. The
  first one with somewhere to go here is the star; "you are standing where it
  leads" ends the road (no star, and not the next quest's either, which would
  read as this one's). A quest with nowhere to go right now (lighting a fire,
  cooking) passes to the next.
- The pinned quest card (top left) shows the tracked quest, and the quest's
  page says "Your map's gold star follows this quest." once tracked.
- Off the Wheel (the farm, a dungeon's arena) only a giver standing there and
  the resources lead anywhere, as before.

Tests: `server/test/quest-route.test.mjs` (52: the storage, the order, every
live quest has a way at every step but the few done anywhere, every written
way means something, the lands on both Wheels, the owner's pair step by step,
the wiring); `mp-trackquest` (a phone with the owner's pair: a star with
nothing tracked, the real taps on Track moving the star, the pinned card and
the world map's road, untracking, and the road ending at the door).

## 4. Life skills as app tiles

The owner: "Make the lifeskills menu look more like iPhone apps instead of
elongated pills."

The page was ten pills, two to a row. It is now a home screen: five across in
two rows (the five gathering skills over the five crafting ones, the order
the page has always kept), each skill a 52 px rounded-square tile tinted by
its own colour with its painted icon big in it, its name under it on one line,
its level on a chip at the tile's corner, a thin green XP bar along the
tile's bottom edge, and the dashboard's brass dot on a skill with a level-up
you have not looked at yet. A press shrinks the tile, as a phone's home screen
does. A tap opens the skill's own page, as before.

This reverses the owner's own earlier ask, "make the expanded view pill shaped
so they all fit without scrolling" (v2.3.1312b). Its other half still holds:
upright, all ten are on one screen with no scrolling. Sideways the column is
narrow, so the tiles go three across and the column scrolls.

Tests: `mp-skillapps` (the grid at 390 and 360 px upright, the dot, a tap
and back, sideways), `mp-hitladder` and `mp-xpfade` (they open skills by
their tiles).

## 5. In town the world map opens on the town

The owner: "When you're in town make the expanded map zoomed in on the town
so you can see which building is labeled where on the map."

The world map always opened on the whole Wheel, where BroTown is a dot and
its buildings are only named past zoom 6; even the ◎ button stopped short of
the names. Now, opened while you stand in BroTown, it opens on the town: every
building's door on the screen with room round it, each named, wearing its
picture (item 7), at the zoom the names come in or closer. A name that would
run into its neighbour's is tried on two lines ("Auction" over "House"). The
buildings are placed before your own mark, so a name keeps its room. Anywhere
else the map opens on the whole Wheel, as before, and ◎ in town brings the
town's view back.

Tests: `mp-wheelmap` (opened in town: all the town's buildings on the screen,
named and wearing their pictures; then zoomed out to the whole Wheel for the
rest of its walk; ◎ in town).

## 6. One big close button on every shop window

The owner: "Vendor menus need a more prominent escape icon in the corner."

A building window's ✕ was a faint 28 px disc (white at 10%, a 60% grey
cross). Worse, it scrolled away with long menus (the forge, the Woodworker,
the Gem Works), leaving no way out on screen. Diego's was a 30 px rounded
square and Ace's a bare grey "×": three windows, three different ✕s.

Now there is one, `.bt-xclose` (`src/styles/game.css`): a 40 px dark disc,
near-opaque so it reads on any room picture, a light ring, a bold warm-white
✕, red while pressed (Lantern Slate's destructive colours), and a 44 px press.
On a building window it sits in a bar of no height at the top of the card that
is sticky, so the ✕ stays in the corner while the menu scrolls under it. The
names the game finds it by are kept: `.bt-inspect-close` (Esc, the close
sound, the computer's key cap), `data-shop-close`, `data-esc`. The Gem
Works' tab strip keeps 52 px clear for it where it lies over the strip (was
44).

Tests: `mp-buildingrooms` (21 passed: "the close button on top" on every
window, upright and sideways), `mp-shopkeeper` (46 passed: Diego's ✕ shuts
the drawer, upright and sideways), `mp-deskkeys` (Esc).

## 7. Each building's picture on the maps

The owner: "Have icons representing each building on each building in the
minimap/compass."

The minimap drew each building as a roof-coloured footprint with a notch at
its door: where the buildings stand, not which is which. Each now wears the
picture its Enter button already wears (`WHEEL_BUILDING_ICONS` in
`src/data/wheelBuildingDoors.js`: the forge's anvil, the Woodworker's saw,
the Saloon's mug, the Bank's vault...), 14 px on its footprint, a shut one in
grey; and on the world map, up close, the same picture at each door over its
name, in place of the little drawn house.

The Land Office had borrowed the Feed & Seed's barn on its Enter button; two
barns on a map say nothing, so it wears the game's parchment map now, on its
Enter button too. The shut Hotel wears the owner's bed (the right stick's
sleep picture), grey.

Memory (CLAUDE.md "Memory is budgeted"): the sixteen pictures are drawn down
to 64 px canvases as they load, behind the Wheel's own loading screen
(`src/rendering/wheelBuildingIcons.js`, from `preloadZoneAssets('wheel')`),
and the full-size images are dropped: 17 canvases (the Hotel's grey copy
included) of 16 KB, about 0.27 MB, and the same again once on the graphics
chip as the minimap's textures, about half a MB in all, only while you are in
the Wheel. Leaving the Wheel takes the minimap's sprites off them first and
then lets them all go. The minimap builds its pictures once, with the town, so
nothing is redrawn a frame.

Tests: `tools/world/test-world-core.mjs` (every plot of the town has its
picture, a file on disk, none shared; each the Enter button's but the Land
Office's; drawn on both maps, loaded and freed with the Wheel), `mp-wheelmap`
(every footprint wears its picture at arrival; the pictures let go on leaving
the Wheel).

## 8. The Points window stays after Spend

The owner: "After spending a stat allocation point keep it on the menu
confirmation window instead of closing it to return to the points main
screen."

Spend closed the window at once, before the server had answered. Now
(`src/ui/mobile/InfoPopup.jsx`, `src/ui/mobile/sheet/HeroExpanded.jsx`):

- the window stays; Spend reads "Spending…" and takes no second tap, and the
  stepper waits;
- when the server has spent every point sent, the same window is put up again
  with the stat's new value, the next point's before-and-after, the stepper
  back at 1, and "✓ 3 points spent" in green;
- with no points left, the button shows the reason it always did;
- if the server has not spent them all within 3 s, the window comes back with
  whatever did land (and the button by itself a beat later), so it can never
  stick on "Spending…";
- closing the window meanwhile simply forgets the wait.

Tests: `mp-catgrid` (268 passed: every spend answered and its "✓ n points
spent" read, the window closed by its ✕ afterwards).
