# Ace standing still, the copper shoulder, and the flicker coming back (v2.3.3512)

Three of the owner's notes after the 2026-10-19 playtest:

> Ace is stuck in one walking frame (and his shadow beneath him is apart from
> him).
>
> East jog armour shoulder on the copper torso (it was solved for iron torso).
> Fix it for all future tiers.
>
> Flicker of hair/torso after returning/joining.

The land trees' effects and the land emblems from the same notes are in their
own PR (v2.3.3511).

## 1. A townsperson who stands, stands

The Wheel's townsfolk never walk (`pathRadius 0`), and a still walker was drawn
on frame 0 of his walk strip (entityRenderer `_updateNPCs`).

- **Ace's frame 0** is the most extreme mid-stride pose of the video his strip
  was cut from: body leaning, coat flying, legs splayed, and both feet off the
  ground. His lowest drawn row is 214, against the feet line at row 222.
- **His shadow** is cast from the sprite's anchor at the feet line
  (lightfx/casters.js), so it lay a few pixels apart from the feet drawn above
  it.
- **Diego and Lil Bro** were planted on frame 0 but leaned off their spot. Diego
  stood 21 px right of where he is placed (in the strip's own pixels), Lil Bro
  11 px.

Now each walker's row in NPC_DATA names the frame it stands on (`walk.stand`).
These were measured on the strips: planted, feet together, head over the spot.

| Townsperson | Stands on |
|---|---|
| Ace | frame 3 |
| Lil Bro | frame 1 |
| Diego | frame 2 |

`_updateNPCs` draws a still walker on that frame (`npcStandFrame`), and so does
his first frame when he is made. His shadow comes from that same frame, so it
meets his boots. The breathing every still townsperson already has
(worldLife.js `_updateBreathing`) keeps him from being a statue.

## 2. The armour rides the arm capsule: the copper shoulder, every tier

Every metal shares one art set: the steel plate and greaves, tinted
(gearVariants.js). So iron was never "solved" in the art. An iron torso **with
iron greaves** jogs as the full-set knight figure, whose body texture is
the armour itself. Every other outfit jogs layered: a copper torso with other
greaves, a torso alone, black steel over copper greaves, and so on.

The arm capsule (v2.3.200) runs on every east jog with a weapon out. It
re-draws a clone of the body from the shoulder to the hand over everything
else, so the arm covers a slung shield.

- **In the layered figure the body is the masked bake.** Its head-and-shoulder
  band is the whole bare body, normally hidden under the plate.
- **So the clone stamped the bare shoulder and upper arm over the plate.**
  That was dozens to hundreds of skin pixels every frame (a torso alone: bands
  across the whole plate).
- **The shirt (v2.3.2134) and the cape (v2.3.2138) had already learnt to ride
  along** over the clone. The plate and the greaves never did.

Now they do, the same way. `_handArmLegs` and `_handArmChest` are clones of the
**live** pieces, carrying their texture, place, scale, rotation, anchor, tint
and alpha. They are drawn through the same capsule and stacked body → shirt →
greaves → plate → cape, the order the real layers are drawn in.

- **No table to keep:** a clone copies whatever the live piece looks like, so
  every metal there is, and every one added later, is covered.
- **The knight figure draws no plate layer,** so its clones stay hidden.
- **The sheen and grade rim apply to the clones too** (glint.js `self:c` /
  `self:l`), so the clone of a rare plate shines blue like the plate under it.

On the way, three loose ends were tidied:

- **Every clone hides together** (`_hideArmClones`). The shield's and the
  empty hand's branches hid the body clone alone, so the shirt's and cape's
  clones could linger for a frame as a stripe after a shield came up mid east
  jog.
- **The death keep-lists hold every clone's mask.** They listed the body
  clone's mask alone, so a death hid the shirt's and cape's masks and nothing
  ever showed them again.
- **The figure's shadow is unchanged.** Each clone is the same picture in the
  same place as the piece under it, so its shadow piece lands exactly on that
  piece's own. The body's, shirt's and cape's clones have always cast the
  same way.

## 3. The flicker after coming back

The owner: "flicker of hair/torso after returning/joining".

**What was measured** (`mp-joinflicker`). The character has an auburn afro and
a beard in its saved look, and a copper torso and iron greaves the worker holds
as worn. It stands for 3 s, then jogs east:

- **The ordinary road** (a reload, Continue, your row, the loading screen):
  2 of 62 frames drew the plain body under the armour. No layer blinked or
  swapped back.
- **After a renderer rebuild** (an iPhone's lost graphics context) and **after
  a tab switch**: 0.
- **The straight-in road** (the black-screen watchdog's recovery reload, or a
  row in the picker that switches keys): 26 of 58 frames drew the plain body,
  and the body swapped back to the plain picture twice within 400 ms. That is
  the flicker. This road has no loading screen: the world went up while the
  armour's masks were still being baked, behind a small spinner.

**What changed.**

- **The straight-in road waits behind a veil of its own** until the bro's
  pictures are made (`src/game/avatarHold.js`, from BroTown's auto-rejoin,
  `AVATAR_HOLD_CAP_MS` 20 s at the most):
  - It waits for the same preload the loading screen waits for on the ordinary
    road (`preloadPlayerAssets`: the body, the gear, the masks, the hair).
  - It is its own element, not the shared zone overlay. Six callers put that one
    away without knowing about each other, and any of them would have lifted
    this too soon. Its class (`.bt-zone-loading`) is what the black-screen
    watchdog already reads as "veiled".
  - `S._avatarArtHold` holds the bro still under it, as the other holds do.
  - The cap is the loading screen's own on the same preload (IntroVideo's
    `hardCap`). `?avatarholdms=` (1-180 s) lengthens it for a slow test
    machine, where the preload outlasts it: there the veil came down on the
    cap and 3 frames after it were still plain, against 26 with no veil.
- **The loading screen waits for your hair, hat and beard colours** and the
  hat's hair-clip shapes (entityRenderer `preloadTraits`; `coloredHairReady`,
  `coloredHatReady`, `coloredFacialHairReady`, the hair mask's `ready`). They
  were made the first time they were drawn, so the first frames showed the
  picture's own colour.

**What the first cut of the test got wrong.** It dressed the bro with
`window.__btGearSet`, which sets the drawn layer alone. The worker's first full
player_state took the plate off again (the layer follows the worn armour,
`syncArmorLayers`), so the reload started bare. Every "plain frame after
joining" it counted (23 of 59) was the first bake after the test put the plate
back on. Now:
- the admin kit mints the pieces and they are worn through the equip lane
  (`stats_update` by id);
- the look is in the creator's stores before the character is made, so the
  record keeps it.

**Not changed: the 2 frames on the ordinary road.** A frame the worker has not
baked yet draws the plain body under the armour for a frame. That is
v2.3.2904's choice: the right pose, missing only the mask's trim, rather than
the last frame's body under armour that has moved on.

The two are this box's. The loading screen lifts on its own 20 s cap when the
bro's pictures take longer, and here they take minutes. Each step of the
preload is stamped (`window.__btPreloadSteps`), and on the recovery road it
read:

| Step | Done after |
|---|---|
| downloads | 15.6 s |
| the worn set's masks | 58.6 s |
| the sets one equip away (v2.3.2872's owned-gear prebake: the kit's copper and iron pieces give three) | 155.8 s |
| uploads, done | 162 s |

Each bake is a few ms in a phone's worker and about a hundred times that in
this box's software rendering. Nothing hung. The veil waited for "done" and lifted on
`ready`, and nothing after it was plain.

## Tests

- **`mp-armcapsule` (new).** One held frame of an east jog with a greatsword
  out, extracted three ways:
  - with the capsule's clones, as drawn;
  - without any clones;
  - without the armour's clones (the code before this version).

  It is run for:
  - a. a copper torso alone;
  - b. a copper torso with iron greaves;
  - c. black steel over copper greaves;
  - d. the full iron set.

  It counts the bare **skin** each way lays over the figure. The capsule still
  changes other pixels in every outfit, the knight's included: the arm lying
  over what is behind it, which is its job.
  - Without the armour's clones: 49-233 skin pixels a frame.
  - With them: 0-15, the hand.
  - The knight: no armour clone made, and the clones change nothing.

  `BT_ARMCAP_DUMP=1` writes the three pictures side by side.
- **`mp-wheelfolk` 4b.** Ace, Lil Bro and Diego stand on their stand frames
  (Ace 3).
- **`mp-joinflicker` (new).** Watches each layer of your figure for blinks and
  swaps back, and counts the frames drawn plain under the armour
  (`window.__btLocalMisses()` names each one and its body picture). Four roads:
  - a reload into the world;
  - a renderer rebuild;
  - a tab switch;
  - the recovery reload, watched from when the bro's veil comes down. Its veil
    must be seen and must come down because the pictures are made (the test
    opens with `?avatarholdms=170000`), and nothing after it may be plain.

  It also says whether the loading screen of the ordinary road lifted before
  the bro's pictures were made (its 20 s cap, on this box), from
  `window.__btAvatarReadyAt` and `S.__introLiftedAt`.

Client only, so it is safe to deploy in either order.
