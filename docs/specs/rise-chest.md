# The Lagoon Islet is open to everyone, and its chest pays (v2.3.4438)

> The owner, 2026-11-09: *"I'm thinking of how I can make elevation changes in
> brotown. ... The character has a 'double jump' feature and I think it would
> be cool to have some elevation and obstacle areas where that can be put to
> use."* Asked where the first public obstacle area goes and what its prize
> is, 2026-11-10: *"Go recommended."* And then: *"This codebase is yours: what
> it's for and how it's built are your call."*

The recommendation was the order in `docs/ELEVATION-PLAN.md`: the Lagoon Islet
first (it was built in v2.3.3994, behind `?climb=1`), its chest a real prize,
then a jump course near BroTown.

## What a player sees

- **The Lagoon Islet's climb is there for everyone.** Off the Water Caves'
  land, through the shallows: a terrace you climb by its vines (or clear with
  a sprinting jump), and a taller ledge that only the flip (the double jump)
  reaches.
- **The chest on the ledge pays 300 coins, once a character.** Walk into it
  (or press E / tap it): "+300 coins!". A second touch says "Already opened".
- Too far from it (the worker's word): "Step up to the chest".
- `?climb=0` in the address lays no rises for that one phone (the way back,
  as a memory saver's is).

## How it is built

- **For everyone**: `public/tools/world/core/ground-worker.js` `climbOn` is
  true unless the address says `climb=0` (off, false). Nothing else read the
  address; everything downstream asks `wheelRises()`. The faces are painted
  in code and the climb uses the jog's frames, so nothing new is loaded. The
  long Wheel only: the short one has no islet.
- **The worker's table**: `server/src/wheelfaces.js`, generated by
  `tools/world/bake-wheel-spawns.mjs` (`wheelFacesSource`) from the plan's
  `rises`: each rise's box, face, vines and chest in game px, the boxes the
  phone is sent. `test-world-core` fails when it is stale.
- **The prize** (`server/src/risechest.js`, a mixin):
  - `rise_chest_open {id}` from the phone; `rise_chest {id, ok: true, coins}`
    or `{id, ok: false, why}` back (PRIVILEGED; the phone's direct switch).
  - `why`: `off` (the switch, or a Wheel with no rises), `none` (no such
    chest), `far` (your feet are not at it), `got` (this character has had it).
  - **Where**: the worker's own copy of your place, plus the 52 px from the
    body to the boots, must be on the chest's rise (its box less its face, 12
    px of slack) and within 66 px of the chest's box (the phone's own 36, and
    30 for the copy being a move behind). In the Wheel, alive.
  - **Once**: `_creditPlayer` with the op id `risechest:<pid>:<rise id>`. The
    stamp (`oplog:`) and the coins go in one run, so a retry, a second tab or
    a worker that has forgotten everything all answer `got`. No new storage
    key.
  - The amount is `RISE_CHEST.COINS` by the rise's id. It is a number every
    player reads over the chest, so it is not a hidden setting.
- **Cap and switch**: `caps.risechest` (said only on the long Wheel); the
  phone asks only a worker that says it, and otherwise the chest says "Empty
  for now" as before, so either side may ship first. `risechest: false` in
  the live flags un-advertises the cap and answers `off`.
- **The phone**: `src/game/climbActions.js` `openRiseChest` (the ask),
  `onRiseChest` (the answer, said over the chest; a chest had is remembered
  for the session so the next touch does not ask). `src/game/climb.js`
  `CHEST_WORDS`, `chestPrizeNote`.

## What the worker checks, and what it does not

It checks **where you are** and **that it is once**. It does **not** check
that you jumped.

`ELEVATION-PLAN.md`'s "(a) the worker learns the faces" was a movement clip: a
move into a face refused unless a jump or a climb carried it. Reading the code
for it found two things:

1. **The worker never moves a phone back.** A move past its budget is
   followed, never refused (`movement.js`, TRAPS §203), and the phone does not
   read its own place from the tick. So a clip only holds the worker's COPY
   behind a face while the phone walks on, and the one it would leave behind
   is an honest player whose jump relay was dropped (the relay bucket drops
   silently when empty).
2. **The jump is the phone's word.** `player_jump` carries its own time,
   height and flip. An app changed enough to walk through a face can say it
   jumped.

So a clip would cost honest players and stop nobody who meant it. The prize is
small and once; the place and the once are what a worker can hold, and it
holds them. **When a rise goes where monsters fight** (a ledge nothing can
reach you on), the copy's place matters to the fight, and that is when the
clip is worth building. It is not built.

## Costs

- A chest opened: one credit (the save and the stamp, one run). Standing and
  climbing write nothing.
- Bytes: about 400 more in the ground worker's first message to every phone
  on the long Wheel (the rises); the server's table is under 1 KB.
- Memory: no line raised. Nothing is loaded for it.
- This merge deploys the worker (it adds server files).

## Not built

- The movement clip (above).

  words say it).
- A jump course near BroTown (the next step), BroTown on a hill, diving.
- Monsters on the islet: none, as decided for v1.
