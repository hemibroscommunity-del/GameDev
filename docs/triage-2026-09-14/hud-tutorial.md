## Q1(b) Lock-on rule (KNOWN, from src/game/targeting.js, 520 lines)

- `targetCandidates(S)` = alive tangible monsters within TARGET_PERIMETER_PX (220px, from src/data) of player, nearest-first -> `S._targetCands` (only when `autoAcquires(S)` i.e. melee).

For MELEE, `tapStealable()` (targeting.js ~"v2.3.2263"): after TAP_PIN_MS=900ms, if cands[0] is a different monster and best.d2 <= curD2*0.88^2, the tap lock FALLS THROUGH to auto rule and is re-pointed at the nearest -> THIS is where "tap on a farther monster" gets overridden (owner's own v2.3.2263 directive "go by the nearest monster").
- So Q1(b) "tap to lock onto a farther monster even when hero is closer to another" = for melee the tap IS honored for 900ms then stolen by nearest. Ranged/staff: honoured indefinitely. Fix = logic change in tapStealable (e.g. drop steal for tap-owned locks, or raise TAP_PIN_MS, or only steal when tapped monster leaves hysteresis ring) — conflicts with v2.3.2263 directive; owner must reconcile.
- `S._engaged`/`S._engagedId` (v2.3.2285) = melee "I picked a fight" intent, anchored to tapped monster id, survives lock moving.
- `engagedStance(S)` = S.autoAttack || tap lock. `S._lockDroppedWhy` in {'gone','dead','range','weapon'}; `S._lockDroppedAt`.
