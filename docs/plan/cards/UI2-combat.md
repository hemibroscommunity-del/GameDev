## The facts (the audit's, with their lines: read the line before building on it)

- **THE POINTS RULE IS THE GAME'S, NOT THE DOCUMENT'S.** The specification's part 4.2 says every weapon level-up gives 3 points for that weapon and 3 shared points. The game: a weapon's level-up gives its 3 points (`src/data/prog3.js`:29-31); SHARED points come with the CHARACTER level, which is the best weapon's (one level, v2.3.3538: `src/data/prog3.js`:667; dealt by the character level, `server/src/prog3.js`:16, :663), so they are earned only when the BEST weapon levels; and a weapon's points may also be spent on body stats (points remember the skill that earned them, v2.3.2176: `src/data/prog3.js`:735). The pools: `src/data/prog3.js`:690-790. By the owner's rule the game's stands: the screens show the worker's counts and never say or imply "3 shared points a weapon level". (`SHARED_POINTS_PER_LEVEL`, :35, is the level banner's number from before one level: read what the banner says today before reusing its words.)
- Combat level: the best of the three weapon levels (`src/data/prog3.js`:556, :1127), unless the live flag `onelevel` is off. A weapon's level cap is 100 (:26).
- The names: six a weapon, Range, Power, Speed, Luck, Special, Element; seven shared, Max HP, Defense, Max Mana, Stamina, Dodge, Speed (movement), Resist (elemental only). Two vocabularies today: Melee, Bow, Magic on the points screen; Sword, Bow, Staff on the card.
- The preview EXISTS WHOLE and is kept: the Build section of `sheet/HeroExpanded.jsx` (3,539 lines; four lanes), `InfoPopup.jsx` (595), `sheet/statPreview.js`:161-216, `sheet/StatDemo.jsx`, `statSim.js`, the action at `src/game/actions.js`:266. - DPS is one total: `calcDisplayDps`, `src/data/gameSystems.js`:5839-5882, read by `sheet/broStats.js` (:58, :99: thirteen stats as finished strings). Its parts: the hit's range (`calcCombatDmgRange`, :5804-5823), critical chance (:5859), critical multiplier (:5861), the flat critical part (:5864), a critical hit (:5880). Average hit = (least + most) / 2; a critical hit = the greater of average x multiplier and most x 2, plus the flat part; DPS = (average + chance x (critical hit - average)) / seconds a swing / 5. Element is not in it. No breakdown is shown today (`sheet/HeroExpanded.jsx`:1837 says so).
- **No effective-health number exists anywhere in the code.** A monster's hit meets block, dodge, defense, then armour (`server/src/combat.js`:431 on).
- Where weapon XP (now, and needed for the next level) is held was not cited by the audit: find it by `src/data/prog3.js`'s level reader before the page shows it.

## The readings, chosen so you do not guess

1. **The hub: three equal kit tiles, Sword, Bow, Staff, each with its level, its XP bar and its points' count; under them one wide tile, Shared Attributes, with the shared count.** ONE vocabulary: Sword, Bow, Staff. A pool's words come from ONE rule (`combatHub.js`): "n points" for the count the worker holds, 0 included; nothing about how points are earned on the hub.
2. **A weapon's page, in the screen's zones**: level and XP (a continuous bar; now and needed); DPS as the headline (it LOOKS tappable: the kit's stat headline); the supporting stats under it, subordinate (damage, attack speed, critical chance, range: the game's own strings from `broStats.js`); then "Attributes" with the weapon's points available, and the six as kit tiles in two rows of three. No permanent preview, no plus buttons, no weapon selector, no tab strip: Back goes to the hub.
3. **Tapping an attribute opens today's preview, unchanged** (hosted, not rewritten), with zero points too (it then reads, and Spend stays greyed with its reason). `InfoPopup.jsx` gains only a read-only mode (for step 9: no stepper, no Spend).
4. **A weapon's roadmap**: its level, its XP and one true line of what a level gives, in the game's own words, read from the rule; no empty milestones (a weapon's level unlocks nothing with a name).
5. **The stat tree (PR 2), one layer at a time**: DPS, then its branches (the average hit; what critical hits add; the seconds a swing takes; the display's scale), each a row that opens ITS factors, down to a leaf that names its source (a base value, placed points, a worn item: a leaf that is an item is a door to that item, the item page from step 5 when it exists, today's item card until then). The operator that joins a layer is shown in words (a contribution is not always a sum). `calcDisplayDps` is changed ONLY to hand back its parts with its total, from the same lines: never a second sum.
6. **The shared page**: the same shape. Its headline is **Max HP, with Defense beside it**, numbers the game has: NO effective-health number ships (flagged, F1); the headline's place is built so that number can take it on the owner's word. Its seven attributes open the same preview. The shared count is shown as the worker holds it.
7. **Weapon points spent on body stats** is the game's rule (the notes' table 1, line 1). Whatever the preview offers today is offered; this card neither adds nor removes a way to spend, and it words each pool so a player sees which count a Spend will draw on BEFORE pressing (the preview's own line; if it does not say it today, add the line from the rule).
8. **"Attack speed" and "Move speed"** where both stats called Speed could be read on one screen.
