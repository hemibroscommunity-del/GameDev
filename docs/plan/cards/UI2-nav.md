## What the player sees (structure only; the game's slate and pictures as they are)

- **The row of CLOSE and six buttons becomes one header, the same 52 px.** At Gear's root: `Close | Gear | Menu >`. At the Menu's root: `< Gear | Menu | Close`. On any page under the Menu: `< (where Back goes, in words) | the page's name | Menu | Close` (the small Menu button is the owner's decision, 14 §2.1: it goes to the Menu's ROOT and leaves no page under it, so Back from there goes to Gear; opening the Menu from play, by the closed band's button or a key, RESUMES the last page instead).
- **The Menu's root is a grid of eight tiles**, each the picture its button or More tile has today and one word: Bro, Quests, Social, Ranks, Codex, Homestead, Map, Settings (question 1). Four across and two down upright and on a computer; one row of eight sideways (78 px of page there). Equal weight: no tile bigger than another.
- **Every page reachable today is reachable**: Party and Guilds from Social; Pets, Life Skills and Achievements from Bro's own doors as today; Journey from Bro; Account, Notifications, Feedback and About from Settings; Map opens the World map overlay as More's tile does. Guilds keep a door inside Social (they are not at its front once phase 7 builds the network, 14 §5.1). Homestead opens a small page saying what the Bro row says today (plots ready, where the farm is) and nothing more: a place kept for it with an honest line, never a feature shown as playable (14 §2.1, empty and locked states). List every one of More's fifteen in the PR body with its new road.
- **Closed, the band keeps its handle and a row of two buttons**, Gear and Menu (question 2). Gear opens Gear where you left it; Menu opens the page you were last on, with its scroll and selection.
- **Back goes where you came from, to the same spot.** A page you return to shows the list position or the selected thing you left (14 §2.1). From a quest opened by the card on the world, Back closes to the world's Gear or Menu state you were in, not to the Quests list you never saw. Back's words name its target.
- **A computer**: I Gear, O Menu, and P, J, U, K, L, M open their pages as today (pressing again closes); a tile wears its key (`data-key`); Close takes `data-esc`.

## How it is built (finish.md part 5, rule 5)

- `src/game/dashNav.js`: ONE state (which workspace is up; each workspace's own stack; hidden or shown) and ONE transition function (open Gear, open Menu, go to a page, Back, Close, a shortcut's arrival). A stack entry holds its page's id, its kept scroll and selection, and two fields later phases read and this card only keeps and tests: `who` (whose page it is: none means yours; phase 5 sets it, and the header then names the owner, the addendum's question 46) and `from` (where the page was opened from: a tile's cell in its grid, a tap's point, or none; phase 1b's motion grows from it).
