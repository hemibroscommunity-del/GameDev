## What the player sees (structure only; the game's slate and pictures as they are)

- **The row of CLOSE and six buttons becomes one header, the same 52 px.** At Gear's root: `Close | Gear | Menu >`. At the Menu's root: `< Gear | Menu | Close`. On any page under the Menu: `< (where Back goes, in words) | the page's name | Menu | Close` (the small Menu button is question 2's pick: it goes to the Menu's root).
- **The Menu's root is a grid of eight tiles**, each the picture its button or More tile has today and one word: Bro, Quests, Social, Ranks, Codex, Homestead, Map, Settings (question 1). Four across and two down upright and on a computer; one row of eight sideways (78 px of page there). Equal weight: no tile bigger than another.
- **Every page reachable today is reachable**: Party and Guilds from Social; Pets, Life Skills and Achievements from Bro's own doors as today; Journey from Bro; Account, Notifications, Feedback and About from Settings; Map opens the World map overlay as More's tile does. Homestead opens a small page saying what the Bro row says today (plots ready, where the farm is) and nothing more. List every one of More's fifteen in the PR body with its new road.
- **Closed, the band keeps its handle and a row of two buttons**, Gear and Menu (question 2). Gear opens Gear where you left it; Menu opens the page you were last on, with its scroll and selection.
- **Back goes where you came from.** From a quest opened by the card on the world, Back closes to the world's Gear or Menu state you were in, not to the Quests list you never saw. Back's words name its target.
- **A computer**: I Gear, O Menu, and P, J, U, K, L, M open their pages as today (pressing again closes); a tile wears its key (`data-key`); Close takes `data-esc`.

## How it is built (finish.md part 5, rule 5)

- `src/game/dashNav.js`: ONE state (which workspace is up; each workspace's own stack; hidden or shown) and ONE transition function (open Gear, open Menu, go to a page, Back, Close, a shortcut's arrival). A stack entry holds its page's id and its kept scroll and selection. No imports. `src/game/dashDests.js`: the destinations' table (id, word, picture, its place in the Menu, which workspace), which later phases add rows to.
- `server/test/dash-nav.test.mjs`: what it does, not how it is spelled. THE DODGES it must catch, each planted and seen red (name the plants in the PR body): Back goes to the category's parent instead of the page you came from; Close empties a stack; opening Gear clears the Menu's stack (or the reverse); a shortcut to the page already on top pushes it twice; the same page twice in one journey shares one scroll; resume lands on a page the worker no longer offers (a cap gone) instead of falling to the Menu's root; Back at the Menu's root does nothing instead of going to Gear.
- `src/ui/mobile/dashboardPanelBus.js` becomes a thin face over `dashNav` (`open`, `push`, `pop`, `toBar`, `state` keep their meaning for every caller and for the 44 phone tests that drive `window.__broDashPanelBus`). `src/ui/mobile/BottomDashboard.jsx` gains calls and loses lines (say the count before and after).
