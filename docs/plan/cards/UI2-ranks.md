## The readings, chosen so you do not guess

1. **The landing is three equal group tiles**, under the one header: **Combat**, **Life Skills**, **Feats**, each with the picture of Bro's door of that name (step 3's), in that order. No Equipment tile (no board exists). A group the worker keeps nothing of is left out.
2. **A group is its boards as tiles** (the board's own picture and word, from today's lists): Combat holds Overall, Sword, Bow, Staff (the boards Melee, Bow, Magic, under the game's one vocabulary, notes line 9), Combat level, and one door **Points placed** that opens the eight stat boards; Life Skills holds the ten; Feats holds Kills, AP, Daily spin, Coin flip, Biggest fish, as the worker keeps them. A board no group names goes under Feats, last (none is dropped). Upright every group fits whole (at most ten tiles, five across); sideways one row.
3. **A board is a standings list** under a header with the board's name: the heads Rank | Player | the metric with its unit ("Level", "Power", "Points", "Kills", "AP", the spin's and flip's gold, the fish's weight, each the word today's board uses). A row: the place (the game's gold, silver, bronze medal for 1-3, the number below), the face and name, the one value. NO Lv chip except on Combat level, where the level IS the value. No bars, no gradients, no laurels: subdued rows, at least 44 px each; the list scrolls inside its zone. A board with its own choice today (spin's and flip's sides, the fish's species) keeps it in the board's top zone.
4. **Your standing**: pinned at the foot ONLY while your real row is out of view (worked out from the kept scroll, the row's pitch and the list's kept size: a rule, never a measure in a tap or a frame); never a second copy when it shows. Not on the board: one quiet line at the foot, "Not ranked on this board" (a new say key). Loading: the kit's placeholder, never "nobody is on it" before the answer.
5. **A tap on a row opens that player's Character page** (step 3's passport, its other-player form, the entry's `who` = `{ id, name, look }`), named as theirs, read-only, and fed ONLY by what the game holds of them: the board's row, through a new reader `passportFromRow(row)` in `ranksPage.js` (the name, the face, the three weapon levels from `series`, AP, the clan tag, kills). A line the row does not carry says "Not shared" (never your value, never "No title equipped" for a title the game was not told), the combat level only from a field verified above, and no door whose page reads only your state (the weapon doors, Titles, Journey: they come with step 9). Your own row opens your own Character page. A deleted character's row is not a door (it says so). A records row with no fields beyond a name opens the page with the name and "Not shared" lines.
6. **Back returns to the same board at the same place** (the board on the entry's `sel`, the scroll the frame keeps); the Menu resumes the board last open. The fish road lands on Biggest fish at that species, under Feats.
