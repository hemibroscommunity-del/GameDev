## The readings, chosen so you do not guess

1. **One shell.** `housing.png` is the only backing: the stick's lobe at the left, a low rail in the middle, the taller lobe at the right, its gold outline unbroken. Part 1's two grips (`PadHousings.jsx`'s left and right housings) go. The shell spans the play column's width, bottom edge ON the dashboard's top edge, band open or folded, and moves with it; the band's height and the world's view are unchanged. So it fits any width with the outline whole, draw it as three cuts of the one picture (left lobe, a middle that stretches, right lobe), never a squeezed whole.
2. **Sizes from `layout.json`**, scaled together to the width: at a 430 px phone the shell is about 131 px tall, Attack and Jump about 65 px, the three small wells about 37 px, the weapon wells about 41 px, the stick's base about 83 px. That is the owner's "too large" answered: do not scale it up. TOUCH BOXES are not the art: every control keeps a touch box of at least 44 x 44 CSS px, none overlapping another, none smaller than its art; where today's box was larger than can fit, say the new size in the PR's words. Only real touch boxes take a touch: the shell's empty space lets the world's taps through.
3. **The wells** (the mockup): left, the stick (base still, the thumb piece moved by today's stick code: its behaviour unchanged; in this design the base is ALWAYS drawn, the thumb at its middle at rest). Middle rail, THREE WEAPON WELLS in today's order with today's selection rules (`weapon_slot_selected.png` on the one in hand, the weapon's icon inside; an empty slot the plain well): they REPLACE the weapon pill while the controller is on. Right lobe, top row left to right: the shared mobility well (Skate where the skateboard is offered, Charge where Charge is offered, the game's own rules, never both: as part 1), then the middle well, then Special. Bottom row: the big left button, and Attack (its face by the weapon in hand: `attack_sword`, `attack_bow`, `attack_staff`; where an attack is not allowed, the dimmed face: `attack_sword_disabled.png`, the same dimming for the other two by CSS).
4. **Jump becomes the shield in a fight** (the owner's own words; the art's notes put the shield in the top row: the owner's words win). OUT of a fight: the big left button is Jump and the top row's middle well is empty. IN a fight (exactly when today's rules show Block: `src/game/controlVisibility.js`, do not write a second rule): the big left button wears the shield's face (`shield.png`, drawn at the big size) and IS Block; Jump moves to the top row's middle well, small, so nothing the player could do is lost. (Chosen by the orchestrator: the owner did not say where Jump goes; told to them as "chosen for you".)
5. **The shield is a tap, up and down.** A tap on the shield button raises the guard and it STAYS up with no finger on it; a tap on it again lowers it; a tap on Attack lowers it and attacks. Everything else that lowers a guard today still does (energy gone, a guard break, death, riding, the fight ending: then the button is Jump again). The page sends the worker exactly what a held Block sends today and stops sending it when the guard comes down: the worker's rules (the 3 s after a break, the 400 ms before a parry is ready, the energy a tick) are untouched and stay the judge. The raised state shows on the button (the art's "active" ring: a quiet ring, opacity only). This is ONE phase in one field with one transition function (CLAUDE.md §5), a module under `src/game/` with a suite that kills its mutants (plants: a second tap not lowering; Attack not lowering; the guard still shown up after the worker broke it; the button staying a shield after the fight ends; a held finger needed).
6. **States** (CSS, as part 1): pressed (the face about 0.96, 100 ms), cooldown (the dark arc over the face, never the rim), unavailable (dimmed), a control the game hides right now: its EMPTY WELL (`action_socket.png`). No number on a cooldown (the game has none: part 1's list).
7. **The switch.** `?padskin=0` still gives today's controls back, whole (the pill, a held Block, Jump always Jump). The shield-as-a-tap and the Jump swap live only with the controller on. No new switch unless the tap-shield needs its own way back: then `?tapshield=0` (Block held, in the top row's middle well, Jump always big), its row in the switch table.
8. **Art law.** The 18 pieces go through `public/tools/style/process.js`, 256 colours at most each, at the size they are drawn (2 px a CSS px is enough: the big pieces are far larger than needed, cut them down), loaded behind the loading screen (PRELOADING IS LAW), part 1's pieces that are no longer drawn REMOVED from the repo and the preload list (memory: freed, not hidden). Aim under 200 kB in all; `mp-membudget`'s lines do not rise.
9. **Upright, sideways, a computer.** Upright as the mockup. Sideways: the same one shell along the band's top edge, the middle stretched, the lobes at their ends; if it cannot fit a short sideways phone without covering the minimap or the top bar, keep the controls in their wells and say what gave. A computer: unchanged (it has no touch controls).
