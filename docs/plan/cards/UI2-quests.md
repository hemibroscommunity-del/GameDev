## The readings, chosen so you do not guess

1. **The landing is your active quests as rows**, under the one header ("Quests"): a row is the giver's face, the quest's name, its count ("0/4") where it counts, a ready mark when it can be handed in, and the tracked star; the tracked quest first (the journal's own order, `activeOrder`). The rows are a collection and scroll inside their zone by intent. Under them, never behind the scroll, a row of doors: **Daily** (only when the game shows it today), **Completed (n)**, and **On offer (n)** when the journal lists offers (today's picker's second half: nothing is lost). No tabs, no page marks, no picker.
2. **Empty**: no quest in hand shows the kit's `EmptyState` with the game's own line (`questsPanel.none`) and the same doors; never a blank page.
3. **A row opens the quest's own page**, the page id `questDetail` (its header word is the game's, "Quest"), the quest's id on the entry's `sel` (and `questDetailBus` as today). Its zones: identity (the giver's face, the quest's name, its state); the journey, done steps first (a tick and their words, what the giver handed over), then **NOW, dominant**: the thing's picture, the words, the count and its bar, the place; then one quiet line, "The adventure continues" (a new say key). Steps still to come are NOT drawn, the hand-in is NOT drawn until the quest is ready (then it IS the now), and no "Then:" line, no step count, no percent. The done steps scroll inside their zone and open on NOW (`journeyFocus`); NOW and the actions never sit behind a scroll.
4. **Its actions, in the `ActionRow`**: Track (in hand only; immediate, no confirmation, no pending mark: it is the phone's own) and **Show on map** (only when `where.map`; absent otherwise, never shown disabled for a quest the game cannot map). Rewards is a door (raised), not an action.
5. **Rewards** is its own page (`questRewards`, a `FRAME_PAGES` row): the completion rewards as the game's tiles (`rewardTiles`). **Story is left out** (no text exists; the notes' line 17): no door, no "coming soon".
6. **A completed quest's page** (from Completed) is its whole history: every step done, the hand-in done, its rewards door, and the journal's own "Next: ..." line (what to do NOW after it, not the future of this quest). No Track, no Show on map.
7. **An offer's page** (from On offer): NOW is the talk to its giver, Show on map where the game maps it; nothing past the talk.
8. **Daily** and **Completed** are frame pages (`questsDaily`, `questsDone`) drawing today's `DailyPane` and `DonePane` under the header (Completed's row opens that quest's page). Back follows the history and restores the landing's scroll (the frame's).
9. **The pinned card's road** lands on the quest's own page with its way home: Back says World and closes the dashboard (mp-ui2frame 8's reading, kept). `questDetail` leaves `HANDS_OVER` (it is a real page now: a push from it must not replace it); `dash-nav.test` 3h is rewritten to that and its plant kept red.
10. **Sideways and on a computer** the same pages; sideways (63 px) the landing's doors sit at the rows' end and the quest page's actions beside NOW; your judgment, said in the PR with its picture.
