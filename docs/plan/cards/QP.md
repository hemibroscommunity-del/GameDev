## The pages (an upright phone)

1. **The quest** (opens first; mockup 01): the row Active (n) | Daily | Completed; the quest's head (the giver's portrait, its name, "IN PROGRESS" and the giver's name, Track with its star lit when tracked); **what to do now** (the target's picture, "Collect 4 Slime Remains", the bar and "0/4"); **where** (the pin, "Earth Forest", a Map button that opens the World map as today's road to it does); **Rewards** in one row of tiles (gold, AP, the items with their pictures).
2. **Quest journey** (mockup 02): the same head, then the quest's steps as a line: done steps green with their tick and what they gave, the step in hand gold with its count, the steps to come grey.
3. **Rewards and what is next** (mockup 03): the head with the count, "Completion rewards" as tiles, "Next quest" (its picture, name, giver and place, a lock and "Available after <this quest>").

Rules for what is on them:
- **Every word and number is the game's own**, from where today's Quests page and quest detail read them (`src/ui/mobile/sheet/questModel.js`, `questJournalModel.js`, the quest tables, `src/ui/mobile/dash/QuestsPanel.jsx`, `QuestDetailPanel.jsx`). A piece of a mockup the page cannot know today WITHOUT a server change (a step's "Received" items, "West of town", the next quest) is drawn only where the client's own tables already say it; otherwise it is left out and LISTED in the PR body as a follow-up. No server file, no cap, no invented text.
- **Nothing of today's is lost**: every quest today's page lists (Active, Available, Done, Daily) is reachable, with several active quests you pick one (the mockup shows one: a compact picker, a row of chips or a stepper over the head, the tracked quest first); Track, the hand-in and the roads that open a quest by name (the top-left tracker's "Open quest", a townsperson's panel, the coach) land on that quest's page 1. Available quests and Completed show as today's lists inside their tab where a page design is not drawn: those lists may scroll.
- **Getting about**: three small page marks (Quest, Journey, Rewards) with the open one lit; a tap on the "what to do now" block opens the Journey, a tap on Rewards opens page 3. Taps only: no sideways swipe in this part.
- **Wide** (600 px and wider: a phone on its side, a tablet, a computer's band): the three pages stand side by side, each whole, and the page marks are not drawn.
- **No vertical scroll** on pages 1 and 3 at an upright iPhone's page (measure 390 x 844 and 375 x 667 as the phone tests size them) and sideways. The Journey is whole for a quest of up to four steps; a longer one scrolls with the step in hand in view when it opens. Say in the PR body what could not fit, with the picture.

## How it is built (finish.md part 5, rule 5; CLAUDE.md)

- The rules are a module under `src/game/` (`questPages.js`: the page is ONE field with one transition function; which quest is shown; the steps' states; what a tap opens), with a suite that tests what it does and kills every mutant of its lines or lists the survivor with why. The panels gain calls and lose lines; the pages are components under `src/ui/mobile/dash/` or `sheet/`.
- `?questpages=0` gives back today's Quests page (a trial becomes the default with its switch): its row in the switch table (`tools/guards/lib/switches.mjs`, `--write`).
- The same-change rows that apply: a screen (the `quests` and `dash-quest-detail` states of `tools/qa/ui-review/states.mjs` updated, the new pages added; the section "The quest log" of `docs/UI-DESIGN-NOTES.md`, about line 783, rewritten for the pages with the owner's words above; `data-esc` where a window closes); a phone test (`tools/qa/mp/mp-questpages.mjs`, `world: 'wheel'`, its `lane`: each page whole, `scrollHeight <= clientHeight`, upright and sideways; Track tracks; the Map button opens the map; a second active quest can be picked; the switch gives the old page back); a read of the page's layout only through `docs/specs/layout-reads.md`'s ways; `touch-action: manipulation`; words a computer reads through `deskWords()`.
- `mp-questjournal`, `mp-trackquest`, `mp-questguide` and the `playable` scenario (`mp-questline`, run on EVERY PR) are mended where the page's shape changed: `mp-questline` must pass on your box before the push.
- No new art: pictures are the game's (the givers' portraits, the monsters' and items' icons, the star, the pin, the lock: an icon the game lacks is drawn in CSS or left out, never a new file). `mp-membudget` runs on the PR; no line is raised.
- **Beside you**: card BRO (the Bro tab in pages) and card CT (the two buttons) are open. `tag-blocks.json`, `docs/UI-DESIGN-NOTES.md`, `states.mjs`, the switch table and its generated documents (`docs/SWITCHES.md`, `docs/SYSTEMS.md`) are theirs too: keep your lines in those files few and in your own section; when the orchestrator says one of them has landed, merge `main` once and write the generated tables again (`--write`), never by hand.
