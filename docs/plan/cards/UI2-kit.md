## The readings, chosen so you do not guess

1. **Tokens, one source.** A module with no imports (`src/game/ui2Tokens.js`): surfaces (the game's own eight colours are the base; the benchmark's richer blue-slate metal is a tuning of them, yours to judge on the fixture), the five action colours (gold routine, green accept, blue guide, red destroy, silver dismiss: LIGHT metallic faces, ONE dark label colour on all five), text (ivory on dark; muted labels), the rarity outlines by the GAME's grades and colours (Normal, Rare #5B99DE, Elite #E8893A, Godly's rainbow: never "common" or "epic"), radii, bevel depth, shadow, shine strength, spacing, the type scale, motion. The style sheet's custom properties are WRITTEN FROM that module (generated, held equal by a guard), under ONE scope class (say `.ui2`), so nothing that exists today changes its look.
2. **The pieces** (`src/ui/kit/`, each small): the dashboard's frame (shallow, a thin restrained gold rim, a fine low-contrast brushed slate by CSS); the header (Back with its label, the title, Menu, Close: raised controls on the slate, no title plate); a destination tile (raised; full-colour art over a readable label; primary and compact sizes); a recessed information group; a stat headline that can be tapped for its breakdown (it LOOKS tappable); the action button (five colours; full-width single, or a bottom row with a price or requirement beside it); the rarity slot (recessed, outline by grade, a non-colour cue too); the progress bar (a continuous metallic fill in a recessed track); the badge (a count, a dot, the rare warm glow); the disabled action with its reason beside it; the pending mark on a button; the "still loading" placeholder for a list.
3. **States**, all from tokens: default, pressed (momentary), SELECTED (deeply pushed in: inverted depth, shadow and lighting, never only a tint; the same footprint), focused (a visible ring: a computer's keyboard), disabled, loading. Static and interactive must read apart with the shine OFF.
4. **All chrome is code**: CSS gradients, borders, inset and drop shadows, pseudo-element highlights. NO picture file for a surface, a button, a rim or a texture; nothing cut from the references. No filter or backdrop-filter, no animation that runs while nothing changes, nothing measured inside a frame (CLAUDE.md §7; `docs/specs/layout-reads.md`). Shine is one token and `?ui2shine=0` turns it off (its row in the switch table); `prefers-reduced-motion` stops every loop.
5. **The serif.** Choose ONE free-licence serif (OFL, on Google Fonts as the game's other fonts are) that keeps the benchmark's feel and stays readable at 11 px with lining, tabular numerals; load it the way the game loads its fonts, only the weights used, behind the loading screen; its row in `CREDITS.md`. It is used ONLY inside `.ui2`. Say in the PR which you chose, which two you turned down and why. (Found by the audit: `src/index.html` asks for Raleway, which nothing uses: remove that request and say so.)
6. **Contrast is a test, not a hope.** A suite (`tools/guards/ui2-tokens.test.mjs`, not `server/test`: a file there deploys the worker) works out the contrast of the one dark label against every stop of every action colour's face, normal and pressed, and of ivory and muted text on every dark surface: 4.5 to 1 at least for ordinary text; and holds the least touch box (44), the least text (11; 10 for a count badge), and that the generated style sheet equals the module. It kills its mutants or lists the survivor with why.
7. **A page to see it** (the spec's "component preview/test fixture"): `?uikit=1` on a QA page (its row in the switch table; its state in `tools/qa/ui-review/states.mjs`; its section in `docs/UI-DESIGN-NOTES.md`) shows, INSIDE a box of the dashboard's real size (390 x 243: the 52 px header, the 191 px page): (a) every piece in every state; (b) TWO composed samples from the kit with the game's real pictures and the player's real data where a QA page has it: the Bro landing (six tiles: Character, Combat, Equipment, Life Skills, Feats, Pets, under one header; no banner) and the Character page (the passport: portrait, name, title, combat level, the three weapon levels, and what the game truly has of the rest; where the game has nothing (a clan, a guild profile), an honest empty line, never an invented value). These two are SAMPLES on the fixture, not the live screens: the live Bro tab is untouched in this PR.
8. Nothing the memory check reads rises (no art added). A computer's band (480 wide at most) shows the same pieces.
