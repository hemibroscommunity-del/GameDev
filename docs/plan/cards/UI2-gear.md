## The readings, chosen so you do not guess

1. **PR 1, the ONE item page** (`src/game/itemInspect.js`): identity (picture in the kit's rarity slot, name, grade by the GAME's names with its stars, whose it is and where: "In your bag", "Worn", "Diego's", another player's name); the facts that matter for its kind; doors to deeper facts; the actions its context allows, from `itemActions.js`, in the bottom `ActionRow` (an action that is off stays with its reason: "Sword level 18, requires level 25"). Read-only for another player's item. Gear's "Details" opens it first; nothing else moves yet.
2. **PR 2, two panels**: left INVENTORY (the storage grid, gold and the filter in its label row; it scrolls inside its panel, by intent); right EQUIPPED, the nine cells arranged like a body, not a grid: the three weapons down one side, Chest, Legs and Cape on the figure's line, Shield, Amulet and Gem at the other side (your judgment on the exact shape at 189 x 172: every cell at least 44 px, and the two panels told apart by SHAPE with the colours off). The game's names: Chest, Legs, Gem. No middle column.
3. **The health, mana and energy bars leave Gear with the middle column**: they are on the world's own screen already. Say so in the PR body with a picture that shows them there.
4. **The quick look replaces the OPPOSITE panel, never moves the source.** Tap a bag item: the Equipped panel becomes its summary. Tap a worn item: the Inventory panel becomes its summary. The picked slot is the kit's SELECTED state (pushed in, its footprint and rarity outline unchanged). A clear way back to management (tap the slot again, or the summary's close) that restores the panel and the bag's scroll.
5. **The summary, by kind.** Equippable: the comparison, ONE headline and at most two supporting rows, a plain-words line of the tradeoff where there is one, then `Details` and the one meaningful action (Equip, gold). A consumable, material, tool or fish: its own useful facts (what it restores, what it is used for, how many you hold) and its action (Eat, Drink, Open...), from the game's own tables: never only a name and a Details button.
6. **The comparison is the transformation form** (`src/game/compare.js`): two labelled subjects ("Now", "If equipped"), each row's value now, value compared and signed change; a percentage only where it means something (never from a zero baseline, never for a thing with no worn counterpart: then "Nothing worn" and the plain values); each row knows which direction is better (attack seconds: lower is better); unchanged reads "No change"; a restricted item says why; direction by a word and an arrow as well as colour. Weapons lead with DPS, by `calcDisplayDps` on YOUR character with that weapon (`src/game/equipProject.js`: you now and you if equipped, by the same reader as the Bro pages; never raw item numbers). Armour leads with **"Damage cut"**, the number the game has, until effective health is decided (F1). No item is called better or worse overall.
7. **The workspace is shared** (`src/game/workspace.js`; the kit gains `TwoPanel` and ONE grid of item cells): management, a look from the source panel, a look from the other, the full page: one state, one transition function. Step 8 calls it for the shop, the bank, a bench and a trade.
8. **PR 3**: Bro's Equipment door opens a SHOWCASE (the figure and the nine worn cells in the same body shape, no bag; each cell opens the one item page; "Manage Gear" for yourself, nothing to press for another player), and the other item screens (`ItemTooltip`, the Codex's drop card, the profile's equipment row) open the one item page. A screen that cannot move without its own step (a shop's goods) is LISTED in the PR body, not half-moved.
9. **Seeing it at once**: Equip shows immediately as today and the worker corrects it; the button carries the kit's pending mark while the request is out (step 3's `pending.js`; if step 3 is not on `main` yet, PR 2 keeps today's behaviour and says so).
