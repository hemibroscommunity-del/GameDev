The owner, 2026-11-26 (`docs/design/UI_FEEDBACK_2026-11-26.md` parts 4 and 5, read it WHOLE first): *"**Combat.** The three weapon cards have too much empty space and surprisingly small weapon icons. Make the weapons visually dominant, enlarge the level numbers and only show available points prominently when there are points to spend."* *"**Weapon attributes.** Currently the messiest menu. Eliminate the left-side Attributes column, consolidate the combat statistics into one summary strip, and give the six attributes a full-width three-by-two grid."* *"This is how I'd consolidate the Sword menu specifically, with Bow and Staff following the identical template."* Its mock-up: *"‹ Combat / Sword / Menu / Lv 23 / 4,393 / 7,333 XP / 120.5 DPS › / Damage 26–44 / Critical 38% / Speed 2.10/s / Range 2.9 / Attributes: 0 points available / Range 12 / Power 17 / Speed 11 / Luck 11 / Special 12 / Element 3"*; *"Same information, but with a much clearer progression from level → performance → attributes. When points become available, the attributes can receive a small gold plus indicator and the available points counter can become gold."* Their standing rule: *"keep my game content over the mockup stuff"*.

PURPOSE: Combat shows three big weapons with big levels, and points only when you have some; every weapon's page (and Shared) reads top to bottom as level, then how it fights, then what you can spend, in one template, with the six in view at once.
