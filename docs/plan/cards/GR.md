## The measures (each by STEPPED frames or the renderer's own clock, never wall time: this box draws a few frames a second, TRAPS §200; play a steady 60 by feeding the step the phone would get, 16.667 ms, if `frameClock.js` or a QA probe lets you; say how you did it)
Play the world (`world: 'wheel'`, BroTown; never the old map), the phone's shape (430x775, deviceScaleFactor 3, touch), jogging and then riding the board at a constant heading: east, north, and a diagonal.
- **M1, the camera's step.** For each drawn frame: the step, the player's place in the world, the camera's place in device px, and how many device px the view moved since the last frame. Report the pattern (for example 3,3,2,3) and its spread, for the default and for `?camsnap=0`, `?dtsmooth=0`, `?pace=0`. A whole-pixel camera at a speed that is not a whole number of device px a frame steps unevenly: is that the grit, and how big is it at jog speed and at ride speed?
- **M2, who sits where (the owner's own guess).** For each frame, the place on the screen, in device px, as placed AND as it lands on the pixel grid, of: a ground piece, a building, a tree or prop, a shadow, the bro, a townsperson, a monster. While the view slides at a constant speed, does each one's distance to the ground stay the same every frame? Name every layer whose distance changes (by a device px, or by a part of one under nearest sampling), with the file and line that places it (a `roundPixels`, a container at a fractional place, its own scale, a baked ground piece at another resolution, a snap that the others do not have).
- **M3, the pictures' own pixels.** Device px a picture px on this screen for the ground, buildings, props, the bro; the sampler of each (nearest or linear). Screenshot a building's edge and a prop at twelve camera places one device px apart: do the widths of a picture's pixels change from frame to frame (a crawl)? Find the `?zoom=` at which a picture px is exactly 1 device px on a 3x phone (and say what the view then shows: more or less of the world than today), repeat the twelve shots: is the crawl gone?
- **M4, the bro against the world.** The camera is on whole device px; is the bro's own place fractional, so that he trembles a pixel against the world at a constant speed? The same for the board under him and his shadow.
- **M5, the rubber band on the board.** How the camera follows (a lead, a lerp, a dead zone?), and the board's speed over a ride with the stick held full (the push and roll-off, `?ridecoast=0` to compare): the distance from the bro to the screen's middle, frame by frame. Does it swing, by how many device px, at what period?
