Almost every piece of information has its own large beveled button, even when it isn't an actionable or particularly important piece of information."* Part 3: *"**Navigation buttons**: Raised, beveled, prominent. Reserved for destinations and actions. **Important statistics**: Recessed surfaces with large numbers. No heavy beveling. **Supporting information**: Flat, aligned within shared containers. No individual heavy borders."* Part 4: *"reduce the button bevels and shadows, and use the same typography across all navigation buttons."* Part 6: the six colours (base #14232F, navigation #223D53, recessed #0E1D29, borders #4B6275, text #F1F0E9, gold #EAC474) and *"gold for progression and important numbers, and other colors only when they communicate something meaningful."*

## The readings, chosen so you do not guess

1. **RAISED** (`.u2-raised`: `Ui2Tile`, the header's controls, a `button` given the class): a destination or an action ONLY, always a `button` (or `role="button"`) with a handler.
2. **THE STAT** (`Ui2Stat`): recessed (the well, an inner shadow, no bevel, no drop shadow), the number large (`--ui2-fs-big`, gold where it is progression or the important number). With a handler it is a `button` and wears the chevron (*"120.5 DPS ›"*); without one it is a `div`, no chevron. A second size, `size="pill"`: one line, a picture and the number, radius pill (PR 5's gold). The kit page's pressed and selected demos move to the stat that opens.
3. **FACTS** (new: `Ui2Facts` the ONE container, a well; `Ui2Fact` a row: label muted left, value right, flat, no border, background or shadow of its own; rows aligned on one column). A row that opens is a `button` with the chevron, still flat (PR 3's strip). Exported from `kit/index.js`.
4. A caller's `className` is JOINED to the kit's in `Ui2Stat`, `Ui2Tile`, `Ui2Fact`, never replaces it (Group.jsx:23, Tile.jsx:43).
5. `ItemPage.jsx`:101's doors become the raised door form (`Ui2Tile size="compact"`, `data-item-door` and the handler kept): the kit's change must not turn a destination quiet. The kit page's demo tiles get a named handler recording the tap on `window.__btUiKit`. Nothing else on a screen changes in this PR.
6. **The palette**: page and the frame's middle stop #14232F (the frame's top and bottom stops around it, said); raisedMid #223D53 (its top and bottom around it); well #0E1D29 (wellSoft, wellDeep around it); `UI2_TEXT.ivory` #F1F0E9; `UI2_TEXT.gold` and `UI2_BRASS.highlight` #EAC474 (brass #D8AA58 and the fill stay). #4B6275 is a NEW token, `--ui2-line`, in its own table (`UI2_LINE`, never in `UI2_SURFACE`: suite 2 would read it as a surface and fail muted and gold), and replaces the four hand-written edges.
