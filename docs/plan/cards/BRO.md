# Card BRO: the Bro tab in pages, a showcase of your character (part 1, one PR)

Model and effort: the strong model, high. Tags: 6600 to 6619 (a new block: add it to `tools/dev/tag-blocks.json` and move `nextBlock` to 6620 in this PR), one a PR; this part has one: 6600. Branch `claude/plan-bro-1-pages`, from `main`.

The owner, 2026-11-25, with four mockups (made with ChatGPT; cropped to the menu, on the branch `claude/plan-orc` under `docs/plan/status/assets/bro-mock-0[1-4]-*.jpg`): *"I also want to redesign the bro character menu area. ... I want it so that it shows "pages" where all the information needs to be displayed together (instead of scrolling little by little) so all related information is shown at once. The biggest improvement is that the Bro tab becomes a showcase of the player's character rather than a long directory of features. Players see the big picture first and can investigate the details whenever they want."*

PURPOSE: on an upright iPhone the Bro tab opens on ONE page that shows who your character is, whole, with no scrolling; the detail is one tap away on pages that are each whole with no scrolling. The mockups give the content and its look (the game's own Lantern-Slate look, `docs/LANTERN-SLATE-SPEC.md`); they still scroll (01 runs on into 02, 03 into 04), and that scrolling is exactly what the owner wants gone. The band's height stays today's (the owner, earlier: "No don't change todays dashboard height").

## The three pages (an upright phone, the page 340 to 599 px wide)

1. **Overview** (opens first; mockup 01): your portrait, name, title line and the "LV N" chip (an XP bar ONLY if the top bar already shows a number it can read: never an invented one; today's note says there is no general combat XP, then no bar); **Combat overview**, four numbers of the weapon in your hand: DPS, Damage, Defense, Gear worn "n/6", with "Details" opening page 2; **Weapon mastery**, the three tiles Sword, Bow, Staff with their levels, "Equipped" on the one in hand, a tap opening page 2 on that weapon; **Your progress**, ONE row (achievements "n/33", lifetime kills, achievement points) that opens page 3.
2. **Combat stats** (mockups 03 and 04 on ONE page): the weapon picker Sword | Bow | Staff, then Offense (Damage, DPS, Attack speed, Range, Crit chance, Crit damage, Special damage, Element power), Survivability and Mobility, ALL in view at once (a grid of small cells, three or four across; the label over its number without the mockup's overlap).
3. **Progress** (mockup 02): the three counters with the achievements bar, and the doors as tiles two across.

Rules for what is on them:
- **Every number is the game's own**, from where today's card reads it (`src/ui/mobile/sheet/broStats.js` and the card's models). A mockup's stat the game does not have (its "Armor", perhaps) is left out, and a stat today's card shows that the mockup forgot is kept in its group. Nothing today's card shows or opens is lost: every door of today (Achievements, Life Skills, Points with its glow while points wait, Equipment to the Gear tab, Homestead, Pet, Titles, Clan or Party as today names it) is on a page, and the XP that flies to a combat skill's tile still lands on it while the Overview is open.
- **Getting about**: a strip of three small page marks (Overview, Combat, Progress) at the page's top or foot, the open one lit, so any page is one tap away; "Details" and the tiles as above; a back chip to the Overview as the pages under Bro already have. Taps only: no sideways swipe in this part. The Points window and the pages pushed under Bro (Achievements, Life Skills, Pets) work as today.
- **Wide** (600 px and wider: a phone on its side, a tablet, a computer's band): the three pages stand side by side as today's three panels do, each whole with no scroller, and the page marks are not drawn.
- **No vertical scroll** on any of the three at an upright iPhone's page (measure 390 x 844 and 375 x 667 with Safari's bars as the phone tests size them) and on a phone on its side. If one cannot fit at 375 x 667 without text under the spec's smallest size, it may scroll THERE only: say so in the PR body with the picture.

## How it is built (finish.md part 5, rule 5; CLAUDE.md)

- The rules are a module under `src/game/` (`broPages.js`: the page is ONE field with one transition function; which groups and which stats stand on which page; what a tap opens), with a suite that tests what it does and kills every mutant of its lines or lists the survivor with why. `BroCard.jsx` (387 lines) gains calls and loses lines where it can; the pages are components beside it under `src/ui/mobile/sheet/`.
- `?bropages=0` gives back today's card (a trial becomes the default with its switch): its row in the switch table (`tools/guards/lib/switches.mjs`, `--write`). No server file, no cap.
- The same-change rows that apply: a screen (the `dash-bro*` states of `tools/qa/ui-review/states.mjs` updated and the two new pages added; the section "Bro: your card, the Points window and Equipment" of `docs/UI-DESIGN-NOTES.md` rewritten for the pages, the owner's words above quoted; `data-esc`, `deskKeys.js` only if a key is added: none is asked); a phone test (`tools/qa/mp/mp-bropages.mjs`, `world: 'wheel'`, its `lane`: each page whole, `scrollHeight <= clientHeight`, upright and sideways, every door opens what it opened, the switch gives the old card back); a read of the page's layout only through `docs/specs/layout-reads.md`'s ways; `touch-action: manipulation`; words a computer reads through `deskWords()`.
- `mp-brocard`, `mp-switchbro` and `mp-panelguard` are mended where the card's shape changed (a test's mend says what it checked before and after).
- No new art: the icons are the ones the game has (the mastery tiles' pictures, the doors' icons). The memory check (`mp-membudget`) runs on the PR; no line is raised.
