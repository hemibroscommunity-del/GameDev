# Take BroTown's capture off the HP bar

The capture designs that fail a player who kills in one hit are exactly the ones that make the attempt wait on how much life the creature has left (Monster Hunter's roughly 15–30% threshold, WoW battle pets' 35%, Path of Exile's 2018 nets and BroTown's own 20% rule), and nearly everything else survives because it never asks. Path of Exile is the decisive precedent: when its Bestiary joined the core game in patch 3.5.0, the developer replaced "lower the beast's life, then net it within 3 seconds" with automatic capture on the kill, because the nets were unpopular ([PoE Wiki](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Net); [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/path-of-exile-how-capture-beasts/)). The survivors fall into four families (capture triggered by the kill; capture at full health, with odds set by the tool's tier, the target's awareness and the player's aim; damage turned into capture progress while the target cannot die; and capture taken out of combat through placed traps, bait and timers), and shared worlds add a fifth requirement: nobody else may steal or erase your attempt. BroTown should run two lanes: in combat, a trap armed on a monster at any health turns its death into a capture roll for every armed player who did at least 5% of its damage, with the kill's XP, gold, loot and quest credit paid as normal, the odds shown before the tap, and failures building pity instead of burning traps. In the skill lane, Trapping becomes a real life skill built on the Wheel's resource-node machinery: bait cooked from a land's catches decides which creature comes, Trapping level alone sets the odds, and the catch belongs to the trap's owner and is settled on the server clock when the trap is checked. After the catch, pets should stay cheap for the worker and the phone (owner bonuses, farm timers, a collection log, tinted variants) rather than becoming simulated combat companions; ownership should be stamped by the owner's own act, pets should stay out of No man's land loot, wild-trapped pets should stay bound, and the $2 supporter pass should carry only storage and evergreen cosmetics, never traps, capture boosts, random eggs or tradeable pets. Today's code also needs repairs in the same pass: a capture pays no kill credit and ignores who did the damage, a trap burns on every failed roll, Trapping stops levelling at six pets, and Verdant Wilds pets lose their element at the next login.

## One blow skips every HP window, so only HP-gated designs break

BroTown's capture is a gate followed by a coin toss. The worker accepts an attempt only on a live, hittable monster at or below **20% of its HP** and within **200 px**, from a player with one of six pet slots free and a **20-gold basic trap**. It then spends the trap and rolls once at **40% + 0.5 points per Trapping level + 0.2 per Woodcutting level − 5 points per level the monster stands above the player's combat level**, clamped to 10–95% ([pets.js](../../../server/src/pets.js); [shop.js](../../../server/src/shop.js)). A blow that takes a monster from above 20% straight to zero leaves no window at all: the worker answers `too-healthy` one moment and `no-monster` the next, so the rule stops working as soon as a player outgrows a land. The odds have a second flaw, because the combat-level gap moves them ten times faster than the trapping skill does. For a new trapper at equal level a pet costs about 2.5 traps (≈49 gold); five levels up, about 6.4 traps (≈127 gold); seven or more levels up, the 10% floor makes it **10 traps, or 200 gold**.

The genre's other HP gates show the same weakness at different scales. Monster Hunter makes a monster capturable below roughly **15–30%** of its health, depending on the monster and the game, then asks for a trap and two tranquilisers, and it signals readiness with a limp and a flashing skull ([Kiranico](https://mhworld.kiranico.com/en/guide/trapping); [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/582010/discussions/0/3335371283879396289/)). It holds up there largely because its monsters carry enormous health pools, and even so players report killing capture targets by accident with heavy weapons ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/582010/discussions/0/2244426186203384394)). WoW battle pets can be trapped only below **35%** ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/pet-ability=77/strong-trap)). Path of Exile's 2018 league gave a **3-second** net window on a low-life beast, which broke free, healed and enraged if the net failed ([poewiki](https://poewiki.net/wiki/Path_of_Exile:_Bestiary)). That is the design its developer then scrapped.

Games that allow a throw at full health keep HP as a bonus at most. In mainline Pokémon the HP term is worth at most ×3 ([pokeemerald](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald/blob/master/src/battle_script_commands.c)). In Palworld a Giga Sphere thrown at a level-30 Pal succeeds **12.6% of the time at full health and 33.7% near death**; the bigger factor is the sphere's capture power against the Pal's level (zero chance once the Pal out-levels it by 50), and a throw that hits from behind multiplies the roll by about 1.5 ([Palpedia](https://palpedia.io/en/guides/capture/); [Palworld Wiki](https://palworld.wiki.gg/wiki/Spheres); [lootlab](https://lootlab.app/palworld/guides/capture-formula/)). Pokémon Legends: Arceus pays ×1.75 for a "back strike" on a Pokémon that hasn't noticed you ([Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/pokemon-legends-arceus-hone-your-catching-techniques-with-this-guide/)), and Temtem charges ×0.8 for an alerted one ([Temtem Wiki](https://temtem.wiki.gg/wiki/Taming)). Pokémon GO drops HP entirely and puts its biggest multipliers on aim: landing inside the shrinking ring is worth ×1–2 and a curveball ×1.7 ([GamePress](https://pogo.gamepress.gg/catch-mechanics); [PokeMiners game master](https://github.com/PokeMiners/game_masters/blob/master/latest/latest.json)). A species with a 0.2 base rate at level 20 therefore goes from **16.7%** with a plain ball to about **46%** with a perfect excellent curveball: execution outweighs the best ball. WoW's hunters skip dice and HP alike. Tame Beast is a **6-second channel** that damage does not push back but stuns, knockdowns and silences break, usable only on beasts at or below the hunter's level ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1515/tame-beast); [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tame_Beast)). Black Desert's horse lasso deals no damage at all: one timed press, then ten seconds of presses to hold a gauge above half ([GrumpyGreen](https://grumpygreen.cricket/horse-taming/)).

Other games separate the capture from the HP bar with a second meter, or make damage feed it. Ark knocks creatures out with torpor, a meter separate from health, and lowers a tame's quality for damage taken along the way ([ARK Wiki](https://ark.wiki.gg/wiki/Taming)). Two monster-collecting RPGs go further and turn overkill inside out. In Cassette Beasts a creature being recorded cannot be defeated and keeps at least 1 HP; damage dealt to it raises the record chance, damage taken by the recorder lowers it, a live percentage hangs over the target, and a failed recording costs nothing ([Cassette Beasts Wiki](https://wiki.cassettebeasts.com/wiki/Recording); [devblog](https://www.cassettebeasts.com/2022/08/19/roll-the-tape/)). Dragon Quest Monsters shows a scouting percentage computed from the damage your party's attacks *would* deal; a failure can anger the target, and in one entry feeding it prevents the anger ([Dragon Quest Wiki](https://dragon-quest.org/w/index.php?title=Monster_scouting&mobileaction=toggle_view_desktop)). Under these rules a stronger player captures faster and allies help rather than steal. Pokémon's False Swipe is the cheap guard: a hit that would kill leaves the target at 1 HP ([pokeemerald](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald/blob/master/src/battle_script_commands.c)). It only helps a player who remembers to switch to it.

The simplest family makes the kill itself the trigger. Path of Exile's Einhar captures beasts as they die ([PoE Wiki](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Net)); Ni no Kuni raises a heart over a defeated creature about **25%** of the time for docile ones and about **4%** for rare ones ([Prima Games](https://primagames.com/news/ni-no-kuni-wrath-white-witch-recruiting-familiars)); MapleStory's familiars come as card drops ([Dexless](https://dexless.com/threads/familiar-revamp-details-maplestory.1675/)), and Siralim's as cards players put at about 0.1% ([Siralim 3 Wiki](https://siralim3.fandom.com/wiki/Cards)). Overkill cannot spoil any of them, but the pure-luck versions draw the loudest complaints in the whole survey. Ni no Kuni players call it "completely random" ([GameFAQs](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/998014-ni-no-kuni-wrath-of-the-white-witch/65581785)); MapleStory players reported 20–60 minutes per low-level familiar after a 2020 revamp ([Dexless](https://dexless.com/threads/familiar-revamp-details-maplestory.1675/)), and one forum thread is titled "Familiar card drop rate is insane" ([Nexon forum](https://forums.maplestory.nexon.net/discussion/comment/104595/)); and a fan mod flattened Yo-kai Watch Blasters' 0.5–6% befriend rates to 8% ([Blasters++](https://x.com/Blastersplusmod/status/1840451478692982982)). A kill-triggered capture needs visible progress (pity, a shown chance or a meter) or it reads as just another drop.

Put side by side, the verdict for a player who one-hits everything is clear:

| Design family | Examples | Reads HP at capture? | Survives a one-hit player? | What BroTown would need |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weaken to a threshold, then trap | BroTown (20%), Monster Hunter (~15–30%), WoW battle pets (35%), PoE's 2018 nets | Yes, as a gate | **No**: one blow skips the window | Retire it |
| Odds at any HP from tool tier, level and awareness | Palworld, Legends: Arceus, Temtem, mainline Pokémon | Only as a bonus | **Yes**, if thrown before the killing blow | Trap tier against land tier, rolled on the worker |
| Aim sets a multiplier | Pokémon GO's ring and curveball (up to ×3.4) | No | **Yes** | An input judged on the worker's clock |
| Channel or damage-free restraint | WoW's Tame Beast, Black Desert's lasso | No | **Yes** | A monster-side root, channel checks, a lock against interference |
| A separate non-lethal meter | Ark's torpor | Its own meter | **Partly**: the knockout weapons also wound | Damage-free tools only |
| Damage fills the capture while the target can't die | Cassette Beasts, Dragon Quest Monsters | Floors it at 1 HP | **Yes, and overkill helps** | An HP floor, a counter and an owner per monster |
| A no-kill clamp on your own hits | False Swipe | Clamps it | Only if the player switches mode | Cheap, but easy to forget |
| The kill is the trigger | PoE since 3.5.0, Ni no Kuni, MapleStory cards, Siralim | No | **Yes**: overkill is irrelevant | One branch on the kill path, plus pity and shown odds |
| Placed trap, bait or timer | OSRS box traps and bird houses, RS3 Big Game Hunter, Stardew crab pots | No | **Yes**: no combat at all | Trap spots on the resource-node pipeline |
| Feed, befriend or charm | Valheim, Minecraft, Guild Wars 2 juveniles, Ultima Online | No | **Yes** | A calm state and skill gates |
| Eggs, breeding, incubation | Palworld, Temtem, Albion | No | **Yes** | Farm timers |

How the result is shown matters as much as the rule. Pokémon's capture routine passes the number of checks the ball survived (0–3) to its animation, so the familiar wobbles are honest suspense wrapped around a single probability ([pokeemerald](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald/blob/master/src/battle_script_commands.c)). BroTown's worker can roll the same way and send a shake count for the phone to draw in code, with no new textures under the memory budget ([CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md)). Hiding or bending the odds backfires. Players called Niantic "shady" for pulling Pokémon GO's base catch and flee rates from its data file ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/pokemon-go-players-call-niantic-shady-for-hiding-base-capture-flee-rates-2139501/)), and the file downloaded for this research still carries none ([PokeMiners](https://github.com/PokeMiners/game_masters/blob/master/latest/latest.json)). Palworld's reticle showed a Lamball at **49% whose true chance was 18.25%**, a gap a mod now exists to undo ([r/Palworld datamine](https://embedez.com/gallery/6a63d06e403c7d0b4b772a03); [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/palworld/mods/5216)). Near-miss research cuts both ways: in Clark and colleagues' 2009 study, near-misses raised the urge to keep playing only when the player had control over the gamble ([Neuron, via ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/24010632_Gambling_Near-Misses_Enhance_Motivation_to_Gamble_and_Recruit_Win-Related_Brain_Circuitry)). An honest "it broke free on the last shake" is information; a staged one is manipulation. Extra steps inside fast combat get rejected or ignored: Path of Exile dropped its nets, and Nexomon's button prompt can be mashed for roughly 5% ([GameRant](https://gamerant.com/nexomon-extinction-capture-creatures-how/); [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1196630/discussions/0/2916598577627003793/)). On a phone the skill belongs in preparation — the right trap, the right bait, an unaware target — not in a minigame played while dodging.

## Trapping holds up as a skill when its own level decides the catch

Old School RuneScape's Hunter is the reference for trapping as a skill, and every lever it pulls is Hunter level. Traps at a time rise from **2 at level 1 to 3, 4 and 5 at levels 40, 60 and 80**, with one extra in the PvP Wilderness ([OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Hunter_training)). Each creature has its own catch curve from its unlock level, so a red chinchompa goes from about **45% at level 63 to 89% at 99** ([OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Carnivorous_chinchompa)). New creatures and trap types arrive every few levels, from ferrets at 27 to black chinchompas at 73 ([OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Hunter_creatures)), and the 2024 Hunters' Rumours add contract tiers at 46, 57, 72 and 91, each designed to take **8–10 minutes** ([OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Hunters%27_Rumours); [Jagex](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/a=903/the-hunter-guild---varlamore?oldschool=1)). The catch roll has no combat term and no health term. Even the skilling pet barely moves with level: the grey chinchompa's baby is 1 in 130,070 at level 53 and 1 in 128,920 at 99 ([OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Baby_chinchompa)). The pet is flavour; the skill's real rewards are traps at a time, catch chance and access. BroTown's +0.5 points per Trapping level, applied flatly to every monster and swamped by the −5 points per level of combat gap, is the opposite design.

The skill runs at two tempos. Active trapping is a loop of a minute or less: RuneLite's Hunter plugin models a laid trap as standing **1 minute** before it collapses ([RuneLite](https://github.com/runelite/runelite/blob/master/runelite-client/src/main/java/net/runelite/client/plugins/hunter/HunterTrap.java)), and a chinchompa that comes out either walks into a trap or dismantles one and hops away ([OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Chinchompa_%28Hunter%29)). Passive trapping runs on the server clock. A bird house baited with 10 seeds fills in about **50 minutes of wall-clock time, logged in or not**, and RuneLite tracks exactly **four** fixed bird-house spaces ([OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Bird_house_%28item%29); [RuneLite](https://github.com/runelite/runelite/blob/master/runelite-client/src/main/java/net/runelite/client/plugins/timetracking/hunter/BirdHouseSpace.java)). Stardew Valley's crab pots work the same way as daily income, with professions that remove the bait cost or the junk ([Stardew Wiki](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Crab_Pot)), and Don't Starve spends bait only when something is caught ([Don't Starve Wiki](https://dontstarve.wiki.gg/wiki/Trap)). Loops that need the player present fare worse. A Valheim tame advances only while the animal is fed, calm and within about 64 m of a player, for roughly 30 minutes ([PlayerEcho](https://playerecho.com/valheim/taming-guide)), which is a quarter of a free BroTown player's planned two-hour day. Black Desert's horse training runs on a client-driven auto-path while the game sits minimised ([BDFoundry](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/training-guide/)): a bot pattern, and for BroTown a cost, since a room's capacity is set by the messages it receives ([CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md)). A trap the worker settles only when it is checked sends nothing in between.

Bait is where trapping hooks into the rest of a game, and it works only when it is decisive. RuneScape 3's Big Game Hunter, a non-combat activity needing 75 Hunter and 55 Slayer, gives each of its nine dinosaurs its own bait on its own bait pad, draws each one's detection range as a coloured circle on the ground, and hides a species for an hour after five or six hunts ([RuneScape Wiki](https://runescape.wiki/w/Anachronia_Big_Game_Hunter)). Varlamore's jerboa tails, caught in box traps, bait the fennec fox's deadfall, so one catch is the key to the next ([Jagex](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/a=903/the-hunter-guild---varlamore?oldschool=1)). Stardew's Targeted Bait, refined from a chosen fish at a Bait Maker unlocked at Fishing 6, makes that catch **2–4 times** as likely ([Stardew Wiki](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Targeted_Bait)). Beside these, RuneScape's box-trap bait, worth about +3% ([RuneHQ](https://www.runehq.com/item/box-trap)), is invisible to players. Bait should either decide whether a creature can come at all or weight it by ×2 to ×4, and it should be another skill's output — the role BroTown's cooking, fishing and farming can play.

The other routes that skip combat also ignore HP. Herbiboar is a trail of clues ending in a catch, gated by 80 Hunter and 31 Herblore ([OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Herbiboar)). Guild Wars 2 lets any ranger charm a juvenile that no other ranger owns, and the juvenile is not used up, so nobody is crowded out ([GW2 Wiki](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Pet)). Ultima Online gates each creature on taming skill alone (a dragon at 93.9, a greater dragon at 104.7) and charges each pet 1–5 of a player's 5 control slots by its power ([UOGuide](https://www.uoguide.com/Greater_Dragon); [uo.com](https://uo.com/wiki/ultima-online-wiki/skills/animal-taming/pets-ownership/)). UO also shows the abuse a repeatable skill attracts. Re-taming the same creature earns no skill, yet players still script "mostly-AFK" taming macros ([UO Outlands forum](https://forums.uooutlands.com/index.php?threads/guide-and-script-animal-taming-afk-skill-gain-in-classicuo.3453/)). Commercial bots catch red chinchompas on autopilot, and Jagex reportedly banned more than **6.2 million** bot accounts in 2026 ([bottinghub](https://bottinghub.com/hunter-ai/); [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Old-School-RuneScape-finally-feels-fair-again-as-Jagex-bans-6-2-Million-bots.1313053.0.html)). The defences that hold are structural: a fixed payoff per stretch of real time, inputs made by the player's own skills, outputs bound to the player, and a check no faster than an honest human, which BroTown's worker already enforces on every harvest gesture ([CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md)).

BroTown's current skill has one more structural fault. The capture handler is the server's only writer of Trapping XP, it refuses a seventh pet before awarding any, and the server has no way to release a pet, so **a player's Trapping stops levelling after six captures** ([pets.js](../../../server/src/pets.js)). OSRS never hits this wall because much of what Hunter catches is goods that feed other skills: antelope meat and horns, fox fur, jerboa tails ([Jagex](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/a=903/the-hunter-guild---varlamore?oldschool=1)), and in RuneScape 3, furs that go into Summoning pouches ([RuneScape Wiki](https://runescape.wiki/w/Larupia_fur)).

## After the catch, the cheapest pet roles have lasted longest

What a pet does afterwards decides what it costs to build. BroTown's pet today is the cheapest kind: an emoji drawn beside its owner whose only effect is to widen the owner's loot pickup from 160 to **240 px**, measured from the owner's own position, because the worker does not track the pet at all ([pets.js](../../../server/src/pets.js); [pets spec](../../../docs/specs/pets.md)). The client already sketches more, with an `evolvePet` that merges two pets into the next tier and gem-and-gold enchanting. Neither has a server handler, and the join-time sanitizer keeps only its whitelisted fields, so both vanish at the next login ([gameSystems.js](../../../src/data/gameSystems.js); [pets.js](../../../server/src/pets.js)). Across the games surveyed, six roles recur, and their cost differs by an order of magnitude:

| Role | Examples | What the server must do | Fit for BroTown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cosmetic follower | OSRS skilling pets, FFXIV minions; BroTown today | Remember which pet is out; each phone draws it | Keep, as the emoji or a small mini-sprite under `mp-membudget` |
| Owner bonus | Loot pets in Torchlight II, Diablo IV, Black Desert, Lost Ark; MapleStory familiar badges; Pokémon GO's Best Buddy | Adjust the owner's own numbers | Yes: the 240 px pickup today, plus small capped bonuses |
| Timed job | Lost Ark's Pet Ranch, Monster Hunter Rise's Meowcenaries, Throne & Liberty's Amitoi, Palworld incubators | Store timestamps and settle them when read | Yes, at the farm |
| Proc pet | Guild Wars 2's Soulbeast | Add the pet's effect inside the owner's own hit | Later, only if combat feel is wanted |
| Simulated companion | WoW hunter pets, GW2 ranger pets, PoE spectres, Palworld base workers | Run a second actor with AI, pathing, threat and PvP rules in every tick | Avoid |
| Mount | Black Desert, Albion | Change the movement bound; add dismount rules | Last |

Owner bonuses are cheap to run but are where pets turned into paid power. Black Desert's looting pets sell for about **1,100 pearls** in its cash shop, and guides tell grinders they need four or five because loot left on the ground is "wasted silver" ([GrumpyGreen](https://grumpygreen.cricket/bdo-pearl-price/); [ClawSkills](https://clawskills123.com/articles/pearl-shop-pets.html)). MapleStory's pets stop working after about **90 days** unless revived for 2,400 NX or reward points ([GameTaco](https://gametaco.net/maplestory-premium-water-of-life/)), and RuneScape 3 sells its drop-scavenging legendary pets through its Premier Club and Marketplace ([RuneScape Wiki](https://runescape.wiki/w/Legendary_pet)). Diablo IV shows a gentler route, giving one looting pet free through a quest and the rest with editions ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/d4/guides/pet-guide/)). A "pure convenience" becomes mandatory once it changes income per hour, so BroTown's pickup radius should stay convenience-sized and never be sold. Small, capped buffs have a cleaner record: MapleStory's familiar set badges are worth a few points (Max HP +5, or Speed +1 and all stats +1%) ([MapleStory Wiki](https://maplestorywiki.net/w/Familiars)), and Pokémon GO's Best Buddy is worth exactly one extra level, and only while it is your active buddy ([Pokémon GO Wiki](https://pokemongo.fandom.com/wiki/Buddy_Pok%C3%A9mon)). BroTown has a natural buff of this size. Each pet's land element could grant a small, capped resistance to that land's on-hit status (chill, burn, gust, hold, daze, shock, soak or poison, all already settled by the worker; [monsterstatus.js](../../../server/src/monsterstatus.js)). That makes "one pet from every land" a collection goal without opening a power gap.

Combat companions are the expensive end, and the games that kept them built whole class budgets around them. WoW's hunters hold 200 stabled pets plus 5 active ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/hunter-pets-guide)), yet Marksmanship's Lone Wolf pays **+5% damage for having no pet** ([Wowpedia](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Lone_Wolf)). Hunter and warlock pets taunted monsters off group tanks for about **14 years**, until Battle for Azeroth switched Growl off automatically inside dungeons in 2018 ([AIPT](https://aiptcomics.com/2018/06/10/world-of-warcraft-battle-for-azeroth-hunter-pets-growl-will-automatically-turn-off-upon-entering-a-dungeon/)). Guild Wars 2's pets take 95% less damage from most PvE area attacks; they were nerfed in PvP after complaints, and players argue that every hit a pet soaks is hidden defence for its owner ([GW2 Wiki](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Pet); [GW2 forum archive](https://forum-en.gw2archive.eu/forum/professions/ranger/Why-no-AOE-damage-reduction-for-pets)). GW2's own structural answer, the Soulbeast, merges the pet into the player as attributes and skills ([GW2 Wiki](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Soulbeast)). Palworld's base workers get stuck and go hungry ([Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/palworld-pal-stuck-pathing-broken-ai/)), and Ark capped tames at 500 per tribe on PC PvE servers because packed areas were crashing players ([Inquisitr](https://www.inquisitr.com/ark-survival-evolved-pc-update-puts-limits-on-tribes-and-tamed-dinos-lets-you-tame-snakes)). In BroTown's Durable Object, a simulated pet is a second monster per player in every tick, a bigger `zone_state`, a PvP target and another full figure on a phone whose memory is already budgeted. A proc pet gives most of the feel at a fraction of the cost.

Care that only punishes has been walked back almost everywhere. WoW removed hunter pet happiness, under which an unhappy pet dealt 25% less damage, in patch 4.1 (April 2011) ([Wowpedia](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Happiness)). Ultima Online's Publish 111 stopped bonded pets going wild from hunger, a rule that had cost players their pets mid-fight ([UO forum](https://forum.uo.com/discussion/9389/pet-loyalty-clarification)). Ragnarok Online's starving pets still run away ([Ragnarok Wiki](https://ragnarok.fandom.com/wiki/Cute_Pet_System)). What lasts is progress that only rises and has a cap. Pokémon GO's Best Buddy takes 300 hearts at no more than 10 a day, about a month ([Pokémon GO Wiki](https://pokemongo.fandom.com/wiki/Buddy_Pok%C3%A9mon)), and Palworld's condensing turns **48 duplicates into four stars worth +20%** ([Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/palworld-condensing-guide)). Hidden rolled stats create a chase but also mass catch-and-release grinding; Pokémon later added Bottle Caps and mints to repair bad rolls ([Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/pokemon-scarlet-and-violet-hyper-training-and-how-to-get-bottle-caps)), while Coromon shows its potential tiers openly, about 1 in 35 "potent" and 1 in 3,194 "perfect" ([Coromon Wiki](https://coromon.wiki.gg/wiki/Potential)). Collections pay off with small capped rewards like the Shiny Charm, which lifts shiny odds from 1 in 4,096 to about 1 in 1,365 ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Scarlet-Violet/archives/392967)). Those odds assume hundreds of encounters an hour, though; a player with two hours a day needs a common variant tier nearer Coromon's 1 in 35. BroTown can draw such variants at zero texture memory, because the Wheel already recolours monsters past level 20 with a sprite tint instead of new art ([CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md)). Since the planned free tier leaves a player away about 22 hours a day, nothing about a pet should decay while they are gone.

Idle loops turn the pets you are not using into value while you are away, which suits short sessions, but every one is a faucet. Palworld incubates eggs in 3, 18 or 36 hours ([GameServerKings](https://www.gameserverkings.com/knowledge-base/palworld/palworld-cake-and-breeding-farm/)). Albion grows livestock in 1 day 20 hours and halves that for Premium subscribers ([Albion Wiki](https://wiki.albiononline.com/wiki/Livestock)), which is speed sold for money and should not be copied by BroTown's pass. Monster Hunter Rise's reserve buddies come back after the hunter finishes five quests of their own ([Fextralife](https://monsterhunterrise.wiki.fextralife.com/Meowcenaries)), which ties progress to play and cannot be idled on a second device. Breeding needs a brake: Temtem gives each parent a fertility of up to 8 that drops by one per egg, and multiplies its 1-in-2,000 rare-colour odds by ten for each rare-colour parent ([Temtem Wiki](https://temtem.wiki.gg/wiki/Breeding)). The failure mode is economic. One critic wrote that WoW's garrisons, by giving every player a mine and an herb garden, "destroyed the economy for ore and herbs" ([Mash Those Buttons](https://mashthosebuttons.com/2015/01/wow-thoughts-on-improving-garrisons/)), whereas Slime Rancher's market halves a product's price as sales saturate it ([Slime Rancher Wiki](https://slimerancher.fandom.com/wiki/Plort_Market_%28Slime_Rancher%29)). BroTown's farm can host incubators, expeditions and pens on the same principle: real-time timers settled when read, output routed into cooking and smithing rather than dumped on the market, and any buy-back price that saturates.

## Shared worlds punish any capture someone else can steal, erase or flood

### Ownership has to come from the owner's own act

In a shared world, one-hit power has a multiplayer twin: a strong passer-by who kills the monster a weaker player was wearing down. Every open capture rule drew theft complaints. In Palworld the first sphere thrown wins, so a player who never attacked can take a Pal others weakened, and players asked for a timed lock ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1623730/discussions/0/4203615689065956058/)). Ark's official PvE rule gives taming rights to the last player to hit a creature, which rewards sniping ([Ark Wiki](https://ark.fandom.com/wiki/Taming)). The fixes that stuck either took the creature out of contention or gave everyone their own reward. WoW phases a pet battle away from other players and respawns "the same exact wild pet" if one is interrupted ([WarcraftPets](https://www.warcraftpets.com/wow-pet-battles/)); WoW's tagging went from first-hit-only to **up to five players sharing a kill** in Legion's patch 7.0.3 of July 2016 ([Wowpedia](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Tap)); Pokémon GO raids give each trainer a private catch, with balls weighted by their own damage ([GO Hub](https://pokemongohub.net/post/guide/go-hub-guide-to-raid-battles/)); Guild Wars 2 rolls loot for each participant "as if they were the only participant" ([GW2 Wiki](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Dynamic_event)); and Monster Hunter pays capture rewards to the whole party ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/582010/discussions/0/1737760710136144371/)). Path of Exile first gave every party member the captured beast, multiplying supply by party size, then cut that back to a separate roll per member ([PoE Wiki](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Beast); [PoE forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3349303)). BroTown's kill path already pays XP and quest credit to every contributor still in the zone, and gold to every one above a **5% damage share** ([combat.js](../../../server/src/combat.js)). Its capture path never reads who did the damage: any player within 200 px can trap a monster someone else weakened, and the capture removes it for everyone with no kill rewards at all ([pets.js](../../../server/src/pets.js)). So one trapper can deny a whole group its rewards, and one strong passer-by can erase a weaker player's capture by killing first.

Placed traps and rare spawns raise the same problem in another shape, and BroTown cannot use the usual escape. Old School RuneScape lets a collapsed trap become a ground item others can take after 60 seconds, so guides tell hunters to carry 10–15 spares ([OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Drop); [osrsbestinslot](https://www.osrsbestinslot.com/osrs-hunter-guide/)), and players escape crowded chinchompa spots by switching worlds ([osrsmoneymaking.guide](https://osrsmoneymaking.guide/news/best-place-to-catch-red-chinchompas-in-osrs-2/)). WoW's rare tameable Loque'nahak appears for about ten minutes after a 6–10 hour respawn and is usually gone within two ([Huntsman's Lodge](https://huntsmanslodge.com/535/tips-and-advice-for-hunters-seeking-loquenahak/)). BroTown is one room, `brotown-1`, with no other world to switch to, so exclusivity has to be designed out rather than escaped. Pokémon GO's lure shows how: its spawns appear for every trainer in range while each catch is private ([Niantic](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/faq/1789-lure-modules/)). A trap's owner should be a field the server stamps when the trap is placed. Guild Wars 2's bug, in which a pet that loaded before its owner looked unowned and could be charmed by another ranger, shows what inferring ownership at check time leads to ([GW2 forum](https://en-forum.guildwars2.com/topic/132909-rangers-pet-charm-does-not-work-for-juvenile-alpine-wolves-or-juvenile-blue-moas/)). A trap that times out should go back to its owner by mail, never onto the ground.

### Pets need a ledger before they get a market

Trading is the strongest engagement lever in a creature game and the riskiest. A Sensor Tower headline put Pokémon GO's daily revenue **up 39%, to $2.5 million**, after trading launched ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/pokemon-go-daily-revenue)), but GO fenced trading heavily: a Pokémon can be traded only once, Mythicals not at all, rare "special" trades are limited to one a day at Stardust costs of up to 1,000,000, and partners must stand within 100 m ([Pokémon GO Wiki](https://pokemongo.fandom.com/wiki/Trading); [trainercodes.app](https://trainercodes.app/guides/trading-basics)). WoW never lets wild-caught battle pets be caged for sale, reportedly because buying them would hollow out the collecting ([Blizzard Support](https://us.battle.net/support/en/article/309267); [MMO-Champion](https://www.mmo-champion.com/threads/1151906-Pets-that-cannot-be-caged-and-sold)). Its one cash-bought tradeable pet, the 2012 Guardian Cub, saw its gold price plummet soon after it appeared in the store ([WarcraftPets](https://www.warcraftpets.com/news/guardian_cub_tradable_pet/)). Pet markets fail through duplication faster than through balance: Grow a Garden switched off pet gifting on 7 June 2025 over a dupe ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/why-pet-gifting-disabled-grow-garden)), and Pet Simulator X automatically deleted duped pets and banned the dupers' alts ([Entertainment Focus](https://entertainment-focus.com/2021/12/14/pet-simulator-x-pet-dupers-get-punished/)).

BroTown is not ready for any of this yet. When the server holds no pets for a player, a join adopts the client's own claimed list, checked only for shape, and every join regenerates pet ids ([pets.js](../../../server/src/pets.js)). That is acceptable while pets are cosmetic, as the code's own comment says, but it is the same gap the gear provenance ledger was built to close for armour, where a join claim "cannot be told apart from a forged one" ([gearprov.js](../../../server/src/gearprov.js)). Pets need stable, server-minted ids with provenance before they carry value, and a capture must be one idempotent server operation, so a reconnect cannot mint twice. Against bots, client-side tricks buy days: Niantic's 2016 request-signing change was worked around in about 3.5 days ([GO Hub](https://pokemongohub.net/post/news/new-niantic-security-measure-deployed-third-party-apps-affected/)). Server rules hold, such as Pokémon GO's unofficial cap of about **4,800 catches a day** ([Future Game Releases](https://www.futuregamereleases.com/2020/08/pokemon-go-daily-and-weekly-catch-limit/)) and Niantic's three-strike policy, whose first strike can remove trading and gifting ([Niantic](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/faq/39-three-strike-discipline-policy/)). Bad-luck protection is accepted when it is per player: WoW's battle-pet traps raise the next attempt's chance by 20–30% after each failure ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/pet-ability=135/pristine-trap)), and RuneScape 3 raises boss-pet odds at fixed multiples of a player's own kill count ([RuneScape Wiki](https://runescape.wiki/w/Bad_luck_mitigation)).

### Pets belong outside the PvP loot pile

Permanent loss of a long-term companion is where studios retreat. Old School RuneScape now insures every pet automatically, and a killer never receives one ([OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Probita); [OSRS Fandom](https://oldschoolrunescape.fandom.com/wiki/Probita)). Ultima Online raises dead bonded pets as ghosts ([UOGuide](https://www.uoguide.com/Pet_Bonding)), and when it split its world in May 2000, Trammel, the side where all PvP was consensual, "quickly became one of the most popular facets" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_Online)). Albion keeps full loot but destroys part of each drop and never lets a skin drop ([Albion Oracle](https://albionoracle.com/en/codex/mechanics/death); [Albion forum](https://forum.albiononline.com/index.php/Thread/45188-Mounts-dropped-on-Death/)). Ark's PvP servers forbid pocketing a creature damaged in the last 60 seconds, or releasing one with enemies within 3,500 units ([Ark Wiki](https://ark.wiki.gg/wiki/Cryopod)). BroTown's No man's land already sends a dying player's bag to the killer while keeping worn gear unless the victim wore a red skull ([CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md)). Pets live outside the bag and should stay there. A pet that falls should be knocked home to the farm rather than killed, and a pet should not be swapped in or out for some seconds after combat. Putting pets at risk would push collectors out of the rings entirely, which is what Trammel's popularity predicts.

### The supporter pass can sell space and looks, never odds

BroTown sells no randomness, which keeps it outside nearly all of the 2024–2026 loot-box rules, and the cleanest pet design keeps it there. Belgium's gaming commission ruled the paid loot boxes in Overwatch, FIFA 18 and CS:GO illegal games of chance in 2018 ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/belgiums-gambling-commission-rules-against-loot-boxes-in-overwatch-fifa-18-and-csgo/)). Brazil banned them in games likely to reach minors from 17 March 2026 ([Pixelkin](https://pixelkin.org/2025/09/29/brazil-becomes-latest-country-to-ban-loot-boxes-targeted-at-minors/)). PEGI rates paid random items 16 by default, and time-limited purchase offers 12, for games submitted from June 2026 ([PEGI](https://pegi.info/news/pegi-expands-age-rating-criteria-interactive-risk-categories)). South Korea has required exact odds since March 2024 and found 266 games in breach by July of that year ([GameWorldObserver](https://gameworldobserver.com/2024/07/08/266-games-violated-loot-box-rules-south-korea)). The US FTC's **$20 million** Genshin Impact settlement bars loot-box sales to under-16s without parental consent ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2025/01/ftc-settlement-order-bans-sales-genshin-impact-loot-boxes-kids-under-16-without-their-parents)). Australia exempts chance mechanics that involve no real money ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/games-featuring-paid-loot-boxes-will-soon-receive-a-mandatory-m-rating-in-australia)), and the EU's Digital Fairness Act, expected late in 2026, is likely to target in-game virtual currencies ([Freshfields](https://www.freshfields.com/en/our-thinking/blogs/technology-quotient/the-eus-proposed-digital-fairness-act-a-game-developers-guide-to-potential-imp-102ltio)). Traps bought only with earned gold sit inside every exemption. A random pet egg sold for money would not, and in Belgium could be criminal. Pokémon GO does sell Poké Balls, at 100 coins for 20 ([Pokémon GO Wiki](https://pokemongo.fandom.com/wiki/Shop)), but in one shared room a bought trap buys capture attempts against other players, which breaks BroTown's no-pay-to-win pledge.

The precedented safe perks are storage and looks. Pokémon HOME Premium raises storage from 30 to 6,000 for $2.99 a month ([GameRant](https://gamerant.com/pokemon-home-how-to-carry-more-pokemon/)), and Path of Exile sells pets that are purely cosmetic followers ([PoE Wiki](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Pet)). Two cautions follow from the rest of the evidence. Never sell a convenience you might later have to cut: Pokémon GO's 2023 Remote Raid Pass price rise and five-a-day cap drew a petition of more than 100,000 signatures ([Charlie INTEL](https://www.charlieintel.com/pokemon/pokemon-go-devs-address-viral-hearusniantic-movement-remote-raid-pass-controversy-252483/)). And make cosmetics evergreen or returning: WoW eventually opened outlet vendors for old items after players criticised how little came back to its rotating Trading Post ([Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow/news/dragonflight-trading-post-items-will-appear-more-than-once/); [Master of Warcraft](https://www.masterofwarcraft.net/2026/08/wow-september-trading-post-old-rewards-return.html)). If the pass adds stable space, collection bonuses should be keyed to species ever logged, not pets currently held, so storage never buys power.

### Recurring windows outlast a launch that fades by week six

A pet system's launch excitement will not carry it. In a BMJ study, Pokémon GO players walked **955 more steps a day in their first week and none more by week six** ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27965211/)). Short, recurring windows are the documented answer. Community Day has been a monthly three-hour event since January 2018, with one species boosted and a shiny rate observed near 1 in 25 ([Serebii](https://serebii.net/pokemongo/communityday/january2018.shtml); [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/insertcoin/2018/01/31/heres-your-boosted-chance-of-getting-a-shiny-on-pokemon-go-community-day/)), and GO's first in-game event, Halloween 2016, lifted spending about 133% over five days ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/pokemon-go-halloween-event-revenue)). For BroTown, a monthly featured-creature day in one land and a weekly spotlight hour suit both the two-hour cap and a single world spread across time zones. Rare creatures should be rolled per player rather than announced to a server that will camp them.

## BroTown should capture at the kill and trap on the clock

The recommended combat lane replaces the HP window with an armed trap that resolves on the kill. The player taps the trap button with a monster targeted, at any HP, within range. The worker checks the gates that belong at the door: a trap whose tier matches the land, the Trapping level that tier requires, and room in the pet list or a farm stable. It then marks that monster for that player for a short window (10–15 seconds is a reasonable start). If the monster dies while marked, the worker rolls a capture for every marked player who dealt at least 5% of its damage, using the `dmgByPlayer` shares the kill path already computes ([combat.js](../../../server/src/combat.js)). The kill pays XP, gold, loot and quest credit exactly as it does now; the pet is an extra for whoever succeeds, never a substitute for the group's reward. A one-hit player arms, swings and gets an immediate roll, and a strong passer-by who finishes a weaker player's monster now adds a helper instead of erasing a chance. The trap is spent only on a success, the way Don't Starve spends bait; a failure gives Trapping XP and raises that player's odds on that species, WoW-style, so a bad streak ends within a few tries. The odds come from things the worker knows and the player can see. Trap tier against land tier should be a multiplier rather than BroTown's flat −5 points per level; Pokémon's ninth generation uses ×0.8 per step, which never slams into a floor ([pokeemerald-expansion](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/blob/master/src/battle_script_commands.c)). Add a per-tier curve on Trapping level, starting near half at a tier's unlock and rising toward 90%, as OSRS's curves do. An element-matched trap can be worth extra, as the Net Ball is worth ×3–3.5 against Water and Bug types ([pokeemerald](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald/blob/master/src/battle_script_commands.c); [pokeemerald-expansion](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/blob/master/src/battle_script_commands.c)), and arming on a monster that is not yet targeting you can earn a bonus, a server-checkable version of the Legends: Arceus back strike. The chance appears on the button before the tap; the worker sends back a shake count; the phone draws the snap, the wobbles and the click or the break in code.

The alternatives each have a case, and each loses on cost or on fit. A snare-and-bind (throw a damage-free snare, then hold the button through a two- or three-second bind the monster fights back against, as WoW's Tame Beast and Black Desert's lasso work) gives the strongest capture moment and ignores overkill. But it needs a monster-side root BroTown does not have, since its hold and daze statuses run only from monsters to players today ([CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md)); it also needs a validated channel, and a reserved state so a passer-by's area attack cannot finish the monster mid-bind. A Cassette Beasts capture meter, which floors the monster at 1 HP for a few seconds while every hit fills a shown percentage, makes power and allies *help*, and is the best later upgrade for elites and bosses; but it needs an owner on the window and holds a monster undying in a shared room. A False Swipe-style capture stance is cheap but is a mode players forget. Egg drops or befriending on defeat survive overkill too, but passive drops ride kill farming, the activity bots and area-attack builds maximise, so they need lower rates and harder caps than a deliberate trap. Capture on the kill is the only option with a fast action-RPG precedent that shipped as the fix to exactly this problem. It plays with one tap on a phone, and it is one branch on a kill path that already knows every contributor. Its weakness, feeling like a random drop, is answered by the shown chance, the wobbles and the pity.

The skill lane gives Trapping a life of its own, built from parts the Wheel already has. Trap spots in every land would be baked by the same bake that places the 259 resource nodes, and gated by Trapping level per tier the way `GATHER_REQ_LVL` gates ore, wood and fish, with the same grey locked tool, the same "Requires … Lv N" try and the same minimap markers ([CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md); [gathering.js](../../../server/src/gathering.js)). The player sets a trap forged from that tier's metal (copper, iron, black steel, titanium, obsidian) and baits it with something cooked from that land's fish or crops; the bait decides which of the land's creatures comes, as Big Game Hunter's pads and Stardew's Targeted Bait do. The trap fills on the server clock on the bird-house scale of tens of minutes, logged in or not, and is settled when the player checks it with the existing harvest gesture under the existing honest-speed floor. Most checks pay materials (fur, horn, tail) that feed the forge and the cookhouse, plus Trapping XP fixed per creature tier and fading for tiers far below the player. Some pay an egg, written straight to a per-player hatchery list rather than the inventory, so it cannot travel through trade, mail, the market or a No man's land death pile, and hatched on the farm on a real-time timer. Spots are not exclusive: each player's trap and catch are their own, so a crowd cannot queue, steal or empty a land, and a high-level player gains little from low tiers. Traps at a time grow on Trapping's five-level steps, with one more inside No man's land for the risk, as the Wilderness grants. Because the worker settles a trap only when it is checked, the lane costs nothing between visits. Because it fills while a free player is offline, it gives value outside the two-hour cap, which is also why caps belong per account and inputs must come from the player's own skills.

Taken together, the change looks like this:

| | BroTown today | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| When you can try | Monster at or below 20% HP, within 200 px | Arm a trap on a monster at any HP in range; it rolls if the monster dies while marked |
| Who can capture | Anyone in range, whether or not they fought | Each marked player with at least 5% of the damage, each with their own roll |
| What sets the odds | 40% + 0.5 per Trapping level + 0.2 per Woodcutting level − 5 per level of combat gap | Trap tier against land tier, a Trapping curve per tier, element and unaware bonuses, pity |
| What a failure costs | The 20-gold trap; 5 XP | Only time; the player gains XP and better odds next time |
| What the kill pays | Nothing to anyone; the monster is removed | XP, gold, loot and quest credit as normal, with the pet on top |
| What you see | A result message | The chance before the tap; server-rolled wobbles drawn in code |
| Where Trapping XP comes from | Capture attempts only, ending at six pets | Trap checks and materials, fixed per tier, plus captures |
| Where a new pet goes | Refused once six are held | Active slots, then a farm stable that grows with Trapping level |

Some of this is repair rather than redesign, and it should land first. Add `flora` to the server's pet element list so Verdant Wilds pets keep their element across logins. Give the server a release path or a farm stable so Trapping can level past six pets. Pay kill credit to contributors whenever a capture happens. Spend the trap only on success. Stop adopting client-claimed pet lists, and mint stable pet ids with provenance before any pet can move between players. Move the client-only evolve and enchant onto the server as one atomic, idempotent operation, so the duplicate-merging the client already sketches survives a relog ([pets.js](../../../server/src/pets.js); [gameSystems.js](../../../src/data/gameSystems.js)). Each new path should ship behind a capability flag and kill switch, the convention every BroTown system follows ([CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md)). Pets themselves should grow along the cheap roles: the follower and pickup radius they already have, a capped elemental resistance, a level that grows with the owner's play and stops at the owner's level, a bond with a daily cap and no decay, a Trapper's Log per land, tinted variants and farm timers. Simulated combat companions and mounts come last, if ever.

These conclusions rest on uneven evidence. The code-read figures are firm: Pokémon Emerald's decompiled capture routine, Pokémon GO's current game master, RuneLite's Hunter plugin and BroTown's own server. Most other numbers come from search-engine digests of wiki and forum pages that the research proxy blocked; they are probably right but unchecked, and a few conflict or stay open, including Monster Hunter's threshold (15–25% in one source, 30% in another), whether WoW's per-failure trap bonus adds or multiplies, and Pokémon GO's ball multipliers, which the current game master does not carry. Player sentiment rests on thread titles and summaries rather than counts, and no source reported retention data for any capture design. Several remembered precedents for touch-screen and full-HP capture could not be verified and were left out. The proposed numbers here (mark length, pity size, catch curves, trap timers, variant odds) are starting points to tune in play, not findings.

## Conclusion

The owner's one-hit problem and the shared world's stealing problem turn out to be the same bug: any capture that can be erased by dealing too much damage, whether by the player or by a stranger, gets worse as players grow stronger and the room fills up, while designs that ignore damage or convert it into progress get better with both. That points to a rule bigger than any one mechanic. In a server-authoritative game, a capture should be decided at an event the worker already owns and records — the kill, with its damage ledger, or the trap check, with its timestamp — never at a moment players race each other for. Fairness (a roll for each contributor), cheat-resistance (the worker rolls) and server cost (nothing ticks between events) then come from the same choice instead of three separate fixes.

The second lesson is about where scarcity should live. Old School RuneScape keeps pets rare and catches common, and its skill is carried by what each level unlocks. BroTown's demo did the reverse: every catch was a pet, so the six-pet cap halted the skill and the dice decided everything. Making ordinary catches plentiful (materials and eggs from traps, pets from armed kills backed by pity) and keeping rarity for tiers, tinted variants and the log lets preparation and Trapping level carry the progression. Almost everything this needs already exists in BroTown's worker: the node bake, the gathering gates and gesture, the damage shares, the farm, the forge's metals, the cookhouse and the tint. The expensive options (simulated combat pets, mounts and open pet markets) also have the worst records elsewhere, which makes the order of the work easy to choose.
