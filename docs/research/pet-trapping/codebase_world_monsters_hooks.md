## 1. Monster roster in the Wheel (zone id 'wheel')

### How the roster is built
- `WHEEL.HOMES` = frost, ember, sky, hollows, thunder, tidal, mist, verdant (server/src/wheelzone.js:92). Each land's spawn list is its OLD element zone's `ZONES[home].spawns` (server/src/data.js:341-375), built through the single stat function `_makeZoneMonster` (server/src/index.js:1512-1582) and moved to baked places on the spoke (wheelzone.js:189-243).
- Places are baked into server/src/wheelspawns.js (GENERATED by tools/world/bake-wheel-spawns.mjs; header lines 1-13). Per land: `anchor`, `band`, 12 `points` for tier 1 (`[x,y,depth]`), and `deeper[]` tiers 2..8 each `{tier, levels:[lo,hi], band, points}` (e.g. frost wheelspawns.js:22-39). Six monsters per tier use the first 6 points (`k % points.length`, wheelzone.js:199/228).
- 'wheel' is NOT in `ZONES` (data.js:412-415 comment; only in VALID_ZONE_IDS); `_getZoneConfig('wheel')` is null (index.js:1283-1299) so no clamps, no population scaler, no random nodes (wheelzone.js:19-26).
- `_spawnZoneMonsters('wheel')` delegates to `_wheelSpawnMonsters()` (index.js:1480-1482).
- Kill switches: `wheelmonsters:false` (wheelzone.js:142-145), `wheeldeep:false` (tiers 2+; :179-182), `wheelpast20:false` (tiers 5-8; :174-177, :223).

### Respawn
- `RESPAWN_TIME = 18750` ms (index.js:937). `_monsterRespawnMs(zone)` returns RESPAWN_TIME unless the zone is population-scalable (spawnscale.js:193-200); `_spawnScalableZone('wheel')` is false (no zone config, spawnscale.js:136-140) => **every Wheel monster respawns 18.75 s after death, flat**, at its own spawnX/Y.
- Kill sets `m.respawnAt = m.noRespawn ? 0 : now + _monsterRespawnMs(zone)` (combat.js:1592). Pet capture does the same (pets.js:194). Respawn resets statuses, dmgByPlayer, burrow/burst state, variant (index.js:1880-1932).

### Fields a monster object carries
Built by `_makeZoneMonster` (index.js:1551-1581): `id, arch, variant, spawnVariant, spawnSpd, level, element (home zone's element), hp, maxHp, dmg, xp, gold, spd, emoji, color, x, y, spawnX, spawnY, alive, targetId, atkCd, respawnAt`. ## 2. Monster art and memory

### How a Wheel monster's look is loaded and drawn
- `src/rendering/wheelMonsterArt.js` (v2.3.2989, header :1-29). A "look" = everything one monster type is drawn from: a variant's sheets (VARIANT_SPRITES) + any recolour, the mummy WITH the skeleton it turns into, or the snowman's own sheets + snowball burst (:24-28, `looksOf` :106-112). Slime/generic bodies are GLOBAL art: "nothing to load, always ready" (:28-29, :107-108 returns [] for non-variant archetypes).
- Per frame the renderer calls `wheelArtTick(S, S.monsters, now)` (entityRenderer.js:8239) which, every 250 ms (`TICK_MS` :82), wants a look when one of ITS MONSTERS (the `monsters` list only) is within `LOAD_R` 2,600 px on your own land (:44, :246-253), another land's only inside `foreignBox` (screen half-size + 700, min 1,000; :61-81, :254-258); frees a look none of whose monsters has been within `FREE_R` 3,600 px for `FREE_AFTER` 10 s (:48-49, :262-268). Freeing respects shared modules (thornShambler/rockmonster, bogLurker/fishman) (:126-145).
- **The draw gate**: `if (_artOn && !wheelArtReady(arch))` the monster's display is DESTROYED (not hidden) and it is not drawn at all, never in a stand-in body (entityRenderer.js:8338-8360; `wheelArtReady` wheelMonsterArt.js:117-124). The lazy first-sighting kick is OFF in the Wheel (`setVariantKicks(false)`, wheelMonsterArt.js:227; monsterVariantSprites.js:178-191).
- Far culling: past the view half-diagonal + `FAR_MARGIN` 400 px a Wheel monster is hidden and skipped (entityRenderer.js:8273-8294).
- Outside the Wheel (old zones) looks load per zone behind the overlay (`preloadZoneAssets`, preloadAnimations.js:87-123) and are freed on exit except what the destination uses (`freeZoneAssets`, preloadAnimations.js:220-260). A Wheel dungeon loads the land's looks behind its loading screen (`loadLandLooks`, wheelMonsterArt.js:199-205).
- Loaders are ALL-OR-NOTHING per look: e.g. `loadFireGoblinSprites()` loads walk x4 + attack x5 + hit x4 + death + fireball + remnants (fireGoblinSprites.js:43-45, :117-133). Strips go through `loadTrackedStrip` (zoneTextures.js:93-125): decoded via Image, CROPPED to the art by `packTrimmed` into a NEW 2D canvas (gearSheets.js:163-252; canvas made at :242) and registered with `Texture.from(canvas)` (zoneTextures.js:103) => the pixels sit in Pixi's Cache (counted by `__btTex`, pixiApp.js:343-371 = the `artCache` budget line), as a live 2D canvas (memprobe hooks `document.createElement('canvas')`, tools/qa/mp/memprobe.mjs:26-29 = the `canvases` line), and on the GPU once drawn (`gpu` line). Cropping saves 27-40% of each cell (zoneTextures.js:72-77). `keepOnGpuOnly` is NOT applied to monster strips (only effectsRenderer.js:2098, :12052).

### THE PET QUESTION: what would drawing a captured pet as its monster sprite cost?
- **It would pin the WHOLE look**, every direction and state, because the loaders are all-or-nothing per look and the crops are made per strip (fireGoblinSprites.js:117-133; zoneTextures.js:93-125). There is no "load one frame" path.
- **Nothing today would keep it loaded for a pet**: `wheelArtTick` only looks at `S.monsters` (wheelMonsterArt.js:220-268), so a pet's look would be freed 10 s after the last wild monster of that type left 3,600 px, and in the Wheel the draw gate would then refuse to draw it (entityRenderer.js:8345). On any zone change `freeZoneAssets` drops looks the destination does not use (preloadAnimations.js:220-236), and a dungeon only keeps its land's looks. A pet look needs its own pin (a `keep` source) and its own free.
- **Cost against the budget at the Flame Fields fight** (the check's worst stop, goblin look already in): a frost snowman pet adds ~13-17.5 MB (decoded, cropped somewhat less) to artCache and the same to canvases -> over both lines (8.6 / 6.1 MB headroom). A sky mummy pet pins mummy+skeleton (~36 MB) -> far over. A fire goblin pet elsewhere (e.g. at Frost Ridge) ~30 MB. Rock monster / fishman / bog lurker pets ~1 MB; slime-family pets (thunder slime, mire wisp) 0 MB extra; blue slime ~4 MB. Other players' pets would multiply this (every distinct species nearby pins a look).
- iPhone Safari kills the tab at ~250 MB of the asset cache (wheelMonsterArt.js:50-56; docs/specs/memory-in-the-wheel.md:18-20).

## 3. Combat hooks

### Damage gate and damage sources
- ONE predicate for "may this monster take damage": `_monsterDamageable(m, now)` = alive && hp>0 && not `_invulnUntil` (combat.js:166-169). Call sites listed at combat.js:162-165: `_handleMonsterDamage`, elemental collision, `_applyMonsterDot` (index.js:3116), block-thorns (telegraph.js:956), `_abilityStrikeMonster` (abilities.js:690/730/806/1087), arrow blast (arrowblast.js:174), and pet capture (pets.js:144).
- Main hit handler `_handleMonsterDamage(session, payload)` (combat.js:986-1471): client sends only intent {monsterId, zone, element, slot, special, orbs, part, noKb}; server rolls damage (`_computeAttackDamage`, combat.js:1165), caps (`_maxDmgForAttacker` :1167), fracture mult (:1209), overkill clamp (:1210), `m.hp -= actualDmg` (:1214). Gates: attacker exists, not dying/disconnected, `attackerPs.z === zone` (:1015-1030), ends any harvest (:1037), melee <= `PVE_MELEE_RANGE` 400 px (index.js:871), Wheel ranged/staff <= `WHEEL.RANGED_MAX` 3,600 px (:1094-1111), hit cadence 210 ms normal / 3 specials per 1,200 ms per (player, monster) (:1133-1156).
- Credit: `m.dmgByPlayer[pid] += actualDmg` (null-proto map, :1225-1226); `attackerPs._lastDealtAt = now` (:1235) (used by `_wheelProvokedBy`). Trained-skill XP per hit `_prog3AwardXp` (:1244-1251).
- Sticky aggro: every hit sets `m._aggroOverrideTarget = pid; m._aggroOverrideUntil = now + 10000` (:1333-1334); with it active the effective aggro range is 1,200 px (index.js:2245).
- Knockback 30 / crit 45 / special 60 px x depth, recorded as `_kbDebt` repaid while chasing (combat.js:1377-1400; `KB_RECOVER_PX_PER_TICK` 1.1 at index.js:960).
- Other monster-HP writers: abilities.js (`_abilityStrikeMonster`, kill at :923), telegraph.js thorns reflect (:956-971) and the slime burst replay (:717), DoT ticks (index.js:3116-3156 -> `_resolveMonsterKill(...,'dot')`), staff splash/big bolt (combat.js:1453-1465), arrow blast, Element Burst (burst.js).

### Kill path `_resolveMonsterKill(zone, m, killerId, killerPs, slot)` (combat.js:1551-1801)
1. Blue-slime burst DEFERS the kill: `_startSlimeBurst` (combat.js:1563; telegraph.js:656-683; SLIME_BURST {SWELL_MS 1600, RADIUS 110, DMG 60} telegraph.js:300-308) — kill replays after the blast with the same killer.
2. `m.alive=false`; event-cape ticket roll (:1574-1583); respawn stamp `noRespawn ? 0 : now + _monsterRespawnMs(zone)` (:1592).
3. Shares: every `dmgByPlayer` contributor alive, connected, in the kill zone gets share = dmg/total; gold only if share >= 0.05; fallback to last hit (:1598-1618).

## 6. THE ONE-HIT PROBLEM, QUANTIFIED (coordinator task)

### The damage formula the server rolls (server/src/combat.js `_computeAttackDamage` :754-984)
`pre = (weaponBase + skillLevel x DMG_PER_LEVEL) x PowerMult x tierMult^1.5 x qualityMult` (:820)
- weaponBase: greatsword 10, sword 6.67, bow 12.80, staff 13.44 (gear.js `_weaponBase` T at :65); + hardness x 1.0417 (hardening.js:74-78).
- DMG_PER_LEVEL sword 1.5 / bow 1.5 / staff 1.8 per TRAINED skill level (prog3.js:435; skill cap 100, prog3.js:102).
- PowerMult = 1 + 1.0 x curve(pts x edge, k=7), curve(q,k)=q/(q+k) (prog3.js:362, :516-518, :984-986): 5 pts x1.42, 10 x1.59, 20 x1.74, 40 x1.85; points 3 per lane level (prog3.js:129 `POINTS_PER_LEVEL` 3; + 3 shared, :141), damage stats capped at 2 x level (`lvlBound` 2, prog3.js:212-214, :1256). EDGE: Power/Luck/Special fade 20%/level when the MONSTER is above your skill, 0 at +5 (prog3.js:508, :519-527) — never against lower monsters.
- tier factor tierMult^1.5 (data.js:879-890): copper 1.12 -> x1.19, iron 1.25 -> x1.40, black steel 1.40 -> x1.66, titanium 1.56, obsidian 1.74, mythril 1.94, diamond 2.16, abyssal 2.40 (data.js BLACKSMITH_TIERS :757-776, wood :757 ... steel :767 ... abyssal :772; WOODWORKING_TIERS :791-800; woods pine 1.00, softwood 1.12, hardwood 1.25, cedar 1.40, maple 1.56, ironbark 1.74, crystalwood 1.94). Equip gate: 5 trained levels per rung above the starting tier, iron exempt (gear.js:280-335).
- quality grade on the whole hit: normal 1 / rare 1.3 / elite 1.75 / godly 5 (data.js:860-869); odds 9% / 0.9% / 1-in-2,000,000 (hardening.js:55-66).
- variance band: greatsword/sword 0.75-1.25, bow 0.6-0.8, staff 0.5-1.65 (combat.js:877-879).
- crit: chance 1% + 60% x curve(luck pts), mult 1.5 + 2.0 x curve, and a crit is ANCHORED to >= 2 x the top of the band (combat.js:676-678, :943-944; prog3.js:973-978) => a crit is >= 2.5x / 1.6x / 3.3x the pre-variance base for sword / bow / staff.
- specials: melee & bow x3, staff x2 (combat.js:895) x Special stat (1 + 1.5 x curve, prog3.js:393, :979-981); staff BIG BOLT = one roll from band 0.3-2.5 x2 x 3 orbs (combat.js:125-129, :1088-1093, :1165-1168) = 1.8x-15x base, mean 8.4x; bow VOLLEY = 3 arrows x (3x roll x 2/3) (combat.js:130, :1190-1196).
- other multipliers: volatile x1.3 (:903), damage buff x1.2 cooked / x2.0 whetstone (shop 35 g, data.js:500; combat.js:908-911), flame-amulet elemDmg (:977-981); plus staff splash 50% (:66, :1453), big-bolt blast 1/3 (:128), Element Burst x1.5 (prog3.js:498 `BURST_DMG_MULT`), burn/root DoT ticks (elemental.js:193-209), elemental collisions up to `COLLISION_BURST_CAP` 3.2x (elemental.js:38).
- The server CEILING `_maxDmgForAttacker` = (maxWeaponDmg x critMult) x comboBoost 5 x specialMult (combat.js:680-734) is >= 7.5x a base hit — it never limits ordinary hits, so it is irrelevant to one-shots (it only clips forged claims).
- Fresh character, starter kit (skill 1, Copper Great Sword / Pine Bow / Pine Staff, no points): mean basic hit greatsword 13.6, bow 10.0, staff 16.4; specials greatsword 40.9, bow volley 60.1, staff big bolt 128 (spread ~28-231) — matches the code's own note (combat.js:76-92).

### What it means (with the arithmetic)
- A basic hit of size h can only be GUARANTEED to stop in the window if h <= 20% of max HP (then some hit must leave 0 < HP <= 20%). For h between 20% and 100% the chance is roughly 0.2/h-per-crossing and depends on the variance; for h >= 100% it is 0. At-level play already sits at h ~ 20-45% of max HP (a kill in ~3-5 swings, the design target: "Fought at your level ... a kill stays ~4 swings", data.js:44-46), so even on-level the window is hit only ~50-90%.
- Hit sizes that make "land exactly in 0-20%" unlikely: any CRIT (>= 2x the band top: a greatsword crit on an at-level monster is ~60-110% of its HP), any SPECIAL (staff big bolt mean 8.4x base: one-shots at-level monsters 77-94%; bow volley 100% through L25), splash/blast/burst, a whetstone (x2) or elite/godly weapon (x1.75 / x5), and every fight more than ~5 levels below you (Table B col c). Luck builds halve the landing rate (Table C col 2).
- Extra risk the HP gate cannot see: DoT ticks (burn/root), other players' hits (credit is per-damage, party.js:4-11), the snowman's 50%-HP burrow makes it untargetable (`_invulnUntil`, telegraph.js:190-215), the mummy becomes a 1.75-speed skeleton on first damage (index.js:1943-1962), and the blue slime's death is a 60-dmg explosion (telegraph.js:300-308).
- Conclusion for the planner: an HP-window capture works only for under-levelled or at-level players using basic hits; pet collectors returning to low tiers (the most common pattern) would one-shot everything from skill ~10 (Lv 1-2 band), ~25 (Lv 8), ~35-45 (Lv 15-20).

## 7. HOOKS FOR A CAPTURE THAT DOES NOT DEPEND ON HP (coordinator task)

### Unaware / idle state before aggro (for a sneak bonus) — EXISTS server-side
- A monster with no target is idle: `m.targetId === null` and no live sticky override (`_aggroOverrideUntil`) (index.js:2136-2172). Idle wander walks to `m._wanderTx/_wanderTy` (picked 30-80 px away, clamped to 0.8 x `WANDER_LEASH` 180 px of spawn) and PAUSES 0.5-1.5 s on arrival (`_wanderPausedUntil`) (index.js:2409-2500, leash :2449, pick :2473-2490, pause :2497).
- Aggro happens within 120 px (snowman 300) x depth of the nearest eligible player (index.js:938, :954, :2239-2245, :2262-2263); the first hit sets a 10 s sticky override at up to 1,200 px (combat.js:1333-1334; index.js:2245).
- On the wire: `tg` = the target player id, explicit `null` for 2 s after losing one (tick.js:439-471); the client draws a "!" notice on acquisition (tick.js:440-449 note). So "unaware" is known to both sides already.
- A player who is EXTRACTING is invisible to target acquisition (`p.extracting`, index.js:1847, :2121-2172) — incl. cooking/firemaking with no node, anchored at the server-stamped `ps._exX/_exY` (gathering.js:689-722). A trap-SETTING gesture could reuse this shield exactly (monsters ignore you while you set it).
- No stealth/sneak/back-attack mechanic exists anywhere (grep stealth|sneak|crouch|backstab|flank: only unrelated comments).

### Facing (for a back-attack bonus) — NOT tracked for monsters
- The server keeps no monster facing (no `m.facing`/`m.dir` in server/src). It knows each tick's movement (chase step toward `nearest`, wander toward `_wanderTx/Ty`, knockback), so a facing could be DERIVED (last move vector, or direction to `targetId`). The client derives monster facing from motion for drawing. Players DO have a server facing for block arcs (`_blockArcCovers(ps, atkX, atkY)`, e.g. index.js:1722, :2088).

### Holds that PLAYERS can already apply (all come WITH damage today)
- Element statuses from the weapon's element: frost FREEZE 3 s (max 5), venom ROOT 5 s (+DoT), wind SLOW x0.4 5 s (elemental.js:25-27, :115-150); freeze/root => no move AND no attack (`ccMoveMult` 0, index.js:1986-1987, :2273, wander :2457).
- STUN `_stunUntil` (cancels wind-ups, delays atkCd): sword dash 1.6 s (free, cd 2.5 s), shield bash 1.6 s (1 block, cd 4 s, needs shield), whirlwind 1.0 s + PULL every monster within 240 px onto a ring 34 px from you (1 block, cd 6 s) (abilities.js:103-205, `pullTo: 34` :208, stamp :893-900). Stun is on the wire as `st` (tick.js:409-428) and drawn as a star ring.
- None of these is a "sleep"; there is no non-damaging CC. A "net/snare" item would be a new server action using the same `_stunUntil`/statuses fields.

### Steering AI toward a point (bait) — no lure exists; two seams
- Monsters only target PLAYERS (nearest or sticky, index.js:2136-2172). The idle-wander target `_wanderTx/_wanderTy` is a free point the AI already walks to (index.js:2473-2500) — a bait could set it, but it is clamped to 144 px of spawn, and the Wheel chase leash 720 / provoked 1,800 px and safe ground apply (index.js:2246-2261).
- The whirlwind pull is the only code that moves monsters to a chosen point (abilities.js:198-210); knockback the other (combat.js:1377-1400).
- A lure would be a new override analogous to `_aggroOverrideTarget/Until` (point instead of player), consumed in `_tickMonsters` before the wander branch.

### Non-hostile critters / wildlife — NONE
- Every server monster is a hostile archetype (data.js:295-304); nothing passive spawns. Client-only ambient life: fireflies/pollen/spore motes (src/rendering/worldFx.js:59-65, :612-635), the fishing spots' fish drawn in code (wheelNodes.js `WheelFish`, CLAUDE.md v2.3.3012), birds only in ambient sound (gameDisplay.js:1670). ### How drop tables work — there is no drops*.js; rolls are lanes on the loot pile
- `_spawnLootForKill` (index.js:3831-3969) builds ONE pile per kill with fixed lanes: coins (by share), the one-of INVENTORY slot (skull/shard/gem — first eligible picker), the WEAPON lane (own claim flag), the ARMOUR lane (own claim flag). Recipients = gold recipients (share >= 5%). Weapon/armour/gem roll only if someone can claim (:3890-3919). A drop not added to the "nothing of value" early-out vanishes on a monster with no gold/skull (:3926-3929; drops.test.mjs point 7).
- Killer-only rolls ride the XP loop in `_resolveMonsterKill` (nugget, raw gem, combat.js:1713-1720), credited straight into ps and flushed.
- Rare/elite/godly items appear as MYSTERY icons until pickup (`_isMysteryGrade`/`_pileHasMystery`, index.js:3742-3765) and play a reveal ceremony (src/ui/reveal/RevealOverlay.jsx) — reusable for a pet-rarity reveal.
- The suite: server/test/drops.test.mjs (rates asserted against the constants AND the roll with Math.random stubbed; independent rolls; claim lanes; server-side credit; early-out).
- => A rare "pet egg" fits as (a) a new pile lane like `gem`/`armor` with its own claim flag and the mystery reveal, or (b) a killer-only roll beside the nugget/raw-gem rolls (combat.js:1713-1720), keyed by the monster's `home`/`variant`/`level`/`tier`; a "tame chance on kill" fits at the same place (before `m.dmgByPlayer` is reset, combat.js:1800). The capture's own no-loot removal is pets.js:186-195.
