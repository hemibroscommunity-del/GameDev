How the test works

  1. Save your bro's picture below. Attach it to every ChatGPT chat for the test's looks, so each is drawn to match him. (The chosen look attaches the style key instead.)
  2. Round 1 (one evening): for each look, copy the Ground and Objects prompts into two new chats and bring the pictures back as version A. The two looks that borrow pictures need none of their own.
  3. Preview each look and walk around. Try night, rain and the open dashboard. Score it. Keep the best two or three.
  4. Round 2: for those, make version B of the ground and objects (a fresh chat each, same prompt), plus the Building and the NPC. Score again. The A-and-B line says whether ChatGPT can keep the look up for hundreds of pictures.
  5. Sleep on it, then show three friends the previews without saying which is which. Copy your results and send them to Claude.
