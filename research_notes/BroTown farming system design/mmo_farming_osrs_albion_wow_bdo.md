## 1. OSRS and RS3: growth cycles, grow times, disease and its prevention, watering, Farming XP, herbs feeding Herblore, Tithe Farm, Farming Contracts, farm runs, RS3 Player-Owned Farm

### Takeaway
OSRS farming is a real-time, offline-growing, many-patch "check-in" skill: fixed server clocks (5/10/20/40-minute cycles) grow crops whether or not you are online, a herb run takes ~5-10 minutes and repeats every ~80-90 minutes, disease risk is bought down with compost tiers (50/80/90% less) or with in-kind gardener payments, and its main value to combat players is the herb -> Herblore potion pipeline (e.g. ranarr -> prayer potions), which is player-made and traded. RS3's Player-Owned Farm (livestock that grows offline) shows the failure mode of rewarding idle time: Jagex cut its XP to 50% after launch.

### Cited Findings
**Growth model and offline growth**
- Planted crops keep growing in real time while the player is logged out, and can become diseased while the player is offline — [OSRS Wiki: Farming](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Farming)
- Growth "cycles" by crop type: flowers and saplings 5 min; allotments 10 min (the "2nd fastest"); herbs and bushes 20 min; trees, mushroom spores and coral 40 min. Each player has a personal offset of up to 30 minutes that persists through logins — [OSRS Wiki: Farming](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Farming); [Allotment patch](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Allotment_patch)
- Farming Timer Rework, 15 February 2018 (date per search summary of the wiki update page): the original 2005 timer code let crops MISS growth ticks depending on when a player logged in/out or hopped worlds, "slowing down the patch potentially by hours or days". The game's clocks tick every 5, 10, 20, 40 minutes and so on (weeds/flowers on the 5-minute tick, allotments on the 10-minute tick). After the rework, logging in and out "should no longer stop a tick from happening", so "once a seed is in the ground, players will know roughly how long it'll take" — [Official OSRS news post](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/farming-timer-rework--skilling-qol?oldschool=1); [OSRS Wiki: Update page](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Update:Farming_Timer_Rework_&_Skilling_QoL)

**Grow times**
- Herbs take ~80 minutes to grow — [Herb patch](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Herb_patch); [osrstools: "Farming Money Making Every 80 Minutes"](https://www.osrstools.net/guides/money-making/herb-runs)
- Potato (allotment) 40 min; oak tree 160 min; yew 365 min (~6.1 h, as the snippet gives it); magic tree 8 h (480 min); all fruit trees 16 h — [Potato plant](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Potato_plant); [Oak tree (Farming)](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Oak_tree_(Farming)); [Yew tree (Farming)](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Yew_tree_(Farming)); [Magic tree (Farming)](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Magic_tree_(Farming)); [Fruit tree patch](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Fruit_tree_patch)

**Disease, compost, watering and paid protection**
- Compost reduces disease chance by 50%, supercompost by 80%, ultracompost by 90% (rounded down to the nearest 1/128) — [Disease (Farming)](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Disease_(Farming)); [Ultracompost](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Ultracompost)
- Herbs: base 27/128 (~21.1%) per growth cycle; compost 14/128 (~10.9%); supercompost 6/128 (~4.7%); ultracompost 3/128 (~2.3%). Fruit trees: base 18/128 (~14.1%); compost 9/128; supercompost 4/128; ultracompost 2/128 (~1.6%) — [Disease (Farming)](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Disease_(Farming)). CONFLICT: a guide says ultracompost takes herbs "from 20.3% disease chance per stage to only 2.9%" and the chance of death to 8.3% — [The Oatrix herb guide](https://www.theoatrix.net/post/complete-herb-farming-guide-for-osrs-herb-run-guide); prefer the wiki's 1/128 fractions (the guide may describe an older formula).
- A living Iasor plant reduces all crops' disease risk by a further 80%, to a minimum of 1/128 per cycle — [Disease (Farming)](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Disease_(Farming))
- WATERING: watering allotment, flower and hops patches with a watering can reduces disease risk (herbs and trees are not watered) — [Disease (Farming)](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Disease_(Farming))
- Herb patches cannot be protected by paying a gardener; the only protection is a disease-free patch — [The Oatrix herb guide](https://www.theoatrix.net/post/complete-herb-farming-guide-for-osrs-herb-run-guide)
- Five disease-free herb patches, each unlocked by progression rather than purchase: Troll Stronghold (quest My Arm's Big Adventure), Weiss (Making Friends with My Arm), Hosidius (Easy Kourend & Kebos Diary), Harmony Island (Elite Morytania Diary), Civitas illa Fortis (16,000 Glory in the Fortis Colosseum). The Farming Guild's herb patch (Farming 65) is a normal patch — [Herb patch](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Herb_patch); [Herb patch (troll)](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Herb_patch_(troll)); [Harmony Island](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Harmony_Island)
- Gardener "payments" are in kind, per patch, in the exact form requested (noted items accepted), and remove disease risk for that growth: potatoes = 2 buckets of compost; onions = 1 sack of potatoes; watermelons = 10 curry leaves — [Potato plant](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Potato_plant); [Onion plant](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Onion_plant); [Allotment patch/Seeds](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Allotment_patch/Seeds)
- Trees: palm = 15 papayas; magic = 25 coconuts; spirit tree = 5 monkey nuts + 1 monkey bar + 1 ground suqah tooth; saplings in plant pots cannot get diseased; the farmer will also clear a grown tree for 200 coins — [Palm sapling](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Palm_sapling); [Magic tree (Farming)](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Magic_tree_(Farming)); [Spirit Tree Patch](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Spirit_Tree_Patch); [Tree patch](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Tree_patch)
- A fully grown matching flower in the neighbouring flower patch protects an allotment from disease, as an alternative to paying — [Allotment patch/Seeds](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Allotment_patch/Seeds); [Marigold seed](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Marigold_seed)

**RS3 Player-Owned Farm (Manor Farm)**

- It was #7 of the "12 Most Wanted Updates of 2017" announced at RuneFest 2016, and Jagex called it "one of the most sought after requests from the community Build-a-Backlog poll". ### Inferences
- OSRS's core loop is "plant -> leave -> come back": crops grow on server clocks, offline, and the player's job is a short circuit of many patches. That is close to the owner's FarmVille-style timers and suits iPhone sessions. The 2018 rework is the lesson: growth must come from wall-clock time since planting, computed on the server, never from ticks that run only while a player is online.
- Disease is OSRS's risk layer, and every mitigation is an earlier ACTION: compost (the "fertilize" step), watering (allotments only), a protective flower, or paying a gardener in another crop. That maps neatly onto the owner's dig / plant / fertilize / water steps. Each step can lower a visible risk or raise yield, so none of them is busywork.
- In-kind gardener payments (compost for potatoes, potatoes for onions, papayas for palms) build demand between crops inside the farming economy.
- Farming feeds combat in OSRS through potions. Herbs come from both farming and monster drops, so farming is the steady supply, not the only one. A low-level herb (ranarr) became one of the most valuable because it makes a staple combat potion, which shows a farmed consumable can carry a crop's value.
- Plots are earned through progress (quests, diaries, levels, minigame currency), never bought; "better" plots are disease-free plots. A paid model (the owner's "paid expansions") has no OSRS precedent.
- RS3 POF warns that offline growth paying full XP/rewards for little activity got nerfed by 50%. Pay mainly for the active steps (planting, tending, harvesting), and let idle time only enable them.

## 2. Albion Online: personal islands, plots, 22-hour crops, Focus watering and seed return, access rights, food and potions, market effects

### Takeaway
Albion is the clearest case of farming as the backbone of combat consumables: nearly every item, all potions and food included, is player-made, and herbs, crops and animal products come from island farms. Personal islands are bought and upgraded with escalating silver (1M up to 26.5M cumulative, with a steep first-time discount), need Premium to buy, run every crop on one 22-hour cycle, and "watering" spends a daily-regenerating Focus budget to return seeds, so the farm is a once-a-day check-in whose profit depends on Premium. The results: recurring complaints that yields were cut and margins are thin, bots pressing on raw-material prices, and a 2023 rework (Wild Blood) that lets one character own an island per city, each with its own crop/animal bonuses meant to push trade.

- Bots: a player report of fishing bots making "300m silver in few days" across several blue zones and pulling resource prices down — [Forum: 300m silver in Few days [bots]](https://forum.albiononline.com/index.php/Thread/216264-300m-silver-in-Few-days-bots/)

### Inferences
- Albion shows farming can carry ALL of a combat game's consumables, but only with a market and many farmers. Every potion and food is player-made, so a combat player either farms or buys from farmers. This is the strongest precedent for the owner's idea of replacing NPC-sold potions with farmed ones, and it also shows the cost: prices are set by players, so they swing and can crash.
- One 22-hour timer for every crop gives a clean once-a-day rhythm (plant, come back tomorrow), and the slightly-under-a-day length lets a player keep the same daily slot instead of drifting later each day.
- Focus watering is a daily-capped action budget that sets how much one character can farm efficiently. It also tied farming profit to Premium (10,000 Focus a day, doubled yield), which players called "pay 2 win". "Watering with Alts" shows per-character caps get dodged with alt characters. A cap should be per account and should not turn a subscription into a doubling of output.
- Paid expansion precedent: soft-currency upgrades with steep escalation (1M to 8M per level), plus a heavy first-purchase discount (20,000 silver) so new players can start. The owner's "free starter plot, then expansions" matches this shape.
- Visiting: Albion's role list (Visitor / Builder / Co-Owner, per player, guild or alliance) is the most developed model among the four for friends visiting or helping on a private farm.
- When farming is cheap to enter and doubled by a subscription, raw-crop prices sink toward the planting cost (the "already depressed farming economy"). The developer's answer was specialization (each island and city favours different crops and animals) so players trade instead of all growing the same thing.

### Gaps
- No time series of crop or herb prices, so "price crash" is supported only by complaints and press wording, not data.
- Official confirmation of the 7-day Premium rule and of current per-level plot counts after Wild Blood (2023) was not obtained.
- Whether herbs exist anywhere besides island farms (mob drops were the pre-2016 source) is not confirmed.
- What happens to crops and animals when Premium lapses (growth vs. harvest restrictions) is not documented beyond "profit will be very low".
- The per-crop watering bonuses look inconsistent (carrot +200% vs cabbage +40%); the full table was not readable.

## 3. World of Warcraft: Sunsong Ranch and the Tillers (Mists of Pandaria), the Garrison herb garden, and later equivalents

### Takeaway
Sunsong Ranch (patch 5.0.4, 2012) was a phased, solo personal farm. Its plots grew from 4 to 16 through Tillers reputation (earned, not bought), seeds were bought for silver, crops ripened in about a day, and random "problem" states were each fixed by a one-click verb or a tiny fight. The harvest fed Cooking (the top +300-stat, 1-hour foods need farm vegetables), converted into Ironpaw Tokens, and could be planted as other professions' materials (herbs, ore, enchanting dust, Motes of Harmony). It was well liked and led to the Garrison and later housing. Its phasing meant friends could not see or help on your farm, and Blizzard later said the Garrison isolated players from the world and became a daily "laundry list of chores".

### Cited Findings
**Personal, phased space**
- Sunsong Ranch (western Halfhill, Valley of the Four Winds), introduced in patch 5.0.4, was "the first area in the game to use phasing technology to give players a personal, semi-customizable space in the game world"; through quests and reputation players upgrade the farm and choose what to plant every day and which NPC to spend time with; the concept "would subsequently evolve into the garrison of Warlords of Draenor and ultimately player housing" — [Warcraft Wiki: Sunsong Ranch](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sunsong_Ranch); [Wowpedia: Sunsong Ranch](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Sunsong_Ranch)
- Phasing: other players fade out as they enter the ranch from your point of view; each player sees only their own plants and progress. The search summary concludes party members cannot see or tend each other's farms — [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Sunsong_Ranch); [WoWWiki archive](https://wowwiki-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Sunsong_Ranch); bug thread on phasing persisting through relogs: [Blizzard forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/sunsong-ranch-plots-still-phased-when-logging-inout-on-the-farm/1057477)

**Plots, seeds, growth**

- Threads/blogs found but not readable in full: "What's the worst crop problem?" — [MMO-Champion](https://www.mmo-champion.com/threads/1333166-What-s-the-worst-crop-problem); "Farming in World of Warcraft. ### Inferences
- WoW put farming into combat through food, not potions: the best stat food needs farm vegetables (25 Green Cabbage a batch), but vegetables could also be bought or swapped through Ironpaw Tokens, so the farm was the cheap, steady source rather than a gate. This fits the owner's "temporary stat boosts".
- Plots were unlocked by reputation, not money, so every expansion was a reward for engagement. That is the opposite of Albion's silver-priced expansions.
- The problem states are almost exactly the owner's tending verbs (water, spray, weed, lift) plus two that start a tiny fight. They made a daily check-in feel like play, and 5.2 made the annoying one (Wild) rarer. Bulk tools (seed bags for 4 plots, Master Plow) cut tapping as the farm grew, which matters on a phone.
- A ~24-hour ripen time plus a "bonus crop of the day" gave a reason to log in daily without punishing a missed day.
- Phasing gave privacy but no social play: friends could neither see nor help. For the owner's "friends can visit", WoW's 2012 farm is a counter-example; its 2025-26 neighborhoods move toward shared space.
- The Garrison lesson is direct: if the private farm becomes where power comes from, it drains the shared world and turns into a chore list. Farm rewards should make a combat player's life easier, not be required.

- Whether an unfixed problem state lowers yield or kills the crop, and whether crops wither if not harvested, was not captured.
- Exact ripening rule (24 hours after planting vs the daily reset) is unclear: sources say both "next day" and "after 24 hours".
- No player-count or usage data for the farm or garrison; the "popular" claim rests on a low-authority retrospective and wiki wording.
- Whether a group leader's farm could be shared (any party phasing) was not confirmed beyond the wiki's "only you" description.

## 4. Black Desert Online: fences, growth, pruning/insects/watering, breeding, worker-managed farming, uses, market

### Takeaway
BDO farms in the open world: you rent a fence (1 to 10 seed slots) with Contribution Points, or since November 2023 buy a permanent 10-slot "Fence of Abundance" for 1,200 pearls (~$12, max 8 per family). Crops take 20-22 hours but only grow while you are logged in. Tending (pruning, pests, water, fertilizer) sets speed and yield, workers can automate pruning and pest removal, and "breeding" a mature crop instead of harvesting it yields seeds and climbs a seed-quality ladder (a Special crop counts as 5 regular ones). Crops feed Cooking (meals that typically last 90 minutes) and Alchemy (elixirs and draughts). Farming is a steady, AFK-friendly but modest income, and online-only growth is a standing complaint.

### Cited Findings
**Fences and plots**
- Fence types: Shabby Fence 1 slot; Small Fence 4 slots (3 Contribution Points); Plain Fence 7 slots (6 CP); Strong Fence 10 slots (10 CP); Old Moon Fence 10 slots (needs Farming Master 1). You need land almost anywhere in the world, a fence on it and seeds; fences are rented with CP and better fences/seeds need higher Farming — [Grumpy Green: fence types](https://grumpygreen.cricket/bdo-fence/); [Grumpy Green: farming guide](https://grumpygreen.cricket/farming/); [BDO Fandom: Fence](https://blackdesertonline.fandom.com/wiki/Fence); [BDFoundry farming guide](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/farming/)
- Paid fences: the Pearl Shop "Fence of Abundance" is a permanent 10-slot fence for 1,200 pearls that uses NO Contribution Points, limited to 8 per family, exchangeable at Klau in Velia for a better "Sturdy Fence of Abundance" at Farming Master 1 (Pearl Shop update of 15 November 2023). 1,000 pearls retail at $9.99, so one fence is ~$12 and eight ~$96 (my arithmetic). A YouTube video title: "Farming is PTW now but you get your CP back! Fence of abundance" — [Grumpy Green](https://grumpygreen.cricket/farming/); [Pearl Abyss NA/EU notice](https://www.naeu.playblackdesert.com/News/Notice/Detail?groupContentNo=6178&countryType=en-us); [Pearl Abyss ASIA notice](https://blackdesert.pearlabyss.com/ASIA/en-us/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=5760); [PlayStation Store: 1000 Pearls](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/product/UP4162-PPSA22500_00-0079545547923297); [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_glnpHUsAPs)
- Most crops take 1 slot per seed (some, e.g. grapes, 2). Artisan farmers can plant Magical Seeds: 5x the slots for 5x the crop (2 magical strawberry seeds fill a 10-slot fence) — [Grumpy Green](https://grumpygreen.cricket/farming/)

**Growth and offline**
- All crops take 20-22 hours depending on temperature, and crops only grow while the account is logged in — [Grumpy Green](https://grumpygreen.cricket/farming/); [BDFoundry](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/farming/)
- Players have asked on the official forum to "Change farming plants to offline growth" — [Official NA/EU forum thread](https://www.naeu.playblackdesert.com/en-US/Forum/ForumTopic/Detail?_topicNo=36465)

**Tending: pruning, insects, water, fertilizer**
- Crops periodically need pruning, pest removal and water; blights and pruning needs appear at random with a fixed chance; a blighted crop's growth "slows way down" until fixed (R to prune/kill pests); mushrooms need more pruning, other crops more insect removal — [Grumpy Green](https://grumpygreen.cricket/farming/); [Dulfy gardening guide, 6 Mar 2016](http://dulfy.net/2016/03/06/black-desert-gardening-and-farming-guide/)

No hard silver-per-hour figures were captured — [Grumpy Green farming spreadsheet](https://grumpygreen.cricket/farming-spreadsheet/); [Mein-MMO, 2021](https://mein-mmo.de/en/farming-silver-in-black-desert-how-to-farm-most-effectively-in-2021,670049/); [Black Desert Analytics: farming](https://blackdesertanalytics.netlify.app/semi_active_lifeskills/farming/)

### Inferences
- BDO's online-only growth is the most criticized timer design of the four. For a phone game played in short sessions it would mean crops grow only while the app is open, the opposite of what FarmVille-style timers need.
- BDO is the main precedent for SELLING farm space for real money. The backlash was muted ("you get your CP back") because the paid fence replaced a rent, matched the best free fence's size (10 slots) and was capped at 8. That suggests a pattern for paid expansions: convenience and permanence, capped, not bigger or stronger than earned plots.
- Breed vs harvest is a cheap, meaningful choice per crop (eat now or invest in seeds and quality). The quality ladder (Special = 5 regular) adds progression without new crop types.
- Workers that do only the chores (pruning/pests) while the player keeps the decisions (water, fertilize, harvest, breed) automate the boring part and keep the interesting part.
- Crops reach combat through cooking and alchemy buffs. BDO's 90-minute meals with tight stacking rules are a reference for "temporary stat boosts" that reward the farmer without becoming mandatory.

### Gaps
- Whether untended crops die or only slow, and whether a harvest-ready crop spoils, was not captured.
- Fence placement rules (distance from towns, overlap, who can see or touch another player's fence) were not confirmed. Visiting/sharing of BDO fences is undocumented here.
- Whether BDO's HP potions are NPC-sold (a likely contrast with player-made buffs) was not checked.
- No silver/hour or market-volume data for crops; the CP-to-slot figures come from guide snippets, not official text.

## 5. Cross-cutting: farming's share of consumables, how food buffs are structured, and how each game avoided (or failed to avoid) the chore

### Takeaway
All four games reach the combat player through consumables, at different strengths. Albion makes farming the base of ALL potions and food (fully player-made). OSRS makes it the dependable source of potion herbs (also dropped by monsters). WoW MoP made it a cheap source of top-food vegetables and some profession materials. BDO feeds cooking and alchemy buffs. Food buffs are one-at-a-time and short (Albion 30 min, WoW 1 h, BDO typically 90 min). What kept farming from feeling like a chore: real-time offline growth on predictable clocks, short runs, bulk actions (seed bags, plows), automation (BDO workers) and rewards that help rather than gate. What made it a chore: online-only growth (BDO), daily obligation tied to power (WoW Garrison), daily-capped watering tied to a subscription (Albion), and big rewards for idle time that were later nerfed (RS3 POF).

### Cited Findings
**Share of the consumables economy**
- Albion: "nearly every item ... is crafted by players", potions and food included — [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/761890/Albion_Online/); herbs for potions come from island Herb Gardens since 2016 — [Albion on Steam: Potions and Alchemy guide](https://steamcommunity.com/games/761890/announcements/detail/3985189839340751275)
- OSRS: prayer potions need ranarr (farmed, or an uncommon drop), and that use makes ranarr "one of the most valuable herbs in the game" — [Ranarr weed](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Ranarr_weed); [Prayer potion](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Prayer_potion)
- WoW MoP: the +300 Strength food needs 25 Green Cabbage (a farm crop) per batch, but vegetables can also come through Ironpaw Token sacks (1 token = 25 of a named vegetable) — [Warcraft Wiki: Recipe](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Recipe:_Black_Pepper_Ribs_and_Shrimp); [Wowpedia: Replenishing the Pantry](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Replenishing_the_Pantry)
- BDO: produce feeds Cooking and other life skills; Special/HQ crops substitute for 5/3 regular crops — [Dulfy](http://dulfy.net/2016/03/06/black-desert-gardening-and-farming-guide/)

**Food-buff structure**
- Albion: 30 minutes, one food buff at a time; buff by food type and tier — [Albion wiki: Food](https://wiki.albiononline.com/wiki/Food)
- WoW MoP: "Well Fed" +300 primary stat for 1 hour from top food — [Warcraft Wiki: Recipe](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Recipe:_Black_Pepper_Ribs_and_Shrimp)
- BDO: meals typically 90 minutes; limited stacking (a second food after 30 minutes in some cases; Cron Meals cannot be overwritten by simpler food); elixirs vs draughts mutually exclusive, a draught overriding elixirs — [Grumpy Green cooking guide](https://grumpygreen.cricket/bdo-cooking-guide-eminent/); [Altar of Gaming](https://altarofgaming.com/black-desert-online-cooking-guide/)

**Timers and offline growth**

### Inferences
- Summary grid (built from the findings above; every cell is sourced in sections 1-4):

  | | OSRS | RS3 POF | Albion | WoW Sunsong | BDO |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Space | Public patches spread across the world | Personal farm (Manor Farm) | Private island(s), instanced, with access roles | Phased personal farm | Fences in the open world |
  | Timer | 40 min to 16 h by crop, offline | Stages offline in pens | 22 h every crop, offline | ~24 h | 20-22 h, ONLINE ONLY |
  | Risk/tending | Disease; compost, water, flowers, paid gardener | Feed animals (health/happiness) | Water with Focus (seed return) | Random problem states, one verb each | Prune, pests, water, fertilizer |
  | Expansions | Progress-gated, free | n/a captured | Silver, steep; Premium to buy | Reputation-gated, free | CP rent, or $12 paid fence (cap 8) |
  | Combat link | Herbs -> potions | (XP, beans) | All potions and food | Stat food, profession mats | Cooking/alchemy buffs |

- For BroTown's goals: (1) growth must continue offline on server wall-clock time (OSRS rework, BDO complaint); (2) each of the owner's steps (dig, plant, fertilize, water) should change a visible outcome, either lowering a risk (OSRS compost/disease) or fixing a problem state (WoW), not just add taps; (3) one daily crop cycle plus faster crops gives both a daily habit and something to do in a five-minute session; (4) farmed consumables can replace NPC potions only if supply is wide (many farmers, plus monster drops as a floor, as in OSRS) and the market can absorb gluts (Albion's specialization), otherwise prices swing; (5) paid expansions are best as convenience or permanence and capped (BDO), with a free start (Albion's 20k first island), never as the only way to get combat power (the Garrison lesson); (6) visiting needs explicit roles (Albion), since the phased WoW model blocks the social goal.
- Food-buff norms across the four: one active food buff, 30-90 minutes, food type sets which stats rise. A 2-tier design (a cheap common buff and a top-tier one that needs a farmed ingredient) mirrors WoW's Way foods.

### Gaps
- No game publishes, and no source gave, a percentage of consumables sourced from farming; "share" can only be described qualitatively.
- Direct Reddit sentiment (r/2007scape, r/albiononline, r/wow, r/blackdesertonline) could not be sampled: reddit.com was refused by the search tool and every wiki page was blocked from direct reading.
- Retention or engagement numbers for any of these farming systems were not found.
- FarmVille-style withering (crops dying if not harvested in time) was not found in any of the four games' captured mechanics; whether any of them withers crops is unconfirmed.
