## RAW FINDINGS LOG (checkpoint 1, 2026-10-06; to be folded into the sections above)

Access note: the egress policy BLOCKS stardewvalleywiki.com, palia.wiki.gg, nookipedia.com, hardcoregamer.com and mmorpg.com for page fetches. Facts below from those domains come from search-result snippets/summaries (WebSearch), cited to the URL the search returned. Treat them as one step less verified than a full-page read.

- Stardew quality formula (search summary of stardewvalleywiki Crops/Deluxe Fertilizer + guides): goldRoll = 0.2*(L/10) + 0.2*F*((L+2)/12) + 0.01, L = farming level, F = fertilizer level (0 none, 1 basic, 2 quality, 3 deluxe); iridium roll = goldRoll/2 and only when F=3. Deluxe at farming level 0: 84% silver / 10% gold / 6% iridium (I re-derived these from the formula: goldRoll 0.11, iridium 0.055, gold ~0.104, silver remainder ~0.841; consistent). Deluxe Fertilizer is the only way to get iridium crops. — https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Deluxe_Fertilizer ; https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Crops (via search)
- Stardew iridium = 2x base price (parsnip 35g -> 70g). — search summary citing guide sites (hardcoregamer crop quality guide, stardewvalley.tools)
- Stardew co-op: host + "farmhands"; a farmhand can only join if there is an empty cabin; each cabin tied to one player. Money shared or separate, set at creation and changeable later at the Town Ledger in the Mayor's Manor. Host-only chat commands: /kick /ban /unban /unbanAll /pause /resume /moveBuildingPermission off|owned|on /unlinkPlayer. Building Move Mode: everyone / host only / building owner only. — https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Multiplayer ; https://www.pcgamer.com/stardew-valley-co-op-multiplayer-guide/ (via search)
- Palia garden buffs (search summary of palia.wiki.gg Gardening + guides): Water Retain (Potato, Napa Cabbage, Tomato); Harvest Boost (Wheat, Rice, Corn; 2x2 Blueberries, Batterfly Beans; 3x3 Apple) = +50% extra yield; Weed Block (Carrot, Onion, Bok Choy) prevents knapweed; Quality Boost (Cotton); Growth Speed (Blueberries, Apples, Spicy Peppers - as summarized; needs verification). Only orthogonal neighbours; a crop never buffs its own type; multi-tile crops need several adjacent crops of the same buff type. — https://palia.wiki.gg/wiki/Gardening ; https://www.thegamer.com/palia-best-farm-layouts/ (via search)
- ACNH flower reproduction chance by number of visitors who watered it that day: 0 -> 5%, 1 -> 25%, 2 -> 35%, 3 -> 50%, 4 -> 65%, 5+ -> 80%; up to five visitors count. — search summary (Nookipedia Flower, Prima Games, Digital Trends, Sportskeeda); to verify
- ArcheAge housing: land is not instanced -> housing shortage; players wait for others' taxes to lapse and rush the plot. After landrush an 8x8 scarecrow farm spot can sometimes be found; smallest house 16x16. Taxes paid with tax certificates bought with labor; tax inflation beyond 2 properties (e.g., 5 certificates for an 8x8, two plots 10). On placing a design you get mail and have a 2-week window to pay; miss it and the structure becomes unprotected, a ~20 s removal countdown starts, and anyone can demolish and place their own. — https://saarith.com/archeage-how-to-get-a-house/ ; https://thefanaticalswordsman.com/2014/11/12/the-pros-and-cons-of-archeage-housing-and-pvp/ (via search; which sentence came from which not certain)
- ArcheAge open-world crops: players can plant crops/trees/livestock in the open world but others may steal them, gaining crime points and eventually trial by a jury of five players; one reported 134-minute jail sentence for stealing open crops. Scarecrow farms (from an early quest) claim land in designated areas; only the owner or those given permission can access the farm. — https://archeage.fandom.com/wiki/Justice_System ; https://www.pcgamesn.com/archeage/i-was-put-on-trial-in-archeage-my-first-punishment-was-to-get-naked ; https://saarith.com/archeage-how-to-start-farming-get-scarecrow-farm/ (via search)

### checkpoint 2 (search summaries; URLs as returned by search)
- Stardew Speed-Gro +10% growth, Deluxe Speed-Gro +25%, Hyper Speed-Gro +33%; Basic Retaining Soil ~33% chance to stay watered overnight, Quality ~66%, Deluxe 100% (water once and it stays watered until harvest). — https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Fertilizer ; https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Deluxe_Retaining_Soil ; https://gamerant.com/stardew-valley-fertilizer-guide/ (via search)
- Stardew sprinklers: Quality 8 tiles; Iridium 24 tiles (5x5); Pressure Nozzle +1 tile each direction: basic -> 3x3 (8), quality -> 5x5 (24), iridium -> 7x7 (48). — https://www.thegamer.com/stardew-valley-sprinklers-guide-enricher-pressure-nozzle/ ; https://stardewvalleyids.com/sprinklers (via search)
- Stardew regrowers: Strawberry 8 days then every 4, sells 120g (silver 150, gold 180) - keeps producing to end of season; Blueberry 13 days, regrow 4, 50g/62/75/100 (iridium), 2-3 berries a harvest; Cranberries 7 days, regrow 5, 75g/93/112. (Price ladder confirms quality x1.25 / x1.5 / x2, floored.) — https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Strawberry ; https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Cranberries ; https://www.thegamer.com/stardew-valley-complete-crop-sell-price-guide/ (via search)
- Stardew 1.6 multiplayer: up to 8 players on PC (host + 7 farmhands). The farm only exists while the host plays: farmhands cannot enter without the host online (hosted servers / the "Always On Server" mod are workarounds). — https://gamingbolt.com/stardew-valley-update-1-6-out-now-includes-8-player-multiplayer-new-festival-and-more ; https://www.gameserverkings.com/knowledge-base/stardew-valley/stardew-valley-multiplayer/ (via search)
- Palia: unwatered crops PAUSE growth, never die; water once a day, water level resets at 6 am (in-game); daily small chance of a weed on a plant unless WeedBlock fertilizer or the weed-prevention crop buff; a weeded plant stops growing and cannot be watered. Five fertilizers: QualityUp, HydroPro (no watering needed), SpeedyGro, WeedBlock, HarvestBoost. — https://www.thegamer.com/palia-farming-gardening-seeds-soil-plots-fertilizer-guide-walkthrough/ ; https://palia.wiki.gg/wiki/Gardening ; https://steamcommunity.com/app/2707930/discussions/0/4701286722076382830/ (via search)
- Palia visiting: "Visit Other Plots Board" beside the entrance gate; you can only visit a friend/party member who is ON their plot, and the plot must be public; visitors can pull weeds, water crops and add materials to pending structures; an "editor" may at most move already-placed items. No helping while the owner is offline. — https://support.palia.com/hc/en-us/articles/7475337549076-Social-Features ; https://palia.wiki.gg/wiki/Housing_Plot ; https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/palia-gardening-guide (via search)
- Palia status (HISTORICAL): Steam release 25 Mar 2024; Daybreak Game Company acquired Singularity 6 + Palia July 2024; PS5 / Xbox Series X|S May 2025 alongside the Elderwood expansion; monthly updates until early 2026, then irregular while preparing a "Royal Highlands" expansion (per Wikipedia as summarized by search, unverified page read). — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palia ; https://www.psu.com/news/daybreak-has-acquired-palia-studio-singularity-6/ ; https://www.daybreakgames.com/news/dbg-palia-xbox-ps5 (via search)

### checkpoint 3 (search summaries; URLs as returned by search)
- ArcheAge land/bots: claim that ~25% of players had no land in the early years; bots and hacks "migrated from Korean and Russian versions"; land-grabber scripts/plugins sniped plots whose taxes lapsed, for resale; alts used as "tax mules"; some grabbers held 30-50 plots. Dev response: better detection of third-party land-claim tools, accounts using scripts/macros removed with associated accounts, their land released within two weeks. — http://forums.archeagegame.com/archive/index.php/t-150686.html (Trion forum archive: "Macro-Using Land Grabbers Evicted from ArcheAge"); https://www.ownedcore.com/forums/general/archive/archeage/archeage-bots-programs/495025-land-grabber-plugin.html ; https://forums.anandtech.com/threads/archeage-thread.2381667/post-36751227 (via search; the 25% figure is a forum-level claim, low confidence)
- ArcheAge NA/EU closed 27 June 2024, 08:00 UTC (Kakao Games), citing declining player count; data deleted two weeks after closure. — https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2024/04/27/archeage-servers-in-europe-and-north-america-will-be-shutdown-on-june-27th/ ; https://archeage.playkakaogames.com/news/3136 ; https://mmos.com/news/archeage-shutdown-announcement (via search)
- ACNH Best Friends: added via the Best Friends app on the NookPhone (only someone who has already visited); only Best Friends may use potentially destructive tools (axe, shovel) on your island; others cannot under any circumstances. Dream Address (Luna): a snapshot of the island visitable while the owner is NOT playing; nothing can be changed, picked up or shaken in a dream. — https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/News/2020/May/Top-tips-for-playing-Animal-Crossing-New-Horizons-with-friends-1783708.html ; https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/50094/ ; https://www.howtogeek.com/683468/how-to-visit-someones-island-in-dreams-in-animal-crossing-new-horizons/ (via search)
- ACNH away: cockroaches appear in your house only after more than a month away (more the longer); the island fills with weeds after a long absence; roaches lower island rating. Flowers do not die (some report islands covered in flowers after long absences, inconsistent). — https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Cockroach ; https://sportskeeda.com/esports/what-happens-play-animal-crossing-new-horizons-month ; https://game8.co/games/Animal-Crossing-New-Horizons/archives/285798 (via search)
- Eric Barone: wanted "a really relaxed and joyous experience", not stressful; Grandpa's evaluation "breaks the relaxed feel" so he made it more open-ended; you can skip farming entirely (mines, fishing); players report feeling "invigorated" not burnt out. — https://kotaku.com/the-past-present-and-future-of-stardew-valley-1766238624 ; https://www.npr.org/2025/01/24/g-s1-44510/the-legacy-and-future-of-the-farming-game-stardew-valley ; https://egw.news/gaming/news/26145/stardew-valley-is-10-years-developer-interview-abo-RE7ooIF8I (via search; quote-to-source mapping uncertain)
- Stardew buffs: exactly one food buff and one drink buff active at once; new food replaces old food buff, new drink replaces old drink buff; Luck, Speed and Max Energy come from both and stack; durations in real-time minutes/seconds, same for all effects of one item (except Squid Ink Ravioli); Qi Seasoning +50% duration. — https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Buffs ; https://gamerant.com/stardew-valley-every-buff-and-how-to-get-them/ (via search)
- Palia sentiment: Steam players call gardening tedious, ask for sprinklers or instant watering instead of holding the button ~5 s; the upgraded watering can waters "randomly instead of in a logical radius", so 81 crops took almost as long as with the starter can. — https://steamcommunity.com/app/2707930/discussions/0/4360121020300548226 ; https://steamcommunity.com/app/2707930/discussions/0/4299320215882764086 (via search; which thread said which uncertain)
- Palia plot expansion (LOW CONFIDENCE, aggregator sites): additional housing slots 50,000 / 75,000 / 100,000 gold (+25,000 each); garden capacity ~9 plots starter, ~18 Expanded I, ~36 Expanded II, 60+ Expanded III. — https://farminggames.help/palia/housing/ ; https://paliaguide.com/plot-guide ; https://paliapedia.com/accomplishment/housinggrid3 (via search)
