# The owner's recordings of 2026-10-28, kept as supplied

Uploaded to the top of the project on 2026-10-28 with the arena's pictures,
and moved here unchanged, byte for byte (the cheer v2.3.3730, the horse
v2.3.3743, with the chariot's picture). The server suite (`sound-cues` §11zd
and §13f) refuses sound files at the top of the project.

**Neither is in the game yet.** The arena (lane C) wires them in, the horse
with the charioteer: cutting and levelling each with the tools in `tools/audio/` (as `cut_owner_sounds.py` does),
giving it a label in the Sound Studio, and an entry in `CREDITS.md`. Check each
recording's licence on its Freesound page first: Freesound's "NonCommercial"
licences are out (see the v2.3.2970 note in CLAUDE.md). The owner says their
Freesound sounds are not NonCommercial; that is their statement, recorded
here, not a check of the page. The horse's licence is marked CONFIRM in
`CREDITS.md` until the owner reads it off its page.

Each name is Freesound's: the sound's number, its maker, then its title.
